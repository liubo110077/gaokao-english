Social Comparisons
"But Dad, everyone in my class gets way more screen time than I do!" My daughter's voice carried the unmistakable tone of teenage injustice. My response — "And if everyone jumped off a bridge, would you?" — was as automatic as it was unhelpful. What neither of us realized at the time was that we were both engaging in one of humanity's most deeply rooted psychological habits: social comparison.

这句是典型的「What ... was that ...」主语从句结构。主语是 What neither of us realized at the time（当时我们俩都没意识到的事），系动词是 was，表语是 that 引导的从句：we were both engaging in... social comparison。

核心意思很简单：我们当时都没意识到，我们俩都在进行「社会比较」这个根深蒂固的心理习惯。

「可是爸爸，我们班所有人的屏幕时间都比我多得多！」女儿的声音带着一种一听就知道的青少年式不公平感。我的回答——「那如果所有人都跳桥，你也跳吗？」——脱口而出，却毫无用处。我们当时谁都没意识到，我们俩都在进行人类最根深蒂固的心理习惯之一：社会比较。
长难句拆解
主语（What从句）What neither of us realized at the time 系动词was 表语（that从句）that we were both engaging in one of humanity's most deeply rooted psychological habits: social comparison.
1
先读主干：What ... was that ...→（某件事）就是（某个事实）
2

3

💡 「What ... is/was that ...」是高考常见的主语从句结构。What 引导的部分做主语，that 后面是具体内容。
In 1954, psychologist Leon Festinger proposed that humans have a fundamental need to evaluate their own abilities and opinions. When objective measures aren't available, we turn to the people around us as reference points. Festinger identified two directions of comparison: upward, toward those we perceive as better off, and downward, toward those we see as worse off.
1954年，心理学家利昂·费斯廷格提出，人类有一种根本性的需求，即评估自身的能力和观点。当客观标准不可得时，我们就会把身边的人当作参照点。费斯廷格确认了两个比较方向：向上比较——与我们认为过得更好的人比较；向下比较——与我们认为过得更差的人比较。
Social media has transformed social comparison from an occasional habit into a constant activity. The carefully curated images on social platforms create an unrealistic standard that is almost impossible to match. Studies consistently show that heavy social media use correlates with lower self-esteem, particularly among adolescents.

这句的核心结构是：Studies show that A correlates with B。studies = 研究，consistently = 一致地，show = 表明，that 后面是研究发现的内容。

关键词 correlates with 表示「相关联」——注意不是「导致」。particularly among adolescents 进一步指出在青少年中尤为明显。

社交媒体已经把社会比较从一种偶尔的习惯变成了一种持续的活动。社交平台上精心策划的图片营造出一种几乎不可能达到的不切实际的标准。研究一致表明，大量使用社交媒体与较低的自尊心相关联，尤其在青少年群体中。
长难句拆解
谁Studies 修饰consistently 表明show that 内容heavy social media use correlates with lower self-esteem, particularly among adolescents.
1
先读主干：Studies show that ...→研究表明……
2
that 从句核心：heavy social media use correlates with lower self-esteem→大量使用社交媒体与较低自尊相关

💡 correlates with = 与…相关联（统计意义），不等于 causes（导致）。高考议论文中常出现这类措辞，注意区分因果与相关。
Yet painting all social comparison as harmful would be an oversimplification. Upward comparison can serve as powerful motivation — seeing a peer succeed at something challenging can inspire us to raise our own standards. Downward comparison, while sometimes making people feel better, can also breed complacency.
然而，将所有的社会比较都描绘成有害的，未免过于简单化。向上比较可以充当强大的动力——看到同龄人在有挑战性的事情上取得成功，可以激励我们提高自己的标准。向下比较虽然有时让人感觉好一些，但也可能滋生自满。
The critical variable, researchers have found, is not whether we compare, but how we interpret the comparison.

这句核心结构是：The critical variable is not A but B。A = whether we compare（是否比较），B = how we interpret the comparison（如何解读比较）。中间插入 researchers have found（研究者发现）是插入语。

关键洞察：决定社会比较效果的不是「比不比」，而是「怎么理解」。

People with a growth mindset tend to view others' success as evidence of what's possible. Those with a fixed mindset, by contrast, often interpret the same information as proof of their own limitations.
研究者发现，关键变量不是我们是否进行比较，而是我们如何解读这种比较。拥有成长型思维的人倾向于把他人的成功视为「什么是可能的」的证据。而持有固定型思维的人，相反，往往把同样的信息解读为自身局限性的证明。

1

2

3
B = how we interpret the comparison→我们如何解读比较
💡 「not A but B」是高频考点——不是A而是B。插入语 researchers have found 用逗号隔开，可以先跳过不影响理解。
Rather than trying to stop comparing — which psychology tells us is impossible — we would do better to teach young people to compare constructively.

这句是全文的核心观点。Rather than A, we would do better to B。A = trying to stop comparing（试图停止比较），B = teach young people to compare constructively（教年轻人建设性地比较）。

中间 which psychology tells us is impossible 是插入定语从句，说明「停止比较」在心理学上是不可能的。

与其试图停止比较——心理学告诉我们这是不可能的——不如教年轻人学会建设性地比较。

28. What does the opening scene with the father and daughter illustrate?
A. The generation gap in attitudes toward technology.
B. The universality of social comparison in everyday life.
C. The negative effects of excessive screen time on teenagers.
D. The difficulty parents face in setting rules for children.
答案：B
"What neither of us realized at the time was that we were both engaging in one of humanity's most deeply rooted psychological habits: social comparison."

解题思路：开头场景的作用是什么？第3句（data-idx=2）直接点明——父女俩当时都没意识到，他们其实都在进行「社会比较」。女儿比（别人有更多屏幕时间），爸爸也在用比较的逻辑回应。说明社会比较在日常生活中无处不在。

A. The generation gap in attitudes toward technology ✗场景提到屏幕时间，但重点不是代沟或技术态度，而是社会比较
B. The universality of social comparison in everyday life ✓原文明确说 "one of humanity's most deeply rooted psychological habits"，证明普遍性
C. The negative effects of excessive screen time ✗文章不是讨论屏幕时间的危害，屏幕时间只是引出话题的素材
D. The difficulty parents face in setting rules ✗文章的主题不是育儿规则，父女对话是引子而非论点

答案：D

阅读技巧：议论/说明类文章怎么读 ▼

C篇常考"议论/说明类"——作者围绕一个观点展开论述，题目考你能不能抓住核心论点和论据。有3个核心策略：

① 首尾段定调，中间找证据

议论文的核心观点通常在开头引出（本文用父女对话引出社会比较话题）和结尾总结（"Rather than trying to stop comparing... compare constructively"）。中间段落是证据和分析。做主旨题（如Q30）时，直接看首尾段就能锁定答案。

② 抓转折词，找作者真正的立场

本文的关键转折：第4段 "Yet painting all social comparison as harmful would be an oversimplification." yet 一转，作者亮明观点——不是所有比较都有害。再看 "The critical variable... is not whether we compare, but how we interpret..." not...but 结构直接给出关键因素。转折词后面往往就是作者真正想说的话。

③ 区分"相关"与"因果"，别被干扰项骗

原文用 "correlates with"（相关联）而不是 "causes"（导致）来描述社交媒体与低自尊的关系。Q30选项D说社交媒体是"primary cause"——这就把"相关"偷换成了"因果"，是典型的干扰项。高考议论文中经常出现这种措辞陷阱，一定要看清原文用的是什么词。

作文拓展：好句式 & 连接词速查 ▼
从文章偷来的好句式
What ... is/was that ...
（某件事）就是（某个事实）——主语从句
原句：What neither of us realized at the time was that we were both engaging in ... social comparison.
作文套用：What impressed me most was that everyone worked together as a team. 用 What 引导主语从句，比直接说 "I was impressed" 高级很多。
not A but B / not whether ... but how ...
不是A而是B / 关键不在于是否...而在于如何...
原句：The critical variable is not whether we compare, but how we interpret the comparison.
作文套用：The key is not whether we make mistakes, but how we learn from them. 写议论文分析问题时特别好用。
Rather than doing A, we would do better to do B.
与其做A，不如做B
原句：Rather than trying to stop comparing, we would do better to teach young people to compare constructively.
作文套用：Rather than complaining about difficulties, we would do better to find solutions. 提建议时用这个句式，比 "We should..." 有层次感得多。
... has transformed A from ... into ...
...把A从...变成了...
原句：Social media has transformed social comparison from an occasional habit into a constant activity.
作文套用：Technology has transformed learning from a classroom activity into a lifelong journey. 描述变化时用，比 "changed" 更有画面感。
Painting all X as Y would be an oversimplification.
把所有X都说成Y，未免过于简单化
原句：Yet painting all social comparison as harmful would be an oversimplification.
作文套用：Painting all technology as harmful would be an oversimplification. 写议论文需要表达"事情没那么简单"时，这个句式很高级。
连接词速查（按功能分）
递进 / 补充
furthermore moreover in addition what's more not only...but also
比 "also" 和 "and" 正式，议论文首选
转折 / 对比
however yet while by contrast rather than
yet 用在句中；however 另起；by contrast 对比两个对象
结果 / 因此
as a result therefore consequently thus
thus 最简洁，thus + 分词短语直接高分
举例说明
for instance for example take...for example such as
for instance 比 for example 更口语化一点点，两者都可用
总结 / 结尾
in conclusion to sum up in short overall in a word
结尾段第一句用，考官扫一眼就知道你会写结论
顺序 / 衔接
first of all secondly finally meanwhile at the same time
列举多个原因/步骤时用，让文章结构清晰
