2025北京卷完形填空 · The IT Lab Confession

Q1–10
完形填空（共10题，每题1.5分）
阅读下面短文，从短文后各题所给的四个选项（A、B、C和D）中，选出可以填入空白处的最佳选项。
At 15, I excelled academically yet felt overshadowed and unnoticed. The (1)desire to stand out consumed me, pushing me towards a regrettable decision.
One day, during a class in the IT lab with my classmates, our teacher mentioned missing computer parts. (2)Curious looks exchanged, everyone wondered how it could happen in a(n) (3)locked lab. "It's possible to open that lock with another key," I said. Instantly, eyes turned to me with newfound interest and curiosity. It was a rush I had never experienced before. Enjoying all the eyes on me, I (4)demonstrated how to open the lock.
Little did I realize that single moment would ruin everything.
The following day, called to the headmaster's office, I found myself accused of stealing the missing parts. "I didn't do it, sir," I argued, but suspicion (5)clouded his eyes. His repeated (6)questioning intensified, pushing me to the edge of fear and tears. In a desperate attempt to escape the mounting pressure, I (7)falsely confessed (承认).
The headmaster asked my father to come to school, whose shaky faith in me brought a mix of relief and (8)shame. My father gently asked me if I understood the gravity of my actions. (9)Tearfully I confessed my misguided longing for recognition. In the headmaster's office, my father listened attentively, and then spoke with quiet authority: "I trust my son. He is not a thief."
With my father's support, I learned a valuable lesson: (10)attention cannot be forced; true recognition comes from honourable acts.
答案解析（1—10题）▼
1. C — desire （渴望）
引起注意的欲望，desire to stand out 想要脱颖而出的渴望。felt overshadowed and unnoticed（觉得被忽视）→ 因此产生了想要引人注目的渴望。promise（承诺）/ competition（竞争）/ hesitation（犹豫）均不符合"想要被关注"的语义。
2. A — Curious （好奇的）
好奇的目光交换，因为电脑零件不翼而飞。Curious looks exchanged（互相交换好奇的眼神），大家都好奇零件怎么会丢。Hopeful（充满希望的）/ Distant（冷漠的）/ Envious（嫉妒的）均与"零件失窃后的自然反应"不符。
3. B — locked （锁着的）
锁着的实验室，后文说 "open that lock"（打开那把锁），说明实验室是上锁的。大家好奇的是：在一个上锁的实验室里怎么会丢东西？expensive（昂贵的）/ modern（现代的）/ abandoned（废弃的）虽可修饰 lab，但与后文"lock"无呼应。
4. C — demonstrated （演示）
演示了如何开锁，为了享受大家的关注。"我"不只是说了，还demonstration（演示）了开锁方法。Enjoying all the eyes on me = 享受众人目光，所以"我"当场表演了。discovered（发现）/ recommended（推荐）/ imagined（想象）均不能表达"当众展示"。
5. B — clouded （笼罩）
怀疑笼罩了他的眼神，suspicion clouded his eyes 是固定搭配，表示"怀疑蒙蔽/笼罩了他的目光"。caught（捕捉）/ hurt（伤害）/ brightened（使明亮）均不能与 suspicion + eyes 构成合理搭配。
6. A — questioning （质问）
反复的质问让他害怕流泪。His repeated questioning intensified（他反复的质问不断加剧），pushing me to the edge of fear and tears。在校长办公室被审问的场景中，questioning（质问）最合理。reminding（提醒）/ complaining（抱怨）/ requesting（要求）均不符合审讯情境。
7. D — falsely （虚假地）
虚假地承认（他没偷但迫于压力承认了）。前文"I didn't do it"说明"我"是无辜的，但在巨大压力下 falsely confessed（做了虚假的认罪）。warmly（温暖地）/ readily（乐意地）/ randomly（随机地）均不能表达"被迫做出不实承认"。
8. C — shame （羞愧）
既感到宽慰又觉得羞愧。a mix of relief and shame——父亲来了，"我"感到宽慰（relief），但同时也为自己的行为感到羞愧（shame）。pride（骄傲）/ patience（耐心）/ surprise（惊讶）均不能与 relief 形成合理的"矛盾情感组合"。
9. B — Tearfully （含泪地）
含泪承认了自己想要被认可的误导性渴望。Tearfully I confessed my misguided longing for recognition——"我"含着泪向父亲坦白了真正的原因。前文已被逼到"the edge of fear and tears"，此处含泪坦白与情感线呼应。Generously（慷慨地）/ Carelessly（粗心地）/ Strangely（奇怪地）均不符合情感语境。
10. D — attention （关注）
关注不能强求，真正的认可来自正当行为。Attention cannot be forced（关注不能被强求）——全文讲"我"为了获得别人的关注而走上歪路，最终领悟到真正的认可只能通过honourable acts（正当行为）获得。tolerance（宽容）/ gratitude（感恩）/ sympathy（同情）均不是全文的核心主题。
重点词汇（10个）▼
excelled 表现出色 考纲内
excel v. 擅长，突出。excelled = 过去式，表示在某方面表现极为出色。
I excelled academically yet felt overshadowed. （课文）
She excels in mathematics and science. 她在数学和科学方面表现出色。
overshadowed 被遮盖，被忽视 课文
overshadow v. 使相形见绌，使黯然失色。over-（在上方）+ shadow（阴影）= 被笼罩在阴影中。
I felt overshadowed and unnoticed. （课文）
His achievements were overshadowed by his brother's fame. 他的成就被哥哥的名气所掩盖。
regrettable 令人遗憾的 考纲内
regret v. 后悔 → regrettable adj. 令人遗憾的。注意：regretful = 感到遗憾的（修饰人），regrettable = 令人遗憾的（修饰事）。
Pushing me towards a regrettable decision. （课文）
It was a regrettable mistake. 那是一个令人遗憾的错误。
demonstrated 演示，展示 考纲内
demonstrate v. 演示/展示/证明。demonstration n. 示范/游行。
I demonstrated how to open the lock. （课文）
The teacher demonstrated how to use the equipment. 老师演示了如何使用这台设备。
suspicion 怀疑 考纲内
suspect v. 怀疑 → suspicion n. 怀疑 → suspicious adj. 可疑的/多疑的。
Suspicion clouded his eyes. （课文）
He was arrested on suspicion of theft. 他因涉嫌盗窃被逮捕。
intensified 加剧 课文
intensify v. 使加剧/使增强。intense adj. 强烈的 → intensity n. 强度 → intensify v.
His repeated questioning intensified. （课文）
The pain intensified throughout the night. 疼痛在整夜中不断加剧。
desperate 绝望的，拼命的 考纲内
desperate adj. 绝望的/拼命的/不顾一切的。despair n. 绝望。a desperate attempt = 孤注一掷的尝试。
In a desperate attempt to escape the mounting pressure. （课文）
She made a desperate effort to save the drowning child. 她拼命努力去救那个溺水的孩子。
confessed 承认，坦白 考纲内
confess v. 承认/坦白（尤指不好的事或罪行）。confession n. 忏悔/坦白。
I falsely confessed. （课文）
He finally confessed to the crime. 他最终承认了罪行。
gravity 严重性（不是"重力"义） 考纲内
gravity 有两个意思：①重力/引力 ②严重性/庄重。the gravity of my actions = 我的行为的严重性。
He asked me if I understood the gravity of my actions. （课文）
I don't think you understand the gravity of the situation. 我觉得你不理解局势的严重性。
honourable 光荣的，正当的 课文
honour n. 荣誉 → honourable adj. 光荣的/正当的/高尚的。美式拼写：honorable。
True recognition comes from honourable acts. （课文）
He lived an honourable life. 他过着正直高尚的生活。
