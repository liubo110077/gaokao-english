2025北京卷语法填空 · 三篇短文

Q11–20
语法填空（共10题，每题1.5分）
A篇（第11—13题）· Walking with Nick
Most days after school, I love walking in the forest with my dog Nick. It's (11)peaceful , especially around sunset. Nick has a fun time smelling the leaves and running after rabbits. When Nick runs ahead, I hear him barking excitedly. I sometimes call him back, worried that he might have someone (12)scared . The truth, though, is (13)what could be guessed — there's never anyone else here. Just me, Nick, and the quiet forest.
B篇（第14—16题）· Making Choices
When was the last time you took on a tough choice? Maybe this morning you debated whether to get up or stay in bed slightly (14)longer . Making choices is part of life. By the time you brushed your teeth, you (15)had made several decisions even without noticing. Some choices are quick, while others take more thought. Usually, better outcomes (16)are achieved through a thorough understanding of strategies like risk tolerance.
C篇（第17—20题）· Travel and Food Waste
When (17)travelling , we engage with cultures and experiences. However, according to a report by the World Wildlife Fund, tourists (18)left over 1.3 billion tons of food waste last year, (19)which accounted for about 8% of the total global gas release in 2024. To reduce our impact, we can take simple steps: empty the fridge before heading out, and choose sustainable dining options. These efforts help us travel more responsibly. After all, exploring the world shouldn't come (20)at the planet's expense.
答案解析（11—20题）▼
11. peaceful
考点：词性转换（名词→形容词）
It's ___，be动词后接形容词作表语。peace（名词）→ peaceful（形容词），表示"宁静的"。
类似构词：hope → hopeful, care → careful, beauty → beautiful。
12. scared
考点：have sb done 结构
have someone scared = 让某人被吓到。someone 与 scare 之间是被动关系（某人"被"吓到），所以用过去分词 scared。
have sb done 表示"使某人被..."，区分 have sb do（让某人做...，主动）和 have sb doing（让某人一直做...）。
13. what
考点：表语从句引导词
The truth is ___ could be guessed。is 后面是表语从句，从句中缺少主语（什么东西能被猜到），用 what 引导表语从句，what 在从句中作主语。
区分：that（引导完整从句，不充当成分）vs what（= the thing that，在从句中作主/宾语）。
14. longer
考点：比较级
slightly ___ 修饰比较级，表示"稍微更久一点"。long → longer，slightly longer = 稍微久一点。
slightly, a bit, a little, much, far, even 等常用来修饰比较级。
15. had made
考点：过去完成时
时间状语 By the time you brushed your teeth（当你刷牙的时候），by the time + 过去时 → 主句用过去完成时：had + 过去分词。
表示"在刷牙之前，你已经做了好几个决定了"，强调动作发生在过去某一时间点之前。
16. are achieved
考点：一般现在时被动语态
主语 better outcomes（更好的结果）是"被实现"的，与 achieve 是被动关系。此句描述一般性规律，用一般现在时被动：are + 过去分词（achieved）。
outcomes 是复数，故用 are achieved，不是 is achieved。
17. travelling
考点：省略主语的时间状语从句
完整形式为 When we are travelling，当从句主语与主句主语一致时，可省略"主语 + be"，简化为：When travelling = When (we are) travelling。
类似：While studying = While (I was) studying。注意 travelling 英式双写 l。
18. left
考点：一般过去时
时间标志词 last year（去年）表示过去某一确定时间，用一般过去时。leave → left（过去式）。
last year / yesterday / in 2024 等明确过去时间 → 一般过去时。
19. which
考点：非限制性定语从句
逗号后接定语从句，which 指代前面整个事实（游客留下了13亿多吨食物垃圾），which 在从句中作主语。
非限制性定语从句中不能用 that，只能用 which。which 指代整件事时，翻译为"这件事（占了...）"。
20. at
考点：固定搭配 at one's expense
at one's expense = 以...为代价（固定搭配）。come at the planet's expense = 以地球为代价。
类似搭配：at the cost of, at the price of。注意：expense 也可表示"费用"，如 travel expenses 旅行费用。
语法填空答题技巧▼

四步解题法：

  • 第一步，判断词性：空格在句中是名词/动词/形容词/副词位置？名词位置看单复数；动词位置看时态/语态/非谓语；副词位置找程度词。
  • 第二步，找时间状语：in recent years → 现在完成时；by the time + 过去 → 过去完成时；yesterday / last year → 过去时。
  • 第三步，判断主动/被动：主语是动作的发出者 → 主动；主语是动作的承受者 → 被动（be done / being done）。
  • 第四步，查固定搭配：at one's expense / account for / come at 等介词搭配要牢记，注意介词选择。
重点词汇（10个） ▼
peaceful 和平的，宁静的 高频
peace n. 和平 → peaceful adj. 宁静的，和平的。It's peaceful, especially around sunset. = 这里很宁静，尤其是日落时分。
a peaceful life 宁静的生活  ·  peaceful protest 和平抗议  ·  peacefully adv. 平静地
sunset 日落 高频
sun 太阳 + set 落下 → sunset 日落。around sunset = 日落时分。
反义：sunrise 日出  ·  a beautiful sunset 美丽的日落  ·  at sunset 在日落时
debated 考虑，权衡 考纲内
debate v. 辩论/考虑 → debated whether to... = 考虑是否要...
a heated debate 激烈的辩论  ·  debate over/about sth 关于某事的辩论  ·  debatable adj. 有争议的
outcomes 结果 考纲内
outcome n. 结果，结局（out + come）。better outcomes = 更好的结果。
positive/negative outcome 正面/负面结果  ·  the outcome of the election 选举结果  ·  近义词：result, consequence
thorough 彻底的，全面的 考纲内
注意发音 /ˈθʌrə/。a thorough understanding = 全面的理解。
区分：thorough（彻底的）vs through（通过）vs though（虽然）vs thought（想法）
thoroughly adv. 彻底地
tolerance 容忍，耐受 考纲内
tolerate v. 容忍 → tolerance n. 容忍/耐受度。risk tolerance = 风险承受能力。
zero tolerance 零容忍  ·  tolerant adj. 宽容的  ·  be tolerant of 对…宽容
sustainable 可持续的 高频
sustain v. 维持 → sustainable adj. 可持续的。sustainable dining options = 可持续的用餐选择。
sustainable development 可持续发展  ·  sustainability n. 可持续性  ·  反义：unsustainable
responsibly 负责任地 考纲内
responsible adj. 负责的 → responsibly adv. 负责任地。travel more responsibly = 更负责任地旅行。
act responsibly 负责任地行事  ·  responsibility n. 责任  ·  反义：irresponsibly
accounted for 占（比例） 高频
account for = 占…比例/解释原因。accounted for about 8% = 占了大约8%。
account n. 账户/描述  ·  take sth into account 将…考虑在内  ·  on account of 因为
expense 代价，费用 高频
at one's expense = 以…为代价（固定搭配）。at the planet's expense = 以地球为代价。
travel expenses 旅行费用  ·  at the expense of 以…为代价  ·  expensive adj. 昂贵的
