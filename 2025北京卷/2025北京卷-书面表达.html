2025北京卷书面表达 · 想象力与创新

Q40–44
第一节（共4题，满分12分）
阅读下面短文，根据题目要求用英文回答问题。

There's something magical about the way imagination works. Around the world, kids are using imagination in ways that were once only found in science fiction.

Take the story of Mark Lee. He loved biking around his neighbourhood but grew upset seeing that many plastic bottles littered the parks and sidewalks. Instead of simply complaining, he decided to do something about it. Using an old 3D printer, Mark started experimenting. He melted (溶化) plastic bottles down and tried shaping them into small bricks. At first, everything went wrong — the plastic broke, the printer jammed, and the pieces didn't fit together. Friends laughed a little, and even Mark's parents thought he should just give up.

But Mark had a different idea. He redesigned the bricks to lock together like puzzle (拼图) pieces and adjusted the heating temperature carefully. After months of trial and error, he succeeded. With a little help from his school and the local community centre, Mark used his bricks to build a small bike shelter for his neighbourhood.

And he's not alone. Thirteen-year-old Sally Carter loved reading fantasy books. Inspired by magical maps in her stories, she wondered: what if we could build a real-world app that helps people explore hidden places in their own cities? She gathered friends, learned some basic programming, and together they created an app that maps small local parks and gardens few people know about. Their app quickly grew popular in town and even won a local youth innovation award.

Next time you see a young person lost in thought, don't rush to pull them back. They might just be imagining the next great idea.

40. What upset Mark when he was biking around his neighbourhood? 2分
参考答案
He was upset seeing that many plastic bottles littered the parks and sidewalks.
定位第二段第二句："grew upset seeing that many plastic bottles littered the parks and sidewalks"。直接引用原文关键信息即可。
41. How does the app created by Sally and her friends help? 2分
参考答案
It helps people explore hidden places / maps small local parks and gardens few people know about.
定位第四段："an app that maps small local parks and gardens few people know about"以及前文"helps people explore hidden places in their own cities"。两种表述均可。
42. Please decide which part is false in the following statement, then underline it and explain why. 3分
"With encouragement from his friends and parents, Mark built a small bike shelter for his neighbourhood after months of trial and error."
参考答案
下划线标注：encouragement from his friends and parents
原文说朋友笑话他（"Friends laughed a little"），父母觉得他应该放弃（"even Mark's parents thought he should just give up"）。实际帮助他的是学校和社区中心（"With a little help from his school and the local community centre"）。因此"encouragement from his friends and parents"是错误的。
43. How can you put your imagination into practice as Mark and Sally did? (In about 40 words) 5分
参考答案（开放性，言之有理即可）
I would use my imagination to address a real problem in my community. For example, I could design an interactive map of local recycling points using basic coding skills, making it easier for residents to sort and recycle their waste properly.
评分要点（5分）：
① 提出自己如何将想象力付诸实践（2分）
② 举具体例子说明（2分）
③ 语言表达准确流畅（1分）
写作建议：回应文中两个案例（解决实际问题+运用技术手段），结合自身经历，约40词。
第二节（满分20分）
写作任务：假设你是红星中学高三学生李华。联合国正面向全球青少年开展倡议征集活动。你的外国好友Jim打算参加，为此发来邮件，就倡议内容咨询你的建议。请你用英文给他回复，内容包括：

（1）提出的建议
（2）建议的理由

提示词：倡议 proposal
要求：词数100左右。
Dear Jim,
I'm thrilled to hear that you're planning to submit a proposal to the United Nations youth initiative. Here's my suggestion.
I'd recommend proposing a global youth environmental action network. The idea is to connect teenagers from different countries to work together on local environmental projects, such as community clean-ups, tree planting, and waste reduction campaigns. The reason I suggest this topic is that environmental protection is a shared challenge that requires global cooperation, and young people have the energy and creativity to make a real difference. Moreover, such a network would promote cross-cultural understanding while tackling one of the world's most pressing issues.
I hope this helps! Best of luck with your proposal.
Yours,
Li Hua
评分要点（20分）：
内容（8分）：明确提出建议（3分），给出充分理由（3分），开头/结尾得体（2分）。
语言（8分）：词汇多样（initiative / proposal / tackling / promote）、句式多样（I'd recommend... / The reason I suggest... / Moreover...）、语法准确。
格式（4分）：书信格式完整（Dear Jim 开头，Yours 署名），段落清晰，词数100词左右。
重点词汇（10个） ▼
imagination 想象力 高频
imagine v. 想象 → imagination n. 想象力。There's something magical about the way imagination works. = 想象力运作的方式有某种神奇之处。
imagination runs wild 想象力天马行空  ·  beyond imagination 超乎想象
experimenting 实验，尝试 考纲内
experiment v./n. 实验 → experimenting 现在分词。Mark started experimenting. = Mark开始做实验。
conduct an experiment 进行实验  ·  experiment with 尝试/试验
adjusted 调整 考纲内
adjust v. 调整，适应 → adjusted 过去式/过去分词 → adjustment n. 调整。
adjusted the heating temperature carefully = 仔细调整了加热温度  ·  adjust to 适应…  ·  make an adjustment 做出调整
trial and error 反复试验 高频
trial 试验 + error 错误 → 通过不断尝试和纠错来找到解决方法。
after months of trial and error = 经过数月的反复试验  ·  learn by trial and error 在摸索中学习
shelter 遮蔽处，庇护所 考纲内
shelter n. 遮蔽处，避难所；v. 庇护，遮蔽。a small bike shelter = 一个小型自行车棚。
take shelter from the rain 躲雨  ·  animal shelter 动物收容所  ·  shelter sb from sth 保护某人免受…
innovation 创新 高频
innovate v. 创新 → innovation n. 创新 → innovative adj. 创新的。
a local youth innovation award = 一个地方青年创新奖  ·  technological innovation 技术创新  ·  innovative thinking 创新思维
programming 编程 拓展
program n. 程序 → programming n. 编程。learned some basic programming = 学了一些基础编程。
computer programming 计算机编程  ·  programming language 编程语言  ·  learn to program 学编程
initiative 倡议，主动性 考纲内
initiate v. 发起 → initiative n. 倡议/主动性。the United Nations youth initiative = 联合国青年倡议活动。
take the initiative 采取主动  ·  on one's own initiative 主动地  ·  a government initiative 政府倡议
proposal 提案，建议书 考纲内
propose v. 提议 → proposal n. 提案/建议书。submit a proposal = 提交一份提案。
put forward a proposal 提出建议  ·  accept/reject a proposal 接受/拒绝提案  ·  a research proposal 研究计划书
tackling 处理，解决 高频
tackle v. 处理，应对（难题）→ tackling 现在分词。tackling one of the world's most pressing issues = 解决世界上最紧迫的问题之一。
tackle a problem 处理问题  ·  tackle challenges 应对挑战
