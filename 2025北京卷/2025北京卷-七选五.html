2025北京卷七选五 · What Is Adulthood?

Q35–39
七选五阅读理解
根据短文内容，从短文后的选项中选出能填入空白处的最佳选项。选项中有两项为多余选项。
What is adulthood? Societies all over the world view the concept of an adult as self-supporting and self-directed, because it identifies a point in the life cycle.
As Alice Black points out, today's parents' fear of letting kids do more housework has added to their inability to feel competent and, thus, confident in themselves. Psychologist Robert Turner wrote about this in his book, The Life Cycle, mentioning a stage when kids learn self-direction and mastery. [35]G
But Turner's other cycles are just as important. [36]E If these life cycles are delayed, interrupted, or denied, you feel incapable of accessing your states of self, controlling unpleasant feelings, and manipulating (操控) the energy that puts together the fabric of your inner world.
Turner wisely said that "doing well" psychologically is being able to roll with the flow of emotions, manage them, communicate clearly and well with others, and master various skills. This requires an internal locus (核心) of control, which implies self-control of your thoughts, feelings, and actions. [37]D You're self-directed and not reliant on the world to tell you who you are. And you know who you are within all this.
So, being an adult isn't just holding a job. Being an adult is about having a rooted sense of self while being capable of identifying and managing your emotions and actions. [38]C We are a social species, so this is important.
While raising an adult is a parent's job, it's also our responsibility as a society to ensure that we support schools that nurture (滋养) emotional and identity development. [39]A
选项 A–G（有两项多余）
A.It's also our job to support adults who need help repairing incomplete life cycles.
B.They talk about someone's inability to support themselves through work.
C.It's being able to show understanding when communicating with others.
D.In other words, you're playing the game of life — life isn't playing you.
E.They teach emotional awareness and management as well as identity.
F.You feel like an 18-year old, especially when playing 80s music.
G.When this stage is denied it creates a sense of insecurity.
答案解析（35—39题）▼
35题 答案：G — When this stage is denied it creates a sense of insecurity.
上文：mentioning a stage when kids learn self-direction and mastery.
逻辑：上文提到Turner描述的一个孩子学习 self-direction 和 mastery 的阶段，G 句说如果这个阶段被否定/缺失，就会产生不安全感。this stage 回指上文提到的那个阶段，代词衔接紧密。
排除 B/F：B 说"谈论某人无法通过工作养活自己"与此处语境无关；F 说"感觉像18岁"是干扰项，与学术论述语气不符。
36题 答案：E — They teach emotional awareness and management as well as identity.
上文：But Turner's other cycles are just as important.
逻辑：上文说Turner的其他 cycles 也很重要，E 句说它们教授情感意识、管理以及身份认同。They 指代上文的 other cycles。下文接着说如果这些 life cycles 被延误/中断就会有问题，逻辑通顺。
排除 B：B 的 They 指代不明，且内容与"其他cycles的重要性"无关。
37题 答案：D — In other words, you're playing the game of life — life isn't playing you.
上文：This requires an internal locus of control, which implies self-control of your thoughts, feelings, and actions.
逻辑：上文说自我控制意味着控制自己的思想、情感和行为，D 句用 In other words 承接，换一种方式解释：你在玩人生的游戏——而不是人生在玩你。生动形象地诠释了"内在控制核心"的含义。下文"You're self-directed"继续呼应。
38题 答案：C — It's being able to show understanding when communicating with others.
上文：Being an adult is about having a rooted sense of self while being capable of identifying and managing your emotions and actions.
逻辑：上文说成年是有自我意识并能管理情绪和行为，C 句补充成年的社会性维度——在与他人沟通时能表现出理解。It's 承接上文对"成年"的定义，与下文"We are a social species, so this is important"（我们是社会物种）紧密呼应。
39题 答案：A — It's also our job to support adults who need help repairing incomplete life cycles.
上文：it's also our responsibility as a society to ensure that we support schools that nurture emotional and identity development.
逻辑：上文说社会有责任支持学校的情感与身份认同教育，A 句说同样，我们也有责任帮助需要修复不完整生命周期的成年人。It's also our job... 与上文 it's also our responsibility 形成并列递进，从支持学校扩展到支持成年人。

七选五做题技巧

  • 看衔接词：In other words / But / While / It's also 等是关键线索，直接锁定逻辑关系（转折/递进/因果/解释）。
  • 找代词呼应：代词（they / this / it）必须指向上文某个具体名词；代词能对上，选项通常就对了。
  • 读前后句：空格填进去后，上下句必须"话题连贯"——前句话题收尾，所选句承接，下句继续展开。
  • 排除法最有效：先排除话题不符或逻辑矛盾的选项（B 与上下文语境无关；F 语气不符学术论述）。
重点词汇（10个） ▼
adulthood 成年期 考纲内
adult n. 成年人 → adulthood n. 成年期。What is adulthood? = 什么是成年？
reach adulthood 到达成年期  ·  childhood / adulthood / old age 人生三阶段
self-directed 自我导向的 拓展
self-（自我）+ directed（引导的）= 自己指导自己行为的。
a self-directed learner 自主学习者  ·  相关词：self-supporting 自给自足的、self-reliant 自力更生的
competent 有能力的 高频
competence n. 能力 → competent adj. 有能力的、胜任的。
feel competent = 感到自己有能力  ·  反义词：incompetent 无能的  ·  a competent teacher 称职的教师
mastery 精通，掌握 考纲内
master v. 精通 → mastery n. 精通/掌控。
learn mastery = 学会掌控  ·  mastery of a skill 对某项技能的精通  ·  achieve mastery 达到精通
incapable 无能力的 考纲内
in-（否定前缀）+ capable（有能力的）= 无能力的。
feel incapable of doing sth = 感到无法做某事  ·  反义词：capable 有能力的
manipulating 操控，控制 拓展
manipulate v. 操控 → manipulating（现在分词/动名词）。本文指操控/调节内心能量（中性义）。
也有贬义：manipulate others 操纵他人  ·  manipulation n. 操控
locus 核心，中心 拓展
拉丁语词，意为"位置/中心"。心理学术语：locus of control = 控制核心。
internal locus of control 内在控制核心（相信自己掌控命运） ·  external locus of control 外在控制核心
reliant 依赖的 考纲内
rely v. 依赖 → reliant adj. 依赖的 → reliance n. 依赖。
not reliant on = 不依赖于…  ·  self-reliant = 自力更生的  ·  heavily reliant on 严重依赖
nurture 滋养，培育 高频
nurture v./n. 培养、滋养。nurture emotional development = 滋养情感发展。
nature vs. nurture 先天与后天（经典辩题） ·  nurture talent 培养人才
insecurity 不安全感 考纲内
in-（否定）+ security（安全）= 不安全/不安全感。
a sense of insecurity = 一种不安全感  ·  反义词：security 安全感  ·  insecure adj. 不安全的
