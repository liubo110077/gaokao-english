Life Stories & Identity
We are all storytellers. From the moment we learn to talk, we begin weaving narratives about our experiences. But psychologist Dan McAdams argues that these stories do far more than simply record events — they actually shape who we are.
我们都是故事讲述者。从学会说话的那一刻起，我们就开始编织关于自身经历的叙事。但心理学家丹·麦克亚当斯认为，这些故事的作用远不止记录事件——它们实际上塑造了我们是谁。
McAdams has spent decades studying what he calls "narrative identity" — the internalized, evolving story that each person constructs to make sense of his or her life. This is not merely a summary of what has happened to us. It is a selective, interpretive account that highlights certain events while downplaying others, creating a sense of purpose and continuity.

这句的核心结构是：It is not A. It is B that C while D, creating E。先说"不仅仅是"（not merely a summary），再说"而是"（a selective, interpretive account）。that 从句说明这个叙述做了什么：highlights certain events while downplaying others。最后 creating... 是现在分词作结果状语。

核心意思：叙事认同不只是客观总结，而是一种有选择性的、带有个人解读的叙述，它突出某些事件、淡化另一些，从而创造出目标感和连贯性。

麦克亚当斯花了数十年研究他所称的"叙事认同"——每个人为了理解自己的人生而构建的一个内化的、不断演变的故事。这不仅仅是对我们所经历之事的总结。它是一种有选择性的、带有解读的叙述，突出某些事件，同时淡化其他事件，从而创造出一种目标感和连贯性。
长难句拆解
主语 + 系动词It is 表语a selective, interpretive account that 定语从句that highlights certain events while downplaying others, 结果状语creating a sense of purpose and continuity.
1
先读主干：It is a selective, interpretive account→它是一种有选择性的、解释性的叙述
2
that 定语从句：that highlights certain events while downplaying others→突出某些事件，同时淡化其他事件
3
结果状语：creating a sense of purpose and continuity→从而创造出目标感和连贯性
💡 「V-ing 作结果状语」是高考长难句常见结构——前面的动作自然导致后面的结果。注意 while 在这里表示"同时"，不是"虽然"。
Consider a man named Bob Johnson who lost his job during an economic downturn. One version of his story might go: "Losing my job was devastating, but it forced me to reconsider my priorities. I went back to school, retrained, and eventually found work that I truly love." McAdams would classify this as a "redemption sequence" — a narrative pattern in which a negative event is transformed into a positive outcome.
想想一个叫鲍勃·约翰逊的人，他在经济衰退期间失去了工作。他的故事的一个版本可能是这样的："失业对我来说是毁灭性的打击，但它迫使我重新审视自己的优先事项。我重返校园，重新学习技能，最终找到了自己真正热爱的工作。"麦克亚当斯会将此归类为"救赎序列"——一种负面事件被转化为积极结果的叙事模式。
But the same events can be narrated quite differently. Another version: "I lost my job and everything fell apart. Even when things seemed to improve, something always went wrong again." This is what McAdams terms a "contamination sequence" — good or hopeful moments are spoiled by subsequent negative outcomes.
但同样的事件可以被截然不同地叙述。另一个版本是："我失去了工作，一切都崩塌了。即使事情似乎有所好转，总有什么东西又出了问题。"这就是麦克亚当斯所称的"污染序列"——好的或充满希望的时刻被随后的负面结果所破坏。
Research consistently shows that people who construct redemption narratives tend to report higher levels of well-being, greater generativity — a concern for guiding the next generation — and stronger mental health.

主干是 Research shows that ... 。that 从句中，主语是 people who construct redemption narratives（构建救赎叙事的人），谓语是 tend to report，宾语是三个并列项：higher levels of well-being, greater generativity, and stronger mental health。破折号中间的 a concern for guiding the next generation 是对 generativity 的解释。

Those who habitually tell contamination stories, by contrast, are more likely to experience depression and a diminished sense of purpose.
研究一致表明，构建救赎叙事的人往往报告更高水平的幸福感、更强的繁衍关怀——一种引导下一代的关切——以及更强的心理健康。相比之下，那些习惯性地讲述污染型故事的人更有可能经历抑郁和目标感的削弱。
长难句拆解
谁Research 修饰consistently 表明shows that 内容people who construct redemption narratives tend to report higher levels of well-being, greater generativity — a concern for guiding the next generation — and stronger mental health.
1
先读主干：Research shows that ...→研究表明……
2
that 从句主语：people who construct redemption narratives→构建救赎叙事的人
3
谓语 + 宾语（三个并列项）：tend to report ① higher levels of well-being, ② greater generativity, ③ and stronger mental health→往往报告更高的幸福感、更强的代际关怀、更强的心理健康

💡 破折号中间的内容是对前面生僻词的解释，高考文章常用这种方式帮你理解术语——看到破折号要利用它来猜词义。
The encouraging finding from McAdams' work is that our life stories are not fixed. With reflection and sometimes professional guidance, people can learn to reinterpret their experiences, finding threads of growth and meaning even in painful chapters. The stories we tell ourselves, it turns out, are not just reflections of our lives — they are among the most powerful tools we have for shaping them.

主干是 The stories are not just A — they are B。主语 The stories 后面有定语从句 we tell ourselves 修饰。it turns out 是插入语（事实证明）。破折号后面用 they are among the most powerful tools 进一步加强语气。we have for shaping them 是定语从句修饰 tools。

麦克亚当斯研究中令人鼓舞的发现是，我们的人生故事并非一成不变。通过反思，有时在专业指导下，人们可以学会重新解读自己的经历，即使在痛苦的人生篇章中也能找到成长和意义的线索。事实证明，我们讲给自己听的故事不仅仅是生活的映射——它们是我们塑造人生最强大的工具之一。

1

4

生词与短语（10个） ▼
narratives 叙事，故事 考纲内
narrate = 叙述，narrative = 叙事/故事（比 story 更学术正式）。
narratives about our experiences 关于我们经历的叙事  ·  narrative structure 叙事结构
devastating 毁灭性的 考纲内
devastate = 摧毁/使震惊。devastating = 毁灭性的、令人崩溃的。

subsequent 随后的 考纲内
sub- = 在…之后，sequent = 跟随的。subsequent = 随后发生的。
subsequent negative outcomes 随后的负面结果  ·  in subsequent years 在随后几年
diminished 减少的，削弱的 考纲内
diminish = 减少/削弱。diminished sense of purpose = 被削弱的目标感。
近义：reduced, weakened  ·  diminished hope 减弱的希望
chapters 篇章，阶段（比喻义） 考纲内
chapter 本义是书的"章节"，这里比喻人生的某个阶段。

weaving 编织，构建 拓展
weave = 编织（过去式 wove），这里是比喻：像编织一样构建故事。
weave a story 编故事  ·  weave together 编织在一起
internalized 内化的 拓展
internal = 内部的，internalize = 内化（把外部的东西变成自己内心的一部分）。
internalized story 内化的故事  ·  反义：externalized 外化的

题目解析（4题，31—34） ▼
31. According to McAdams, narrative identity is best described as _____.
A. a detailed record of all life events
B. a fixed story determined by childhood experiences
C. an evolving personal story that gives life meaning
D. a scientific method for studying human behavior
答案：C
"McAdams has spent decades studying what he calls 'narrative identity' — the internalized, evolving story that each person constructs to make sense of his or her life."

解题思路：细节题。根据第2段"the internalized, evolving story that each person constructs to make sense of his or her life"，叙事认同是一个不断演变的（evolving）、人们用来赋予人生意义的（make sense of life）个人故事。

A. a detailed record of all life events ✗原文说"not merely a summary"，不是详细记录
B. a fixed story determined by childhood experiences ✗原文说"evolving"而非"fixed"，第6段也说"not fixed"
C. an evolving personal story that gives life meaning ✓与"evolving story...make sense of life"完全对应
D. a scientific method for studying human behavior ✗叙事认同是一个心理概念，不是科学方法
32. The example of Bob Johnson is used to _____.
A. prove that job loss always leads to personal growth
B. criticize people who tell negative stories about their lives
C. illustrate the difference between redemption and contamination sequences
D. suggest that economic downturns have psychological benefits
答案：C
"Consider a man named Bob Johnson who lost his job during an economic downturn."

解题思路：写作目的题。Bob Johnson的例子占了第3-4段，分别展示了同一事件的两种叙述方式：救赎序列（坏事变好事）和污染序列（好事被坏结局毁掉）。作者用这个例子来说明这两种叙事模式的区别。

A. prove that job loss always leads to personal growth ✗"always"过于绝对，原文只是展示一种可能的叙事
B. criticize people who tell negative stories about their lives ✗作者没有批评讲负面故事的人
C. illustrate the difference between redemption and contamination sequences ✓两段分别展示redemption和contamination，正是为了说明区别
D. suggest that economic downturns have psychological benefits ✗例子重点不在经济衰退的好处
33. What does the last paragraph suggest about life stories?
A. They should only be changed with professional help.
B. They are accurate representations of what really happened.
C. They become more negative as people grow older.
D. They can be reshaped to promote personal growth.
答案：D
"With reflection and sometimes professional guidance, people can learn to reinterpret their experiences, finding threads of growth and meaning even in painful chapters."

解题思路：推断题。最后一段说"our life stories are not fixed"且"people can learn to reinterpret their experiences, finding threads of growth"——人们可以学会重新解读经历，从中找到成长的线索。

A. They should only be changed with professional help. ✗原文说"with reflection and sometimes professional guidance"，不是"only"
B. They are accurate representations of what really happened. ✗全文强调故事是"selective, interpretive"的，不是准确再现
C. They become more negative as people grow older. ✗文中没有提到年龄与故事正负面的关系
D. They can be reshaped to promote personal growth. ✓"reinterpret experiences, finding threads of growth" = 重塑故事以促进成长
34. What is the main idea of the passage?
A. The personal stories we construct profoundly influence our identity and well-being.
B. Psychologists have discovered the only correct way to interpret life events.
C. Negative life events inevitably lead to poor mental health outcomes.
D. People should avoid comparing their life stories with those of others.
答案：A
"But psychologist Dan McAdams argues that these stories do far more than simply record events — they actually shape who we are."

解题思路：主旨题。全文围绕McAdams的叙事认同理论展开：我们构建的人生故事（narrative identity）深刻地塑造了我们的身份认同和幸福感。

A. The personal stories we construct profoundly influence our identity and well-being. ✓概括了全文核心——personal stories influence identity and well-being
B. Psychologists have discovered the only correct way to interpret life events. ✗"only correct way"过于绝对，文章没有说只有一种正确方式
C. Negative life events inevitably lead to poor mental health outcomes. ✗"inevitably"不对，文章说的是叙事方式（而非事件本身）影响心理健康
D. People should avoid comparing their life stories with those of others. ✗文章讨论的是叙事认同，不是比较

我这篇的卡点：

标记：

阅读技巧：心理学/社科类文章怎么读 ▼

D篇常考心理学/社科类文章——作者围绕一个学术概念展开论述，题目考你能不能抓住核心术语、理解例子的作用、读懂转折结构。有3个核心策略：

① 抓住核心术语及其定义

本文有三个关键术语：narrative identity（叙事认同）、redemption sequence（救赎序列）、contamination sequence（污染序列）。它们都在文中有明确的解释——用破折号或 that 从句给出定义。遇到生僻术语不要慌，往后看一两行，定义通常就在附近。

② 例子服务于论点

Bob Johnson 的例子不是重点，重点是它说明了什么理论概念。同一个人、同一件事（失业），用两种不同方式讲述，分别对应 redemption 和 contamination 两种叙事模式。做写作目的题（如Q32）时，要问"作者举这个例子想说明什么"，而不是纠结例子本身的细节。

③ 注意"不是A而是B"的转折

本文两处关键转折："not merely a summary" → "a selective, interpretive account"（不仅是总结，而是有选择的解读）；"not fixed" → "can learn to reinterpret"（不是固定的，可以重新解读）。这种"否定 + 肯定"的结构里，肯定的部分往往就是作者的核心观点，也是出题的重点。

作文拓展：好句式 & 连接词速查 ▼
从文章偷来的好句式
do far more than simply V — they actually V
不仅仅是...实际上是...
原句：These stories do far more than simply record events — they actually shape who we are.
作文套用：Books do far more than simply entertain us — they actually broaden our horizons. 用来强调某事的作用远超预期，比 "not only...but also" 更有力。
not merely A. It is B that C while D, creating E
不仅仅是A，而是B
原句：This is not merely a summary... It is a selective, interpretive account that highlights certain events while downplaying others, creating a sense of purpose.
作文套用：Education is not merely the transfer of knowledge. It is a transformative process that shapes character while building skills, creating well-rounded individuals. 层层递进，非常高级。
what he calls / what McAdams terms
他所称的...
原句：McAdams has spent decades studying what he calls "narrative identity."
作文套用：Psychologist Carol Dweck introduced what she calls a "growth mindset." 引用他人概念时的学术化表达，比直接说 "He said..." 高级。
The encouraging finding is that ...
令人鼓舞的发现是...
原句：The encouraging finding from McAdams' work is that our life stories are not fixed.
作文套用：The encouraging finding from recent studies is that reading habits among teenagers are improving. 引出积极研究结论时非常好用。
not just A — they are B
不仅是A，更是B
原句：The stories we tell ourselves are not just reflections of our lives — they are among the most powerful tools we have for shaping them.
作文套用：Friends are not just companions in daily life — they are mirrors that help us understand ourselves. 用破折号连接，语气更强。
连接词速查（按功能分）
递进 / 补充
furthermore moreover in addition what's more not only...but also
比 "also" 和 "and" 正式，议论文首选
转折 / 对比
however yet while by contrast rather than
yet 用在句中；however 另起；by contrast 对比两个对象
结果 / 因此
as a result therefore consequently thus
thus 最简洁，thus + 分词短语直接高分
举例说明

for instance 比 for example 更口语化一点点，两者都可用
总结 / 结尾
in conclusion to sum up in short overall in a word
结尾段第一句用，考官扫一眼就知道你会写结论
顺序 / 衔接
first of all secondly finally meanwhile at the same time
列举多个原因/步骤时用，让文章结构清晰
