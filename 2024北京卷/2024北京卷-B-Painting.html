2024北京卷B篇精读 · Rediscovering Art

B
When I was a little girl, I liked drawing, freely and joyously making marks on the walls at home. In primary school, I learned to write using chalks. Writing seemed to be another form of drawing. I shaped individual letters into repeating lines, which were abstract forms, delightful but meaningless patterns.

which 非限定从句：which were abstract forms, delightful but meaningless patterns 是对 repeating lines 的补充说明——在小女孩眼中，那些反复写的字母只是抽象的、好看但无意义的图案。

两层修饰：abstract forms 先说"抽象的形状"，接着 delightful but meaningless patterns 进一步说"好看却无意义的图案"——两个名词短语并列，都是在解释 which。

小时候我喜欢画画，在家里的墙上自由快乐地涂涂画画。上小学后，我学会了用粉笔写字。写字似乎是画画的另一种形式。我把一个个字母写成重复的行列，在我眼里它们只是抽象的形状——好看但毫无意义的图案。
In secondary school, art was my favourite subject. Since I loved it so much I thought I was good at it. For the art O-level exam I had to present an oil painting. I found it difficult, but still hoped to pass. I failed, with a low grade. I'd been over-confident. Now I'd been declared talentless.
中学时，美术是我最喜欢的科目。因为太喜欢了，我以为自己很擅长。O-level 美术考试要求交一幅油画。我觉得很难，但仍然希望能及格。结果我没通过，分数很低。我之前太过自信了。现在我被判定为"没有天赋"。
But other channels of creativity stayed open: I went on writing poems and stories. Still, I went to exhibitions often. I continued my habitual drawing, which I now characterised as childish doodling (乱画).

which 从句：which I now characterised as childish doodling——作者把自己一直在画的东西定性为"幼稚的乱画"。which 指的是 habitual drawing。

心态变化：考试失败后，作者虽然没完全停止画画，但已经不再认真对待，把画画贬低为 childish doodling。

In my 30s, I made painter friends and learned new ways of looking at art. However, I couldn't let myself have a go at actually doing it. Though these new friends were abstract painters using oil paints, or were printmakers or sculptors, I took oil painting as the taboo (禁忌) high form I wasn't allowed to practice.

Though 让步从句：Though these new friends were abstract painters... or were printmakers or sculptors = 尽管新朋友们都是抽象画家、版画家或雕塑家（身边全是搞艺术的人）。

主句核心：I took oil painting as the taboo high form I wasn't allowed to practice = 我把油画视为一种我不被允许实践的禁忌的高级形式。

心理矛盾：身边都是艺术家朋友，但作者依然把油画当作自己不配碰的"禁区"——这是考试失败留下的心理阴影。

但其他创作渠道依然畅通：我继续写诗和故事。我仍然经常去看展览，也继续着习惯性的画画——不过我现在把它称为"幼稚的涂鸦"。三十多岁时，我交了画家朋友，学会了看待艺术的新方式。然而，我无法让自己真正去尝试画画。尽管这些新朋友都是用油彩的抽象画家、版画家或雕塑家，我却把油画视为一种禁忌的高级艺术形式——我不被允许去实践的东西。

One night, in my early 40s, I dreamed that a big woman in red approached me, handed me a bag of paints, and told me to start painting. The dream felt so authoritative that it shook me. It was a form of energy, giving me back something I'd lost. Accordingly, I started by experimenting with water colours. Finally, I bought some oil paints.

三个并列动词：a big woman in red ① approached me, ② handed me a bag of paints, and ③ told me to start painting——梦中的红衣女人做了三件事：走近、递给颜料、让她开始画画。

so...that 结构：The dream felt so authoritative that it shook me = 这个梦感觉如此有权威，以至于震动了我。so + 形容词 + that = 如此...以至于...（高考必考句型）。

一天晚上，大约四十岁出头的时候，我梦见一个穿红衣的高大女人朝我走来，递给我一袋颜料，让我开始画画。那个梦感觉如此有权威，以至于震撼了我。它是一种能量，把我失去的东西还给了我。于是，我先从水彩画开始尝试。最终，我买了一些油画颜料。
Although I have enjoyed breaking my decades-long taboo about working with oil paints, I have discovered I now prefer chalks and ink. I let my line drawings turn into cartoons I send to friends. It all feels free and easy. Un-anxious. This time around, I can accept my limitations but keep going.

Although 让步：Although I have enjoyed breaking my decades-long taboo about working with oil paints = 虽然我很享受打破持续几十年的油画禁忌。但主句转折：I have discovered I now prefer chalks and ink = 我发现自己现在更喜欢粉笔和墨水。

心态转变：从不敢碰油画 → 尝试油画 → 最终发现自己更喜欢粉笔和墨水。关键不是油画本身，而是"不再害怕"。

虽然我很享受打破持续几十年的油画禁忌，但我发现自己现在更喜欢粉笔和墨水。我让线描画变成了寄给朋友们的漫画。一切感觉自在轻松。没有焦虑。这一次，我能接受自己的不足，但继续画下去。
Becoming a successful painter calls for being resolute. I realised I was always afraid of wanting too much. That dream reminded me that those fears and desires could encourage me to take risks and make experiments.

主干：That dream reminded me that... = 那个梦提醒了我...。remind sb that = 提醒某人...（接宾语从句）。

fears and desires：恐惧和渴望——看似矛盾的两种情感，作者意识到它们反而可以推动自己去冒险和尝试。

全文核心观点：创作活动需要信心。恐惧和渴望不是障碍，而是动力。

要成为一名成功的画家，需要坚定的决心。我意识到自己一直害怕渴望太多。那个梦提醒了我：那些恐惧和渴望可以鼓励我去冒险、去尝试。
生词与短语（10个） ▼
abstract 抽象的 考纲内
abstract 的反义词是 concrete（具体的）。在艺术领域，abstract art = 抽象艺术。
which were abstract forms, delightful but meaningless patterns. （课文）
搭配：abstract art 抽象艺术；abstract thinking 抽象思维；abstract concept 抽象概念
delightful 令人愉快的，讨人喜欢的 考纲内
delight（名词/动词，快乐）→ delightful（形容词，令人愉快的）→ delighted（形容词，感到高兴的）。
delightful but meaningless patterns. （课文）
I was delighted to hear the news. 我很高兴听到这个消息。（注意区分：delightful 修饰事物，delighted 修饰人）
characterised 把...描述为，将...定性为 考纲内
character = 性格/特征，characterise = 描述某事物的特征、将其定性为。characterised as = 把...称为、归类为。
which I now characterised as childish doodling. （课文）
= describe as, label as. 搭配：be characterised by 以...为特征
habitual 习惯性的，惯常的 拓展
habit = 习惯（名词），habitual = 习惯性的（形容词）。habitual drawing = 已经成为习惯的涂画。
I continued my habitual drawing. （课文）
搭配：habitual behavior 习惯性行为；a habitual liar 惯性说谎者
taboo 禁忌 考纲内
taboo = 不可触碰的禁忌，社会或个人认为不应该做的事。作者把油画视为自己的个人禁忌。
I took oil painting as the taboo high form I wasn't allowed to practice. （课文）
breaking my decades-long taboo. （课文）
搭配：a social taboo 社会禁忌；break a taboo 打破禁忌
authoritative 权威的，不容置疑的 拓展
authority = 权威（名词），authoritative = 有权威的、令人信服的（形容词）。
The dream felt so authoritative that it shook me. （课文）
搭配：an authoritative voice 权威的声音；an authoritative source 权威来源
accordingly 于是，因此；相应地 考纲内
accord = 一致，accordingly = 相应地、因此。常用作过渡词，连接前因后果。
Accordingly, I started by experimenting with water colours. （课文）
= Therefore, As a result. 写作常用过渡词：Accordingly, we should take action.
experimenting 尝试，做实验 考纲内
experiment = 实验（名词/动词）。experiment with = 尝试某种新事物。experimenting with water colours = 尝试画水彩。
I started by experimenting with water colours. （课文）
搭配：experiment with new ideas 尝试新想法；conduct an experiment 做实验
resolute 坚定的，有决心的 考纲内
resolve = 下决心（动词），resolute = 坚定不移的（形容词），resolution = 决心、决议（名词）。
Becoming a successful painter calls for being resolute. （课文）
近义：determined, firm. 搭配：a resolute decision 坚定的决定；remain resolute 保持坚定
limitations 局限，不足之处 考纲内
limit = 限制（动词/名词），limitation = 局限性、不足（名词）。accept my limitations = 接受自己的不足。
I can accept my limitations but keep going. （课文）
搭配：know your limitations 了解自己的不足；overcome limitations 克服局限
题目解析（4题，24—27） ▼
24. How did the author feel about the result of the art exam?
A. Scared.
B. Worried.
C. Discouraged.
D. Wronged.
答案：C
"I'd been over-confident. Now I'd been declared talentless."

作者考试不及格后说自己"too over-confident"（过于自信），然后"been declared talentless"（被宣判没有天赋）。从此不敢碰油画，把画画贬低为"childish doodling"。这说明考试结果让她感到气馁、沮丧（discouraged）。

✗ A— Scared（害怕的）：文中没有恐惧的描述，只是灰心丧气。
✗ B— Worried（担心的）：文中表达的不是担忧，而是对自己能力的否定。
✓ C— Discouraged（气馁的）：考试失败后认为自己没有天赋，不敢再碰油画 = 气馁、沮丧。
✗ D— Wronged（觉得被冤枉）：作者没有抱怨不公平，反而说自己 over-confident，承认了错误。
25. In her 30s, the author _________.
A. avoided oil painting practice
B. sought for a painting career
C. fancied abstract painting
D. exhibited child paintings
答案：A
"However, I couldn't let myself have a go at actually doing it."
"I took oil painting as the taboo high form I wasn't allowed to practice."

30多岁时，作者虽然交了画家朋友，但原文说"I couldn't let myself have a go at actually doing it"（无法让自己真正去尝试），还把油画当成"taboo"（禁忌）。这说明她回避了油画实践。

✓ A— 原文明确说"couldn't let myself have a go"且将油画视为 taboo = 回避油画实践。
✗ B— sought for a painting career（追求画家职业）：文中没有提到她想以画画为职业。
✗ C— fancied abstract painting（喜欢抽象画）：她的朋友是抽象画家，但没说她自己喜欢抽象画。
✗ D— exhibited child paintings（展出儿童画）：文中没有提到展出任何作品。
26. Which word would best describe the author's dream?
A. Confusing.
B. Empowering.
C. Disturbing.
D. Entertaining.
答案：B
"It was a form of energy, giving me back something I'd lost."

作者用"a form of energy"（一种能量）和"giving me back something I'd lost"（把我失去的东西还给我）来描述这个梦。梦给了她力量和勇气重新拿起画笔，这正是 empowering（赋予力量的）。

✗ A— Confusing（令人困惑的）：梦的信息很清楚——让她开始画画，并不令人困惑。
✓ B— Empowering（赋予力量的）：梦是"a form of energy"，让作者重获失去的东西，开始画画。
✗ C— Disturbing（令人不安的）："shook me"表示震撼而非不安，整体效果是正面的。
✗ D— Entertaining（有趣的）：这个梦的意义在于激励，不是娱乐。
27. What can we learn from this passage?
A. Actions speak louder than words.
B. Hard work is the mother of success.
C. Dreams are the reflections of realities.
D. Creative activities involve being confident.
答案：D
"Becoming a successful painter calls for being resolute."
"That dream reminded me that those fears and desires could encourage me to take risks and make experiments."

全文讲述了作者从考试失败失去信心 → 几十年不敢碰油画 → 通过一个梦重获勇气 → 重新开始创作的过程。最后两段点题：创作需要 resolute（坚定），恐惧和渴望可以推动冒险和尝试。全文核心就是：创造性活动需要自信（confident）。

✗ A— Actions speak louder than words（行动胜于语言）：文章不是在比较行动和语言。
✗ B— Hard work is the mother of success（勤奋是成功之母）：文章强调的是信心和勇气，不是勤奋。
✗ C— Dreams are reflections of realities（梦是现实的反映）：虽然提到了梦，但重点不是梦与现实的关系。
✓ D— Creative activities involve being confident（创造性活动需要自信）：全文核心——克服恐惧、重拾信心才能进行创作。
阅读原文▼

When I was a little girl, I liked drawing, freely and joyously making marks on the walls at home. In primary school, I learned to write using chalks. Writing seemed to be another form of drawing. I shaped individual letters into repeating lines, which were abstract forms, delightful but meaningless patterns.

In secondary school, art was my favourite subject. Since I loved it so much I thought I was good at it. For the art O-level exam I had to present an oil painting. I found it difficult, but still hoped to pass. I failed, with a low grade. I'd been over-confident. Now I'd been declared talentless.

But other channels of creativity stayed open: I went on writing poems and stories. Still, I went to exhibitions often. I continued my habitual drawing, which I now characterised as childish doodling. In my 30s, I made painter friends and learned new ways of looking at art. However, I couldn't let myself have a go at actually doing it. Though these new friends were abstract painters using oil paints, or were printmakers or sculptors, I took oil painting as the taboo high form I wasn't allowed to practice.

One night, in my early 40s, I dreamed that a big woman in red approached me, handed me a bag of paints, and told me to start painting. The dream felt so authoritative that it shook me. It was a form of energy, giving me back something I'd lost. Accordingly, I started by experimenting with water colours. Finally, I bought some oil paints.

Although I have enjoyed breaking my decades-long taboo about working with oil paints, I have discovered I now prefer chalks and ink. I let my line drawings turn into cartoons I send to friends. It all feels free and easy. Un-anxious. This time around, I can accept my limitations but keep going.

Becoming a successful painter calls for being resolute. I realised I was always afraid of wanting too much. That dream reminded me that those fears and desires could encourage me to take risks and make experiments.

阅读技巧▼

记叙文阅读策略：这篇是典型的"个人经历 + 感悟"文章，考点集中在情感变化和主旨领悟。

  • 时间线索词（little girl, primary school, secondary school, in my 30s, early 40s）→ 按时间顺序梳理心态变化
  • 转折词（but, however, though, although）→ 转折后面是重点，心态在这里发生变化
  • 最后一段 → 全文主旨句通常在这里：calls for being resolute = 需要信心和决心
  • 情感词定位法 → 题目问 feel / describe 时，直接在原文找情感词（over-confident, talentless, authoritative, energy, free, un-anxious）

本文心态变化线：自由画画（童年）→ 考试失败、失去信心（中学）→ 回避油画（30s）→ 梦中获得力量（40s）→ 重新创作、接受不足（现在）→ 感悟：创作需要自信。

作文拓展：好句式 & 好短语▼

可以直接用进作文的句子和短语：

  • Becoming a successful painter calls for being resolute.（成为成功的画家需要坚定的决心——calls for = 需要）
  • I can accept my limitations but keep going.（我可以接受不足但继续前行——接受不完美的心态）
  • Those fears and desires could encourage me to take risks and make experiments.（恐惧和渴望可以鼓励冒险和尝试）
  • 短语：have a go at（尝试做），calls for（需要），take risks（冒险），make experiments（做尝试）
