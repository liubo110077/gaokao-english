2024北京卷完形填空 · The Musical Audition

Q1–10
完形填空（共10题，每题1.5分）
阅读下面短文，从短文后各题所给的四个选项（A、B、C和D）中，选出可以填入空白处的最佳选项。
I'd just arrived at school, ready for another school day. I was reading a book in the classroom when there was an (1)announcement. "Today at 1:10 there will be auditions (面试) for a musical." My friends all jumped up in excitement and asked me, "Will you be going, Amy?" "Sure," I said. I had no (2)interest in drama, but I'd try out because my friends were doing it.
At 1:10, there was a (3)line outside the drama room. Everyone looked energetic. I hadn't expected I'd be standing there that morning. But now that I was doing it, I (4)suddenly felt nervous. What if I wasn't any good?
I entered the room and the teachers made me say some lines from the musical. They then (5)tested my singing skills and asked what role I wanted to play. The teachers were smiling and praising me. I felt like I had a (6)chance, so I said, "A big role." They said they'd look into it. I started getting really nervous. What if I didn't get a main role?
Soon, the cast list was (7)posted. My friends checked and came back shouting, "Amy, you got the main role!" Sure enough, my name was at the top. I just stared at it and started to (8)well up. I was so happy.
After two months we were all prepared and ready to go on stage. It was fun. And when people started (9)clapping, that gave me a boost of confidence. It stayed with me and made me feel (10)braver. I realised that by trying something new, I can have fun — even if it means stepping out of my comfort zone.
答案解析（1—10题）▼
1. C — announcement （通知，宣布）
教室里传来广播通知，说1:10有音乐剧面试。announcement = 公告/广播通知，与后面引号中的广播内容呼应。assignment（作业）/ initiative（倡议）/ interview（面试）均与"广播里听到消息"的场景不符。
2. B — interest （兴趣）
I had no interest in drama（对戏剧没有兴趣），but I'd try out because my friends were doing it——虽然没兴趣，但因为朋友们都去才跟着去。have no interest in = 对…没有兴趣。hesitancy（犹豫）/ worry（担心）/ regret（后悔）均与"因朋友才去参加"的转折逻辑不匹配。
3. D — line （排队的人）
1:10到了，戏剧教室外面排了一队人。a line = 一排/一队人（排队）。Everyone looked energetic 说明有很多人在等候。game（游戏）/ show（表演）/ play（戏剧）均不能描述"教室门外的队伍"。
4. A — suddenly （突然地）
我早上根本没想到自己会站在这里，但既然来了，我突然感到紧张。suddenly 强调紧张感来得突然、出乎意料。continuously（持续地）/ originally（最初）/ generally（通常）都不能表达"意外产生紧张感"的意思。
5. B — tested （测试）
老师让"我"念台词后，又测试了"我"的歌唱技巧。tested my singing skills = 测试我的歌唱能力，符合面试流程。advertised（宣传）/ challenged（挑战）/ polished（磨练）均与面试中老师评估考生的语境不符。
6. D — chance （机会）
老师们面带微笑地表扬我，我觉得自己有机会（had a chance），所以才敢说"给我一个大角色"。demand（要求）/ credit（信用）/ dream（梦想）均不能表达"觉得自己可能被选上"的意思。
7. B — posted （张贴）
不久后，演员名单被张贴出来了。the cast list was posted = 名单被公布/贴出来了。posted 是公布名单最常见的搭配。traded（交易）/ questioned（质疑）/ claimed（声称）均不能与 cast list 搭配。
8. A — well up （眼泪涌出）
看到名字在名单最顶端，"我"开始热泪盈眶。well up = 泪水涌出，表示极度开心时的感动。后面"I was so happy"也印证了是喜极而泣。roll in（大量涌入）/ stand out（突出）/ go off（响起/离开）均不符合"看到结果后感动落泪"的语境。
9. C — clapping （鼓掌）
演出时，观众开始鼓掌（clapping），这给了"我"信心。clapping 与 a boost of confidence 呼应，观众的掌声是对表演的肯定。whispering（窃窃私语）/ arguing（争论）/ stretching（伸展）均不能给表演者带来信心鼓舞。
10. D — braver （更勇敢的）
掌声带给"我"信心，让"我"觉得自己更勇敢了。全文主题是"走出舒适区"（stepping out of my comfort zone），braver 与这一主题完美呼应——从没兴趣到尝试，从紧张到自信，最终变得更勇敢。funnier（更滑稽）/ fairer（更公平）/ cleverer（更聪明）均与"走出舒适区"的主题不符。
重点词汇（10个）▼
auditions 面试，试镜 考纲内
audition = 为获得演出角色而进行的面试/试演。常用于表演、音乐等场景。
There will be auditions for a musical. （课文）
She auditioned for the lead role in the play. 她为这部戏的主角试镜。
energetic 精力充沛的 考纲内
energetic = 精力旺盛的、充满活力的。energy n. 能量/精力 → energetic adj.
Everyone looked energetic. （课文）
He is an energetic young man who loves sports. 他是个精力充沛、热爱运动的年轻人。
praise 表扬，赞美 考纲内
praise = 表扬、赞美（既可作动词也可作名词）。praise sb for sth = 因某事表扬某人。
The teachers were smiling and praising me. （课文）
The teacher praised her for her hard work. 老师表扬了她的努力。
cast list 演员名单 课文
cast = 全体演员/阵容，cast list = 选角名单/演员表。post the cast list = 张贴演员名单。
Soon, the cast list was posted. （课文）
I checked the cast list to see if I got a role. 我查看了演员名单看自己是否入选。
boost 提升，增强 考纲内
boost = 提升、增强（名词/动词）。a boost of confidence = 信心的提升/一股信心。
That gave me a boost of confidence. （课文）
The good news boosted his spirits. 好消息提振了他的精神。
confidence 信心，自信 考纲内
confidence = 信心、自信。confident adj. 自信的。with confidence = 自信地。
That gave me a boost of confidence. （课文）
She spoke with great confidence. 她说话非常自信。
comfort zone 舒适区 高频
comfort zone = 舒适区，指让人感到安全、不需要冒险的状态。step/get out of one's comfort zone = 走出舒适区。
Even if it means stepping out of my comfort zone. （课文）
Growth happens when you leave your comfort zone. 成长发生在你走出舒适区的时候。
stepping out 走出（舒适区） 课文
step out of = 走出、迈出（某个范围或状态）。stepping out of my comfort zone = 走出舒适区。
Even if it means stepping out of my comfort zone. （课文）
It takes courage to step out of your comfort zone. 走出舒适区需要勇气。
well up （眼泪）涌出 课文
well up = 泪水涌出、热泪盈眶。well 作动词表示液体"涌出、冒出"。
I just stared at it and started to well up. （课文）
Tears welled up in her eyes when she heard the news. 听到消息时她热泪盈眶。
announcement 宣布，通告 考纲内
announcement = 公告、通知。announce v. 宣布 → announcement n.。make an announcement = 发布通知。
There was an announcement. （课文）
The principal made an announcement over the loudspeaker. 校长通过广播发布了一条通知。
