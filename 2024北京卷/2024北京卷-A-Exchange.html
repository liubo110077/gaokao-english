The Language Exchange Programme allows students in pairs to communicate in two different languages they wish to share and learn each week.

句子结构：主干是 The Programme allows students to communicate（项目允许学生进行交流）。

in pairs：以两人一组的方式。

they wish to share and learn：省略了 that 的定语从句，修饰 two different languages（他们希望分享和学习的两种不同语言）。

与21题的关系：21题问学生在项目中将做什么 → 学生以两人一组进行语言交流，即 practise their language skills（练习语言技能）→ 答案 D。

Students record short entries after each partner meeting noting the language skills practised and the topics discussed. Each pair of students meets three times throughout the term with a teacher who decides if the exchange is effective.

句子结构：主干是 Each pair of students meets three times with a teacher（每组学生和老师见三次面）。

throughout the term：在整个学期期间（时间状语）。

who 定语从句：who decides if the exchange is effective = 由这位老师来判断交换是否有效。who 修饰 a teacher。

注意：21题选项 B（evaluate the exchange）有迷惑性，但原文说的是 teacher 来 decide，不是学生来评估。21题选项 C（meet teachers each week）也有迷惑性，原文是 three times throughout the term，不是每周。

Students who successfully complete the programme will receive one credit each.
语言交换项目允许学生两人一组，每周用他们希望分享和学习的两种不同语言进行交流。学生在每次搭档会面后记录简短的条目，记下练习过的语言技能和讨论的话题。每组学生在整个学期中与老师见三次面，由老师判断交换是否有效。成功完成项目的学生将各获得一个学分。

Since there are many factors involved in the pairing process, not all students who sign up will be matched with a partner and be able to register for the programme.

句子结构：Since 引导原因状语从句 + 主句。

题目解析（3题，21—23） ▼
21. In the programme, students will _________.
A. chair daily meetings
B. evaluate the exchange
C. meet teachers each week
D. practise their language skills
答案：D
"The Language Exchange Programme allows students in pairs to communicate in two different languages they wish to share and learn each week."

细节题。文章开头说项目允许学生"communicate in two different languages"（用两种不同语言交流），学生在每次会面后记录"the language skills practised"（练习过的语言技能）。选 D（practise their language skills = 练习语言技能）。

✗ A— chair daily meetings：原文是"weekly"（每周），不是daily（每天）；且学生是参加会议，不是主持（chair）。
✗ B— evaluate the exchange：原文说"a teacher who decides if the exchange is effective"，是老师来判断，不是学生来评估。
✗ C— meet teachers each week：原文是"meets three times throughout the term"（整个学期见三次），不是每周见老师。
✓ D— practise their language skills：与课文"communicate in two different languages"和"the language skills practised"直接对应。
22. To complete the programme, students are required to _________.
A. develop tests
B. participate in pair meetings
C. welcome new students
D. work on weekly videos
答案：B
"18 weekly one-hour pair meetings"

细节题，直接对照"Requirements for completion"列表。列表中明确要求"18 weekly one-hour pair meetings"（18次每周一小时的搭档会议）。选 B（participate in pair meetings = 参加搭档会议）。

✗ A— develop tests：原文提到"placement tests"（分班测试），但那是在注册阶段确认水平用的，不是学生自己开发测试。
✓ B— participate in pair meetings：与Requirements列表中的"18 weekly one-hour pair meetings"直接对应。
✗ C— welcome new students：原文是"One welcoming session"（一次欢迎会），是学生参加欢迎会，不是去欢迎新生。
✗ D— work on weekly videos：原文是"One five-minute final video"（一个最终视频），不是每周做视频。
23. What do students need to do during the registration?
A. Indicate their languages of interest.
B. Select their own coursework.
C. Make individual contact.
D. Choose their partners.
答案：A
"Students sign up and indicate the languages they can share and languages they are interested in learning."

细节题，定位到"Sign up!"部分第一条。学生报名时需要"indicate the languages they can share and languages they are interested in learning"（标注能分享的语言和想学的语言）。选 A（Indicate their languages of interest = 标注感兴趣的语言）。

✓ A— Indicate their languages of interest：与课文"indicate the languages...they are interested in learning"直接对应。
✗ B— Select their own coursework：原文提到 coursework 是用来确认 proficiency levels 的，不是学生自己选课。
✗ C— Make individual contact：原文说"students will be contacted individually"（学生被单独联系），是被动的，不是学生主动联系。
✗ D— Choose their partners：原文说配对是根据信息"identified"（识别出来的），不是学生自己选择搭档。
阅读原文▼

The Language Exchange Programme allows students in pairs to communicate in two different languages they wish to share and learn each week. Students record short entries after each partner meeting noting the language skills practised and the topics discussed. Each pair of students meets three times throughout the term with a teacher who decides if the exchange is effective. Students who successfully complete the programme will receive one credit each.

Requirements for completion

  • One welcoming session on the second Friday of the term
  • 18 weekly one-hour pair meetings
  • Weekly progress reports for all pair meetings
  • At least three pair-teacher meetings
  • One five-minute final video

Sign up!

The sign-up and registration process is as follows:

  • Students sign up and indicate the languages they can share and languages they are interested in learning.
  • Based on the information entered by each student, potential pairs are identified.
  • Proficiency levels are confirmed through coursework or placement tests.
  • Once a pair has been determined to be suitable, the students will be contacted individually with a special permission number to register for the programme.

Reminders

Signing up for the programme does not automatically mean that you will be able to register and participate. Pairs are matched by languages of interest and proficiency levels. Since there are many factors involved in the pairing process, not all students who sign up will be matched with a partner and be able to register for the programme.

阅读技巧▼

告示/通知类文章读法（A篇是此类文章的典型）：

  • 第一步：看标题和分段标题。本文有三个小标题："Requirements for completion""Sign up!""Reminders"。先扫描这些标题，知道文章分几部分、每部分讲什么。
  • 第二步：看题目，带着问题读。21题问"学生在项目中做什么"，定位到第一段；22题问"完成项目需要什么"，定位到Requirements部分；23题问"注册时做什么"，定位到Sign up部分。
  • 第三步：细节题要找原文依据，不能凭感觉。答案一定能在文中找到对应的原句，不然就是干扰项。

常见陷阱（本篇三道题都有）：

  • 主体混淆：21-B（evaluate the exchange）看似合理，但课文说的是 teacher 来 decide，不是学生来评估。
  • 频率偷换：21-C（meet teachers each week）vs 课文（meets three times throughout the term）——"每周"被偷换了。
  • 主被动混淆：23-C（Make individual contact）vs 课文（be contacted individually）——学生是"被"联系的，不是主动联系。
  • 细节偷换：22-D（weekly videos）vs 课文（One five-minute final video）——"一个最终视频"被偷换成了"每周视频"。
作文拓展：好句式 & 好短语▼

可以直接用进作文的高频表达：

  • indicate（表明/标注）—— The data indicates that students benefit from language exchange.
  • participate in（参加）—— I participated in a language exchange programme last year.
  • be determined to be（被确认为）—— The method was determined to be effective.
  • requirements for（...的要求）—— The requirements for the competition are as follows.
  • potential（潜在的）—— Language learning has great potential to broaden our horizons.

通知/告示类写作结构：

  • 开头：介绍项目目的（The programme allows / aims to / is designed to...）
  • 中间：分条说明要求/流程（Requirements: / The process is as follows: / Students are required to...）
  • 结尾：提醒注意事项（Please note that... / Signing up does not automatically mean...）
