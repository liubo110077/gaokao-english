C
The notion that we live in someone else's video game is irresistible to many. Searching the term "simulation hypothesis" returns numerous results that debate whether the universe is a computer simulation — a concept that some scientists actually take seriously. Unfortunately, this is not a scientific question. We will probably never know whether it's true. We can, instead, use this idea to advance scientific knowledge.

第一段的逻辑：作者先说模拟假说很有吸引力，然后说它不是科学问题、不可证明，但接着话锋一转（instead）给出建议：我们可以用这个想法来推进科学知识。

与28题的关系：作者挑战（challenge）了模拟假说的科学性，目的不是要否定它，而是提出建议（give a suggestion）——用它来推进科学。

我们生活在别人的电子游戏中这一想法，对很多人来说无法抗拒。搜索"模拟假说"一词会得到大量结果，讨论宇宙是否是一个电脑模拟——这个概念一些科学家还真的认真对待。不幸的是，这不是一个科学问题。我们可能永远不会知道它是否为真。但我们可以用这个想法来推进科学知识。
The 18th-century philosopher Kant argued that the universe ultimately consists of things-in-themselves that are unknowable. While he held the notion that objective reality exists, he said our mind plays a necessary role in structuring and shaping our perceptions.

句子结构：While 引导让步从句，主句是 he said our mind plays a necessary role in...

While he held the notion that...：虽然他认为客观现实存在...

our mind plays a necessary role in structuring and shaping our perceptions：我们的心智在构建和塑造我们的感知中起着必要的作用。

与30题的关系：作者引用康德的观点来支持自己的论点（宇宙不可知），态度是赞赏的（appreciative）。

Modern sciences have revealed that our perceptual experience of the world is the result of many stages of processing by sensory systems and cognitive functions in the brain. No one knows exactly what happens within this black box. If empirical experience fails to reveal reality, reasoning won't reveal reality either since it relies on concepts and words that are contingent on our social, cultural and psychological histories.

句子结构：if A fails, B won't either since... 如果A做不到，B也做不到，因为...

empirical experience fails to reveal reality：实证经验无法揭示真实（感官体验不能让我们看到真正的现实）。

reasoning won't reveal reality either：推理也揭示不了真实。

since it relies on concepts and words that are contingent on...：因为推理依赖于受制于（contingent on）我们的社会、文化、心理历史的概念和词语。

与29题的关系：contingent on = 取决于/依赖于 = determined by。推理和概念是被我们的历史背景所决定的。

Again, a black box.
18世纪的哲学家康德认为，宇宙最终由不可知的"物自体"构成。虽然他认为客观现实存在，但他说我们的心智在构建和塑造我们的感知中起着必要的作用。现代科学揭示，我们对世界的感知体验是大脑中感觉系统和认知功能经过多个阶段处理的结果。没有人确切知道在这个黑箱中发生了什么。如果实证经验无法揭示现实，推理也无法揭示现实，因为推理依赖于取决于我们社会、文化和心理历史的概念和词语。又是一个黑箱。

And then, we can shift our inquiry from "Is the universe a computer simulation?" to "Can we model the universe as a computer simulation?" Modelling reality is what we do. To facilitate our comprehension of the world, we build models based on conceptual metaphors that are familiar to us.

conceptual metaphors that are familiar to us：我们熟悉的概念隐喻——用我们已经熟悉的事物去描述/理解不熟悉的宇宙。

核心观点：人类一直在做的事就是用"模型"来理解世界，后面会举例（钟表、粒子物理）。

In Newton's era, we imagined the universe as a clock. In Einstein's, we uncovered the standard model of particle physics.
所以，如果我们接受宇宙是不可知的，我们也就接受我们永远不会知道我们是否生活在电脑模拟中。接下来，我们可以把探究的问题从"宇宙是电脑模拟吗？"转变为"我们能把宇宙建模为电脑模拟吗？"为现实建模是我们一直在做的事。为了促进我们对世界的理解，我们基于自己熟悉的概念隐喻来建立模型。在牛顿时代，我们把宇宙想象成一个钟表。在爱因斯坦时代，我们发现了粒子物理的标准模型。
Now that we are in the information age, we have new concepts such as the computer, information processing, virtual reality, and simulation. Unsurprisingly, these new concepts inspire us to build new models of the universe. Models are not the reality, however. There is no point in arguing if the universe is a clock, a set of particles or an output of computation.

关键句（31题定位）：There is no point in arguing if... 争论...是没有意义的。

列举三种模型：a clock（钟表）、a set of particles（粒子集合）、an output of computation（计算的产物）——分别对应牛顿时代、爱因斯坦时代、信息时代的模型。

作者态度：争论宇宙"是"什么模型没意义，因为模型都只是工具。

All these models are tools to deal with the unknown and to make discoveries. And the more tools we have, the more effective and insightful we can become.
既然我们已经身处信息时代，我们有了新的概念，比如计算机、信息处理、虚拟现实和模拟。毫不意外，这些新概念激励我们建立宇宙的新模型。然而，模型不是现实。争论宇宙到底是一个钟表、一组粒子还是一次计算的输出，没有任何意义。所有这些模型都是处理未知和进行发现的工具。而我们拥有的工具越多，我们就能变得越有效、越有洞察力。

阅读原文▼

The notion that we live in someone else's video game is irresistible to many. Searching the term "simulation hypothesis" returns numerous results that debate whether the universe is a computer simulation — a concept that some scientists actually take seriously. Unfortunately, this is not a scientific question. We will probably never know whether it's true. We can, instead, use this idea to advance scientific knowledge.

The 18th-century philosopher Kant argued that the universe ultimately consists of things-in-themselves that are unknowable. While he held the notion that objective reality exists, he said our mind plays a necessary role in structuring and shaping our perceptions. Modern sciences have revealed that our perceptual experience of the world is the result of many stages of processing by sensory systems and cognitive functions in the brain. No one knows exactly what happens within this black box. If empirical experience fails to reveal reality, reasoning won't reveal reality either since it relies on concepts and words that are contingent on our social, cultural and psychological histories. Again, a black box.

So, if we accept that the universe is unknowable, we also accept we will never know if we live in a computer simulation. And then, we can shift our inquiry from "Is the universe a computer simulation?" to "Can we model the universe as a computer simulation?" Modelling reality is what we do. To facilitate our comprehension of the world, we build models based on conceptual metaphors that are familiar to us. In Newton's era, we imagined the universe as a clock. In Einstein's, we uncovered the standard model of particle physics.

Now that we are in the information age, we have new concepts such as the computer, information processing, virtual reality, and simulation. Unsurprisingly, these new concepts inspire us to build new models of the universe. Models are not the reality, however. There is no point in arguing if the universe is a clock, a set of particles or an output of computation. All these models are tools to deal with the unknown and to make discoveries. And the more tools we have, the more effective and insightful we can become.

It can be imagined that comparable to the process of building previous scientific models, developing the "computer simulation" metaphor-based model will also be a hugely rewarding exercise.

阅读技巧▼

议论文的"目的题"解法：

  • 28题类型：问作者做某事的"意图"（intend to do），需要理解作者的写作目的，而不是表面行为。
  • 第一步：定位"challenging a hypothesis"——第一段作者说模拟假说不是科学问题（挑战了它的科学性）。
  • 第二步：看挑战之后作者做了什么——"We can, instead, use this idea to advance scientific knowledge"（提出建议）。
  • 答案规律：challenge 不是目的本身，challenge 后面的转折（instead/but/however）才是真正的目的。

词义猜测题：29题考 "contingent on"。解题方法：① 看所在句的逻辑（since = 因为，说明推理依赖于概念）；② 看上下文（概念受制于社会文化历史）→ 得出 contingent on = determined by（取决于）。

态度题：30题考作者对康德的态度。判断方法：作者引用康德后，说"if we accept..."，用了 accept（接受），并以康德的观点为基础展开全文论证 → 赞赏/认同（appreciative）。

作文拓展：好句式 & 好短语▼

可以直接用进作文的句式：

  • There is no point in arguing...（争论...没有意义）——用于表达"不值得争论"
  • To facilitate our comprehension of...（为了促进我们对...的理解）——目的状语开头，高分句式
  • The more tools we have, the more effective we can become.（the more...the more... 越...越...）
  • 短语：advance knowledge（推进知识），shift from...to...（从...转向...），comparable to（与...相似）
