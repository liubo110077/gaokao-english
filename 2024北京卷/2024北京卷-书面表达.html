Q40–44
第一节（共4题，满分12分）
阅读下面短文，根据题目要求用英文回答问题。

Growing up, I idealised independence. I always wanted my own efforts to be enough. When I decided to pursue a postgraduate degree, I wanted to develop a novel research programme and quickly establish myself as an independent scientist. But I was unrealistically optimistic about what I could achieve.

As I began designing experiments, my committee members warned me about the challenges I would face. But my need for independence drove me to push forward with my research plan. As a result, the first four years of my postgraduate career were defined by a series of failures.

During my second year, I failed my comprehensive exam because my proposal was unclear. During my third year, I discovered that after treating thousands of seeds, I obtained just one plant I could use for experiments. By my fourth year, my desperation to succeed overshadowed my desire for independence.

My adviser and I devised (想出) a somewhat unusual solution: I would spend three months in a collaborating lab to obtain specialised training. I worked extensively with other students, constantly asked questions, and helped with ongoing projects to learn everything I could. Finally, I conducted an elegant experiment that would not have been possible without the help of the members in the lab.

My adviser saw this experience as a groundbreaking success, emphasising the collaborating skills I acquired. A few months later, when I repeated the experiment in my home lab, I produced more publishable data. By learning when to ask for help, I eventually found myself on the way to becoming an independent scientist.

40. In the beginning, what drove the author to push forward with the research plan? 2分
参考答案
The need to be recognized as an independent scientist. / His need for independence drove him.
定位第二段"my need for independence drove me to push forward"。直接引用原文即可。
41. What was the solution by the adviser and the author after those repeated failures? 2分
参考答案
The author would spend three months in a collaborating lab to obtain specialised training.
定位第四段开头："I would spend three months in a collaborating lab to obtain specialised training"。
42. Please decide which part is false in the following statement, then underline it and explain why. 3分

"The adviser considered the author's experience in the lab a groundbreaking success because publishable data had been produced."
参考答案
错误部分是：because publishable data had been produced
原文说导师认为是成功是因为作者获得了合作技能（collaborating skills），而不是因为发表了数据。发表数据是几个月后回到自己实验室才做到的。
定位第五段："My adviser saw this experience as a groundbreaking success, emphasising the collaborating skills I acquired"。
43. From this story, what can you learn about "independence"? (In about 40 words) 5分
参考答案（开放性，言之有理即可）
True independence doesn't mean doing everything alone. It comes from knowing when to seek help and collaborate with others. The author learned that embracing collaboration actually helped him become the independent scientist he always wanted to be.
评分要点（5分）：
① 对"independence"有个人理解和思考（2分）
② 能结合文章内容分析（2分）
③ 语言表达准确流畅（1分）
写作建议：可以用 collaboration / seek help / ask for help 等文中核心词来呼应原文，字数约40词。
第二节（满分20分）
写作任务：假设你是红星中学高三学生李华。你的外国好友 Jim 准备给其校报的 Asia Today 栏目投稿。得知今年新中国成立 75 周年，他打算重点介绍中国的发展成就，发来邮件询问你的建议。请你用英文给他回复，内容包括：

（1）建议投稿内容
（2）就以上建议简要说明理由

要求：词数100左右。
Dear Jim,
I'm glad to hear you're writing about China's achievements on the occasion of the 75th anniversary. Here are my suggestions.
First, I'd recommend focusing on China's technological breakthroughs, such as the space programme and high-speed rail network. These achievements showcase the country's innovation capabilities and have significantly improved people's daily lives. Second, you might want to mention the remarkable progress in poverty reduction. Over the past decades, hundreds of millions of people have been lifted out of poverty, which is an extraordinary accomplishment.
I believe these topics will give your readers a comprehensive picture of China's development. Looking forward to reading your article!
Yours,
Li Hua
评分要点（20分）：
内容（8分）：建议的投稿内容具体（如科技成就、脱贫等），理由充分（各约3-4分），开头说明写信背景（1分），结尾表达期待（1分）。
语言（8分）：时态（一般现在时/现在完成时为主）、词汇多样（breakthrough / innovation / accomplishment）、句式多样（such as / which is / not only...but also）。
格式（4分）：Dear Jim 开头，署名 Li Hua，段落清晰，词数100词左右。
重点词汇（10个） ▼
idealise 理想化 考纲内
ideal n./adj. 理想 → idealise v.（英式）/ idealize（美式）= 把…理想化。
I idealised independence = 我把独立理想化了  ·  idealise the past 理想化过去
postgraduate 研究生 高频
post-（在…之后）+ graduate（毕业生）= 毕业后继续学习的 → 研究生。
a postgraduate degree = 研究生学位  ·  postgraduate career 研究生生涯  ·  同义：graduate student（美式）
comprehensive 综合性的 高频
com-（共同）+ prehend（抓住）→ 全面抓住的 → 综合的、全面的。
comprehensive exam = 综合考试（博士资格考试） ·  a comprehensive picture 全面的图景  ·  comprehensive school 综合学校
desperation 绝望，急迫 考纲内
desperate adj. 绝望的、急迫的 → desperation n. 绝望。
my desperation to succeed = 我想成功的急切心情  ·  in desperation 在绝望中  ·  out of desperation 出于绝望
collaborating 合作的 高频
collaborate v. 合作 → collaborating adj. 合作的 → collaboration n. 合作。
a collaborating lab = 一个合作的实验室  ·  collaborating skills 合作技能  ·  collaborate with sb. 与某人合作
specialised 专业化的 考纲内
special → specialise v. → specialised adj.（英式）/ specialized（美式）= 专门的、专业的。
specialised training = 专业训练  ·  specialised knowledge 专业知识  ·  specialist n. 专家
elegant 精妙的，优雅的 考纲内
an elegant experiment = 一个精妙的实验（指设计巧妙、简洁有效）。
elegant solution 精妙的解决方案  ·  elegant design 优雅的设计  ·  elegance n. 优雅
groundbreaking 开创性的 高频
ground（基础）+ breaking（打破的）= 打破旧基础的 → 开创性的。
a groundbreaking success = 一个开创性的成功  ·  groundbreaking research 开创性研究  ·  groundbreaking discovery 划时代发现
publishable 可发表的 拓展
publish v. 发表 → publishable adj. 可以发表的 → publication n. 出版物。
publishable data = 可以发表的数据  ·  publishable quality 可发表的质量  ·  publish a paper 发表论文
breakthrough 突破 高频
break（打破）+ through（穿过）= 突破。
technological breakthroughs = 科技突破  ·  a major breakthrough 重大突破  ·  make a breakthrough in 在…方面取得突破
