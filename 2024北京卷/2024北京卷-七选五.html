2024北京卷七选五 · Hedgehog or Fox?

Q35–39
七选五阅读理解
根据短文内容，从短文后的选项中选出能填入空白处的最佳选项。选项中有两项为多余选项。
If you want to develop maximum credibility (可信性), is it better to be a hedgehog (刺猬) or a fox? According to Isaiah Berlin, the hedgehog knows one thing very well, and the fox knows a lot of things.
Is there a clear advantage of one style over the other? Hedgehog thinkers tend to answer yes. [35]G And they are usually very credible in doing so. According to Jim Hart, the "hedgehog concept" is one of the factors that lead companies to greatness. They focus on one thing and do it really well. They figure out what they are good at. [36]C The hedgehog concept makes perfect sense for companies.
[37]D Philip Tate has studied the track records of those folks on the Sunday talk shows who make predictions about what will happen. He has found that hedgehogs are not only wrong more often than foxes, but that they are less likely to recognise or admit that they are wrong when events do not match their predictions.
The advantage that foxes have is that they are more likely to seek out new information from a broader range of sources, and are comfortable with uncertainty and new information. [38]F They try to include it in their viewpoint rather than to exclude it from their thinking. They also have a clearer estimation of what they know and don't know.
So, which is better? The question can be answered in a foxy hedgehog style. [39]A The choice between being a hedgehog or a fox is a false trade-off. The most effective way to go through life is to try to be that rare mixture known as foxy hedgehog.
选项 A–G（有两项多余）
A.In other words, there are clear advantages for each.
B.They are more likely to remember people's mistakes.
C.Hence, they have the advantage of clarity and confidence.
D.But there can be a downside to concentration on one big thing.
E.However, hedgehogs remain open to others' reactions and inputs.
F.When something is contradictory to their view, they don't treat it as exceptional.
G.They come down squarely on one side or the other and fully support their position.
答案解析（35—39题）▼
35题 答案：G — They come down squarely on one side or the other and fully support their position.
上文：Hedgehog thinkers tend to answer yes.
逻辑：上文说刺猬型思考者倾向于回答"是"（answer yes），G 句解释他们怎么回答——他们坚定地站在某一方并完全支持自己的立场（come down squarely on one side）。They 指代 hedgehog thinkers，语义承接流畅。
排除 B/E：B 说"更可能记住别人的错误"与上下文无关；E 说"刺猬型对他人反应保持开放"与刺猬专注特征矛盾。
36题 答案：C — Hence, they have the advantage of clarity and confidence.
上文：They focus on one thing and do it really well. They figure out what they are good at.
逻辑：上文说刺猬型公司专注一件事并做好它，弄清自己擅长什么，C 句用 Hence（因此）承接因果关系——因此他们有清晰和自信的优势。下文"The hedgehog concept makes perfect sense for companies"进一步肯定这一概念对公司是合理的。
37题 答案：D — But there can be a downside to concentration on one big thing.
下文：He has found that hedgehogs are not only wrong more often than foxes…
逻辑：上段说刺猬概念对公司很有道理，D 句用 But 转折——但专注一件大事也有不利之处（downside）。下文 Philip Tate 的研究发现刺猬型预测更容易出错、更不愿承认错误，正好具体说明了这个 downside。D 是段首句，起到"话题转换"作用。
38题 答案：F — When something is contradictory to their view, they don't treat it as exceptional.
上文：foxes … are comfortable with uncertainty and new information.
逻辑：上文说狐狸型善于从更广泛的来源寻找新信息，且能坦然接受不确定性和新信息。F 句具体解释狐狸型如何处理矛盾信息——当某事与他们的观点矛盾时，他们不把它当作例外。下文"They try to include it in their viewpoint"进一步解释：他们把矛盾信息纳入自己的观点而非排除。逻辑链条完整。
39题 答案：A — In other words, there are clear advantages for each.
下文：The choice between being a hedgehog or a fox is a false trade-off.
逻辑：上文提问"哪个更好"并说可以用"foxy hedgehog"风格回答，A 句用 In other words（换句话说）给出回答——两者各有明显优势。下文说"两者之间的选择是一个虚假的取舍"（false trade-off），与 A 句逻辑一致：既然各有优势，就不必二选一。
排除 B/E：B 与该段总结性质不符；E 与上文对刺猬型的批评矛盾。

七选五做题技巧

  • 看衔接词：Hence / But / In other words / When 等是关键线索，直接锁定逻辑关系（因果/转折/解释/条件）。
  • 找代词呼应：代词（they / it / this）必须指向上文某个具体名词；代词能对上，选项通常就对了。
  • 读前后句：空格填进去后，上下句必须"话题连贯"——前句话题收尾，所选句承接，下句继续展开。
  • 排除法最有效：先排除话题不符或逻辑矛盾的选项（B 与文章主题无关；E 与刺猬型特征矛盾）。
重点词汇（10个） ▼
credibility 可信性，信誉 考纲内
credible adj. 可信的 → credibility n. 可信性/信誉。
develop maximum credibility = 建立最大可信度  ·  lose credibility 失去信誉  ·  street credibility 街头声望
hedgehog 刺猬（比喻专注型思维者） 拓展
hedgehog 本义刺猬，文中比喻只专注一件事的思考者。出自以赛亚·柏林的著名比喻。
the hedgehog concept = 刺猬概念（专注核心优势） ·  对比 fox = 狐狸（博学型）
predictions 预测 高频
predict v. 预测 → prediction n. 预测/预言。
make predictions about what will happen = 预测将会发生什么  ·  weather prediction 天气预报
track records 过往记录，历史表现 考纲内
track record = 跑道上的成绩记录，引申为过去的表现/业绩。
study the track records = 研究过往表现  ·  have a good track record 有良好的过往记录
uncertainty 不确定性 高频
un-（否定）+ certain（确定的）+ -ty（名词后缀）= 不确定性。
comfortable with uncertainty = 坦然接受不确定性  ·  economic uncertainty 经济不确定性
contradictory 矛盾的，对立的 考纲内
contradict v. 反驳/与…矛盾 → contradictory adj. 矛盾的。
contradictory to their view = 与他们观点矛盾的  ·  contradictory statements 相互矛盾的说法
estimation 估计，判断 考纲内
estimate v. 估计 → estimation n. 估计/判断。
a clearer estimation of what they know = 对自己知道什么有更清楚的判断  ·  in my estimation 依我看来
trade-off 权衡取舍 高频
trade（交换）+ off = 为得到一样东西而放弃另一样。
a false trade-off = 虚假的取舍（不必二选一） ·  the trade-off between speed and quality 速度与质量的权衡
squarely 完全地，正面地 拓展
square adj. 方正的 → squarely adv. 直接地/完全地。
come down squarely on one side = 坚定地站在某一方  ·  face the problem squarely 正面面对问题
concentration 集中，专注 高频
concentrate v. 集中 → concentration n. 专注/集中。
concentration on one big thing = 专注于一件大事  ·  lose concentration 走神  ·  也指"浓度"：high concentration 高浓度
