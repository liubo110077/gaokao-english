Where to Eat in Bangkok
Bangkok is a highly desirable destination for food lovers. It has a seemingly bottomless well of dining options. Here are some suggestions on where to start your Bangkok eating adventure.
曼谷是美食爱好者梦寐以求的目的地。这座城市的餐饮选择多得几乎无穷无尽。以下是一些建议，帮你开启曼谷美食探险之旅。
Nahm
Offering Thai fine dining, Nahm provides the best of Bangkok culinary experiences. It's the only Thai restaurant that ranks among the top 10 of the world's 50 best restaurants list. Head Chef David Thompson, who received a Michelin star for his London-based Thai restaurant of the same name, opened this branch in the Metropolitan Hotel in 2010.

这句结构：Head Chef David Thompson... opened this branch in 2010。核心非常简单——汤普森大厨2010年在这家酒店开了这个分店。

中间 who received a Michelin star... of the same name 是插入的定语从句，只是在介绍他的背景：在伦敦也有一家同名泰国餐厅，还摘了米其林星。读懂核心后，再回来看插入部分。

Nahm 提供高档泰国美食，是曼谷最佳烹饪体验的代表。它是世界50家最佳餐厅榜单前十中唯一一家泰国餐厅。主厨 David Thompson 曾凭借他在伦敦的同名泰国餐厅荣获米其林星，并于2010年在大都会酒店开设了这家分店。
Issaya Siamese Club
Issaya Siamese Club is internationally known Thai chef Ian Kittichai's first flagship Bangkok restaurant. The menu in this beautiful colonial house includes traditional Thai cuisine combined with modern cooking methods.
Issaya Siamese Club 是国际知名泰国厨师 Ian Kittichai 在曼谷的第一家旗舰餐厅。这栋美丽的殖民风格建筑中，菜单将传统泰国菜肴与现代烹饪方法相结合。
Bo.lan
Bo.lan has been making waves in Bangkok's culinary scene since it opened in 2009. Serving hard-to-find Thai dishes in an elegant atmosphere, the restaurant is true to Thai cuisine's roots, yet still manages to add a special twist.

句子开头 Serving... 是分词短语作状语，说明餐厅是怎么运营的（以优雅氛围提供难觅的泰国菜肴）。

主句核心是：the restaurant is true to roots, yet manages to add a twist。yet 在这里相当于 but——虽然保持了正宗，但同时还能加入创意。两个方面缺一不可，这就是这家餐厅的卖点。

This place is good for a candle-lit dinner or a work meeting with colleagues who appreciate fine food. For those extremely hungry, there's a large set menu.
Bo.lan 自2009年开业以来便在曼谷美食界引起了广泛关注。餐厅在优雅的氛围中提供难以在别处找到的泰国菜肴，忠于泰国烹饪的根本，同时又加入了独到的创意。这里适合烛光晚餐或与欣赏美食的同事聚餐。对于特别能吃的人，还有大型套餐可供选择。
Gaggan
Earning first place on the latest "Asia's 50 best restaurants" list, progressive Indian restaurant Gaggan is one of the most exciting venues to arrive in Bangkok in recent years.

开头 Earning first place... 是分词短语，交代了背景（刚在榜单上夺冠）。主句核心：Gaggan is one of the most exciting venues to arrive in Bangkok in recent years（近年来曼谷最令人兴奋的餐厅之一）。

分词短语里的 latest 是关键词——最新一期榜单上的第一名，说明它现在正当红。

The best table in this two-story colonial Thai home offers a window right into the kitchen, where you can see chef Gaggan and his staff in action.

主句：The best table offers a window right into the kitchen。offers 在这里不是「提供」而是「给人一个…的机会/视角」——最好的桌子能让你直接看进厨房。

后面 where 引导定语从句，修饰 kitchen：在那个厨房里，你能看到 Gaggan 和员工在忙碌。这就是文中说的「美食剧场」——边吃边看厨师表演。

Culinary theater at its best.
凭借在最新一期"亚洲50家最佳餐厅"榜单上夺得首位，前卫印度餐厅 Gaggan 是近年来登陆曼谷最令人兴奋的场所之一。这栋两层殖民风格泰式老宅中，最佳餐桌正对厨房，你可以亲眼看到 Gaggan 大厨和员工们忙碌工作。美食剧场，尽显极致。

题目与解析（3题） ▼
21. What do Nahm and Issaya Siamese Club have in common?
A. They adopt modern cooking methods.
B. They have branches in London.
C. They have top-class chefs.
D. They are based in hotels.
答案：C
Nahm: "Head Chef David Thompson, who received a Michelin star..."
Issaya: "internationally known Thai chef Ian Kittichai's first flagship Bangkok restaurant."

解题思路：找两家餐厅的共同点。Nahm 的大厨 David Thompson 获得过米其林星；Issaya 是"国际知名泰国厨师 Ian Kittichai"的餐厅。两家的共同点都是有顶级大厨。

A. They adopt modern cooking methods ✗只有 Issaya 提到 modern cooking methods，Nahm 未提
B. They have branches in London ✗David Thompson 在伦敦有同名餐厅，但那不是 Nahm 的分店；Issaya 没提伦敦
C. They have top-class chefs ✓两家都有知名大厨，这是共同点
D. They are based in hotels ✗Nahm 在酒店里，但 Issaya 在 colonial house（老宅），不是酒店
22. Which restaurant offers a large set menu?
A. Gaggan.
B. Bo.lan.
C. Issaya Siamese Club.
D. Nahm.
答案：B
"For those extremely hungry there's a large set menu." — Bo.lan段落

解题思路：细节题，直接找原文中"large set menu"出现在哪家餐厅的介绍里。答案就在 Bo.lan 段落的最后一句。

A. Gaggan ✗Gaggan段落没有提到 set menu
B. Bo.lan ✓明确提到"there's a large set menu"
C. Issaya Siamese Club ✗Issaya 只提到 menu 包含传统和现代菜，没提 set menu
D. Nahm ✗Nahm 段落没有提到 set menu
23. What is special about Gaggan?
A. It hires staff from India.
B. It puts on a play every day.
C. It serves hard-to-find local dishes.
D. It shows the cooking process to guests.
答案：D
"The best table... offers a window right into the kitchen, where you can see chef Gaggan and his staff in action."

解题思路：Gaggan 最特别的地方：最好的桌子对着厨房，客人可以亲眼看到厨师工作（in action = 正在干活）。这就是"向客人展示烹饪过程"。

A. It hires staff from India ✗原文说的是印度菜，没说员工来自印度
B. It puts on a play every day ✗"Culinary theater"是比喻说法，不是真的表演戏剧
C. It serves hard-to-find local dishes ✗"hard-to-find Thai dishes"是 Bo.lan 的描述，不是 Gaggan；且 Gaggan 是印度菜不是当地菜
D. It shows the cooking process to guests ✓see chef...in action = 看到烹饪过程，完全对应

标记：

阅读技巧：广告/介绍类文章怎么读 ▼

A篇常考"广告/介绍类"文章——多个并列选项各自特点，题目考你能否精准找到细节。

策略：先看题目，再带着问题找对应段落。不要全文细读，锁定关键词（餐厅名、特征词），精准定位。

  1. 看题先于读文。题目会告诉你要找什么，带着目标读比漫无目的读快一倍。
  2. 段落结构即答题地图。每个餐厅一段，题目涉及哪家就只读那段。
  3. 警惕选项混淆。出题人会把A段的内容放到问B段的题的选项里——读完题选项后，一定回原文核实。
