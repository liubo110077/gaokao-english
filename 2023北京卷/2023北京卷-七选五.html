2023北京卷七选五 · College Rankings and Teaching Quality

Q35–39
七选五阅读理解
根据短文内容，从短文后的选项中选出能填入空白处的最佳选项。选项中有两项为多余选项。
It's a joyful and stressful time of year in the United States for students and their families as they make decisions about where to attend college. Families often turn to rankings systems to help make a decision. [35]D
When I talk to families as a scholar of higher education, they're often surprised that teaching excellence is not counted in rankings. [36]F
Emerging research suggests that courses in lower-ranked universities, on average, scored higher on teaching than courses in higher-ranked universities. [37]G The absence of teaching excellence from the rankings is surprising given the link between high-quality teaching and student success. Quality teaching is one of the most important predictors of a wide range of college outcomes.
Rankings, however, are only one reason why a low value is placed on teaching in higher education. Administrators often don't view teaching excellence as a way to increase enrolment (注册) or funding. [38]B Research shows that the more time instructors spend on teaching, the lower their salary. What is the result? Many instructors continue to teach using traditional lectures, which lead to lower success rates.
[39]C Nevertheless, not much will change until schools with high-quality teaching are rewarded with more resources, higher rankings and increased enrolments. In the long term, universities, organisations that rank schools, and others should work to make teaching a valued, core part of the mission. What should students and their families do? They should give strong consideration to universities where high-quality teaching is valued, even though the schools may be ranked lower.
选项 A–G（有两项多余）
A.Higher education has achieved its true potential.
B.Therefore, it's not highly valued in hiring or promotion.
C.Quality teaching has been an important reputation-building factor.
D.However, the rankings ignore a critical factor: the quality of teaching.
E.Efforts to improve teaching at the university level have recently emerged.
F.They're even more surprised at how teaching is undervalued by universities.
G.In fact, universities often shift emphasis from teaching to other ranking factors.
答案解析（35—39题）▼
35题 答案：D — However, the rankings ignore a critical factor: the quality of teaching.
上文：Families often turn to rankings systems to help make a decision.
逻辑：上文说家庭依靠排名系统做决策，D 句用 However 转折，直接引出全文核心矛盾——排名忽视了教学质量这个关键因素（critical factor: the quality of teaching）。这是全文的论点句。
排除 A/E：A 说"高等教育已实现真正潜力"与全文批评基调相反；E 说"教学改善努力最近出现"逻辑跳跃。
36题 答案：F — They're even more surprised at how teaching is undervalued by universities.
上文：they're often surprised that teaching excellence is not counted in rankings.
逻辑：上句说 families 对"教学未纳入排名"感到 surprised，F 句用 even more 递进，主语 they 指代上文 families，意思是"他们更惊讶的是大学自己也不重视教学"。逻辑连贯，代词呼应。
37题 答案：G — In fact, universities often shift emphasis from teaching to other ranking factors.
上文：courses in lower-ranked universities scored higher on teaching than higher-ranked ones.
逻辑：上句说低排名大学教学质量反而更高，G 句用 In fact 加强并解释原因——高排名大学把重心从教学转移到其他排名因素（shift emphasis from teaching）。这直接解释了教学质量与排名"倒挂"的原因。
38题 答案：B — Therefore, it's not highly valued in hiring or promotion.
上文：Administrators often don't view teaching excellence as a way to increase enrolment or funding.
逻辑：上句说管理者不把教学卓越视为增加入学率/资金的手段，B 句用 Therefore 因果，推出结论：教学在招聘和晋升中也不被重视。接下来"Research shows … lower salary"进一步验证这一结论，逻辑完整。
39题 答案：C — Quality teaching has been an important reputation-building factor.
下文：Nevertheless, not much will change until schools … are rewarded with more resources.
逻辑：C 句先肯定教学曾是重要声誉因素，然后下句用 Nevertheless 转折——尽管如此，不给予奖励就不会改变。C 为 Nevertheless 铺垫了让步语境（即使教学很重要，现实仍不会改变）。
排除 E：E 说"近年出现教学改善努力"与 Nevertheless"不会改变"矛盾。

七选五做题技巧

  • 看衔接词：However / In fact / Therefore / Even more 等是关键线索，直接锁定逻辑关系（转折/递进/因果）。
  • 找代词呼应：代词（they / it / this）必须指向上文某个具体名词；代词能对上，选项通常就对了。
  • 读前后句：空格填进去后，上下句必须"话题连贯"——前句话题收尾，所选句承接，下句继续展开。
  • 排除法最有效：先排除话题不符或逻辑矛盾的选项（A 结论与全文相反；E 与 Nevertheless 矛盾）。
重点词汇（10个） ▼
ranking 排名，名次 高频
rank v. 排名，ranking n. 排名/名次。college rankings = 大学排名系统，全文核心词。
rank among the top 10 = 跻身前十  ·  high-ranking 高排名的
excellence 卓越，优秀 考纲内
excellent → excellence（名词）。teaching excellence = 教学卓越，全文核心概念。
pursue excellence 追求卓越  ·  centre of excellence 卓越中心
enrolment 入学（人数），注册 考纲内
enrol v. 注册/入学，enrolment n. = 入学行为或入学人数。英式拼写（美式 enrollment）。
increase enrolment = 提高入学率  ·  enrolment numbers 入学人数
predictor 预测因素，预测指标 拓展
predict v. → predictor n. = 能预测结果的因素。
Quality teaching is one of the most important predictors of college outcomes. = 教学质量是大学成果最重要的预测因素之一
instructor 教师，讲师 考纲内
instructor = 教学人员（比 teacher 更正式，常指大学讲师或培训师）。
the more time instructors spend on teaching = 教师花在教学上的时间越多  ·  driving instructor 驾校教练
emphasis 重视，强调 考纲内
emphasise / emphasize v. → emphasis n.。
shift emphasis from teaching to other factors = 把重心从教学转移到其他因素  ·  place emphasis on = 重视...
critical 关键的；批判性的 高频
critical 有两个常见义：① 关键的，至关重要的：a critical factor 关键因素；② 批评的，挑剔的：a critical review 批评性评价。
这里是义①：the rankings ignore a critical factor = 排名忽视了一个关键因素
undervalue 低估，轻视 拓展
under-（不足）+ value（重视）= 给予的重视不够。
teaching is undervalued by universities = 大学低估了教学的价值  ·  对比：overvalue = 高估
reward 奖励，回报（动词/名词） 高频
reward v./n.。schools with high-quality teaching are rewarded with more resources = 教学质量好的学校获得更多资源作为奖励。
rewarding adj. = 有回报的，令人满足的（写作常用）
consideration 考虑，重视 考纲内
consider v. → consideration n.。give strong consideration to = 认真考虑，高度重视。
take sth into consideration = 把某事纳入考虑  ·  under consideration = 在考虑中
