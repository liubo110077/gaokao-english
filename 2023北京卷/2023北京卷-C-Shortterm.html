C
In recent years, researchers from diverse fields have agreed that short-termism is now a significant problem in industrialised societies. The inability to engage with longer-term causes and consequences leads to some of the world's most serious problems: climate change, biodiversity collapse, and more. The historian Francis Cole argues that the West has entered a period where "only the present exists, a present characterised at once by the cruelty of the instant and by the boredom of an unending now".

这句话的结构：主干是 Francis Cole argues that the West has entered a period（Francis Cole 认为西方已进入了一个时代）。

where 引导定语从句：where only the present exists——修饰 period（一个只有当下存在的时代）。

a present characterised at once by...and by...：这是对前面 the present 的进一步解释（同位语）。characterised at once by A and by B = 同时具有A和B两个特点：① the cruelty of the instant（瞬间的残酷），② the boredom of an unending now（永无止境的「当下」带来的无聊）。

引用的作用（28题）：作者引用 Cole 的话是为了说明短视主义问题有多严重——highlight a problem。

近年来，来自不同领域的研究人员一致认为，短视主义（只顾眼前不顾长远）已成为工业化社会的一个重要问题。对长远原因和后果的关注缺失，导致了世界上一些最严峻的问题：气候变化、生物多样性崩溃等等。历史学家Francis Cole认为，西方已经进入了一个"只有当下存在"的时代——这个当下同时笼罩着瞬间的残酷感和无休止"现在"的无聊感。

It has been proved that people have a bias towards the present, focusing on loud attractions in the moment at the expense of the health, well-being and financial stability of their future selves or community.

句子结构：主干是 people have a bias towards the present（人们倾向于重视眼前）。

focusing on...at the expense of...：这是一个分词短语，补充说明这种偏见的具体表现：专注于眼前的"大声"诱惑，代价是牺牲了未来自己和社会的健康、幸福与经济稳定。

loud attractions：loud 在这里不是"声音大"，而是"引人注目的、抓人眼球的"——那些当下最热门、最刺激的东西。

In business, this bias surfaces as short-sighted decisions. And on slow-burning problems like climate change, it translates into the unwillingness to make small sacrifices today that could make a major difference tomorrow. Instead, all that matters is next quarter's profit, or satisfying some other near-term desires.
事实证明，人们有一种偏向眼前的偏见，把注意力集中在当下最抓人眼球的事物上，代价是牺牲了自身和社会未来的健康、幸福与经济稳定。在商业上，这种偏见表现为目光短浅的决策。而在气候变化这类缓慢发展的问题上，它变成了不愿意为了明天可能发生的巨大改变而做出今天的小牺牲。人们只关心下个季度的利润，或者满足某些眼前的欲望。

These biased perspectives cannot be blamed on one single cause. It is fair to say, though, that our psychological biases play a major role. People's hesitancy to delay satisfaction is the most obvious example, but there are others. One of them is about how the most accessible information in the present affects decisions about the future. For instance, you might hear someone say: "It's cold this winter, so I needn't worry about global warming." Another is that loud and urgent matters are given too much importance, making people ignore longer-term trends that arguably matter more. This is when a pop star draws far more attention than, say, gradual biodiversity decline.
这些有偏差的看法不能归因于单一原因。但可以说，我们心理上的偏见起了很大的作用。人们不愿意延迟满足，这是最明显的例子，但还有其他原因。其中一种是：当下最容易看到的信息会影响对未来的判断。比如，你可能听到有人说："今年冬天这么冷，全球变暖我根本不需要担心。"另一种是，那些高调紧迫的事被给予了太多重视，让人忽视了可以说更重要的长期趋势——比如一个明星受到的关注，远超生物多样性的逐渐消失。
As a psychologist once joked, if aliens wanted to weaken humanity, they wouldn't send ships; they would invent climate change. Indeed, when it comes to environmental transformations, we can develop a form of collective "poor memory", and each new generation can believe the state of affairs they encounter is nothing out of the ordinary.

句子主干：we can develop a form of collective "poor memory"（我们会发展出一种集体健忘）and each new generation can believe the state of affairs … is nothing out of the ordinary（每一代人都以为现状很正常）。

when it comes to："当谈到/说到...的时候"，高考常见短语，表示"说到某个话题"。

the state of affairs they encounter：they encounter 是省略了 that 的定语从句，修饰 state of affairs。= the state of affairs that they encounter（他们遇到/看到的这种情况）。

short-termism 短视主义，只顾眼前 拓展
short-term（短期的）+ -ism（主义）。short-termism = 只关注短期利益、忽视长远后果的思维方式。
反义：long-term thinking 长远思维，far-sighted 有远见的
consequences 后果，结果 考纲内
consequence = 因某事导致的后果（偏负面）。高考必考词。
face the consequences 承担后果；as a consequence 因此
at the expense of 以...为代价，牺牲了 高频
expense = 代价，at the expense of sth = 以某事为代价。高考写作高频词组。

sight = 视力，short-sighted = 只能看到眼前，比喻"目光短浅的"。

make a difference = 起作用，带来改变。make a major difference = 带来很大的改变。

perspective = 看问题的角度和方式。高考高频词，单数 perspective 也很常见。

gradual = 一点一点慢慢发生的，不是突然的。副词：gradually 逐渐地。

题目解析（3题，28—30） ▼
28. The author quotes Francis Cole mainly to ______.
A. draw a comparison
B. introduce a topic
C. evaluate a statement
D. highlight a problem
答案：D
"The historian Francis Cole argues that the West has entered a period where 'only the present exists...'"

Cole 的话描述了短视主义有多严重——西方社会进入了一个"只有当下"的时代。作者引用这个话是为了强调/突出（highlight）短视主义这个问题的严重性。选 D。

✗ A— draw a comparison（进行比较）：引用没有在比较两个东西，而是在描述一种现象的严重性。
✗ B— introduce a topic（引入话题）：话题在第一句已经引入了（short-termism is a problem），Cole 是在进一步强调，不是引入新话题。
✗ C— evaluate a statement（评价一个观点）：作者没有在评价 Cole 说的对不对，而是用它来说明问题。
✓ D— highlight a problem（突出一个问题）：用名人的话来说明短视主义问题有多严重，就是在"突出这个问题"。
29. What can be inferred from the last paragraph?
A. Climate change has been forgotten.
B. Lessons of history are highly valued.
C. The human mind is bad at noting slow change.
D. Humans are unwilling to admit their shortcomings.
答案：C
"...each new generation can believe the state of affairs they encounter is nothing out of the ordinary."
"Children...have no idea that insect population has dropped dramatically."

最后一段讲：每一代人都以为现状是正常的（集体健忘）。老人记得车窗上爬满虫子，小孩不知道虫子减少了。这说明：昆虫数量是缓慢（gradually）下降的，人类大脑注意不到这种慢变化。可以推断出 C（人类大脑不擅长注意到缓慢的变化）。

✗ A— Climate change has been forgotten：文章没说气候变化被忘掉了，而是说人们不重视它。
✗ B— Lessons of history are highly valued：恰好相反，文章说每一代人都以为现状正常，不会从历史中学习。
✓ C— The human mind is bad at noting slow change：虫子例子直接说明这一点——变化太慢，人们察觉不到。
✗ D— Humans are unwilling to admit their shortcomings：文章没提"承认缺点"，讲的是"察觉不到慢变化"。
30. What does the author intend to tell us?
A. Far-sighted thinking matters to humans.
B. Humans tend to make long-term sacrifices.

答案：A

整篇文章论证的是短视主义（short-termism）的危害——人们太关注当下，导致气候变化、生物多样性等长远问题被忽视。文章想告诉我们：我们需要有远见的思维（far-sighted thinking）。A 正确。

✓ A— Far-sighted thinking matters：short-termism 的反面就是 far-sighted thinking，全文都在论证为什么需要长远眼光。
✗ B— Humans tend to make long-term sacrifices：恰好相反，文章说的是人们不愿意做短期牺牲（unwillingness to make small sacrifices）。

阅读原文▼

In recent years, researchers from diverse fields have agreed that short-termism is now a significant problem in industrialised societies. The inability to engage with longer-term causes and consequences leads to some of the world's most serious problems: climate change, biodiversity collapse, and more. The historian Francis Cole argues that the West has entered a period where "only the present exists, a present characterised at once by the cruelty of the instant and by the boredom of an unending now".

It has been proved that people have a bias towards the present, focusing on loud attractions in the moment at the expense of the health, well-being and financial stability of their future selves or community. In business, this bias surfaces as short-sighted decisions. And on slow-burning problems like climate change, it translates into the unwillingness to make small sacrifices today that could make a major difference tomorrow. Instead, all that matters is next quarter's profit, or satisfying some other near-term desires.

These biased perspectives cannot be blamed on one single cause. It is fair to say, though, that our psychological biases play a major role. People's hesitancy to delay satisfaction is the most obvious example, but there are others. One of them is about how the most accessible information in the present affects decisions about the future. For instance, you might hear someone say: "It's cold this winter, so I needn't worry about global warming." Another is that loud and urgent matters are given too much importance, making people ignore longer-term trends that arguably matter more. This is when a pop star draws far more attention than, say, gradual biodiversity decline.

As a psychologist once joked, if aliens wanted to weaken humanity, they wouldn't send ships; they would invent climate change. Indeed, when it comes to environmental transformations, we can develop a form of collective "poor memory", and each new generation can believe the state of affairs they encounter is nothing out of the ordinary. Older people today, for example, can remember a time with insect-covered car windscreens after long drives. Children, on the other hand, have no idea that insect population has dropped dramatically.

阅读技巧▼

议论文三步读法：

  • 第一步：找论点——第一段最后一句或第一句常是论点。本文论点：short-termism is a significant problem。
  • 第二步：找论据——后面每一段举例说明。本文：商业决策短视、气候变化、心理偏见、集体健忘（昆虫例子）。
  • 第三步：推断作者意图——最后问"作者想告诉我们什么"，就是论点的反面：我们需要 far-sighted thinking（长远眼光）。

引用名人话的目的：高考常考"作者引用XX主要是为了___"。常见答案：highlight a problem（突出问题）/ introduce a topic（引入话题）/ support an argument（支持论点）。判断方法：看引用出现在哪里——第一段中间位置通常是 highlight，不是 introduce。

作文拓展：好句式 & 好短语▼

可以直接用进作文的句式：

  • at the expense of one's health / future（以健康/未来为代价）
  • make a major difference to...（对...带来很大改变）
  • translate into（转变为...）
  • 短语：short-sighted vs far-sighted，dramatically，gradual，at the expense of
