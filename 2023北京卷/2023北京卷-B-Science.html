B
Sitting in the garden for my friend's birthday, I felt a buzz in my pocket. My heart raced when I saw the email sender's name. The email started off: "Dear Mr Green, thank you for your interest" and "the review process took longer than expected." It ended with "We are sorry to inform you…" and my vision blurred. The position—measuring soil quality in the Sahara Desert as part of an undergraduate research programme—had felt like the answer I had spent years looking for.

破折号的作用：两个破折号（—…—）夹住的部分是插入说明，相当于括号。measuring soil quality in the Sahara Desert as part of an undergraduate research programme 是在解释 the position 是什么职位——跳过这部分，主干是：The position had felt like the answer I had spent years looking for.（那个职位曾感觉就是我花了多年寻找的答案。）

过去完成时 had felt / had spent：两个 had + 过去分词，表示在过去某个时间点（收到拒信）之前，作者就已经认为这是梦寐以求的机会了。

the answer I had spent years looking for：这是一个省略了 that 的定语从句，修饰 the answer。= the answer that I had spent years looking for。

我坐在花园里，正在参加朋友的生日派对，口袋里突然传来一阵振动。看到发件人名字时，我的心跳加速。邮件开头写道："亲爱的Green先生，感谢您的申请，审核比预期花了更长时间。"结尾是"我们很遗憾地通知您……"，我的视线模糊了。那个职位——作为本科研究项目的一部分，在撒哈拉沙漠测量土壤质量——曾是我花了多年在寻找的答案。

💡 两个破折号（—…—）等于括号，夹住的是补充说明，直接先跳过，读完主句再回来看。
I had put so much time and emotional energy into applying, and I thought the rejection meant the end of the road for my science career.

So I was shocked when, not long after the email, Professor Mary Devon, who was running the programme, invited me to observe the work being done in her lab.

句子主干：I was shocked when … Professor Mary Devon … invited me to observe the work.（作者很震惊，因为Devon邀请他参观实验室工作。）

两处插入：① not long after the email（收到邮件后不久）——时间插入，夹在 when 和主语之间，可以先跳过。② who was running the programme（她负责这个项目）——非限制性定语从句，补充说明Devon是谁，逗号夹住，可以先跳过。

the work being done：being done 是现在分词被动式，修饰 work。= the work that was being done in her lab（实验室里正在进行的工作）。

我感到十分震惊——就在收到拒信后不久，项目负责人Professor Mary Devon竟然邀请我去她的实验室参观工作。我立刻答应了。几周后，当她邀请我和她谈谈我可以在实验室里从事的潜在项目时，我再次感到震惊，同时又欣喜万分。她提出的方向似乎不如我最初申请的项目那么令人兴奋，但我打算全力以赴。

I found myself working with a robotics professor on techniques for collecting data from the desert remotely. That project, which I could complete from my sofa instead of in the burning heat of the desert, not only survived the lockdown but worked where traditional methods didn't.

句子主干：That project not only survived the lockdown but worked where traditional methods didn't.（那个项目不仅熬过了封控，还在传统方法失败的地方成功了。）

which 从句：which I could complete from my sofa instead of in the burning heat of the desert——非限制性定语从句，补充说明：这个项目我可以坐在沙发上完成，不用去沙漠。逗号夹住，先跳过。

When I applied to graduate school, I found three programmes promising to allow me to follow my desired research direction. And I applied with the same anxious excitement as before. When I was rejected from one that had seemed like a perfect fit, it was undoubtedly difficult. But this time I had the perspective to keep it from sending me into panic. 申请研究生院时，我找到了三个有望让我沿着心仪方向深造的项目。我带着和以前一样的既紧张又兴奋的心情提交了申请。当我被一个看起来完美契合的项目拒绝时，确实很难受。但这次我有了足够的心态，不再让这件事让我陷入恐慌。最终，我被另一个同样让我兴奋的项目录取了——这也是一种安慰。
Rather than setting plans in stone, I've learned that sometimes I need to take the opportunities that are offered, even if they don't sound perfect at the time, and make the most of them.

句子主干：I've learned that I need to take the opportunities and make the most of them.（我明白了需要抓住机会并充分利用它们。）

Rather than：开头的 Rather than setting plans in stone 是对比，意思是"不再把计划定死了"——用来引出作者的改变。

两个动词并列：take the opportunities … and make the most of them——and 连接的是 take 和 make，都属于 I need to 下面的动词。

even if：即使（they don't sound perfect at the time）——even if 引导让步状语，意思是"就算当时听起来不完美"也要去抓住。

我明白了，与其把计划定死，有时候我需要抓住眼前给出的机会，哪怕当时听起来并不完美，然后充分利用它。

生词与短语（10个） ▼
heart raced 心跳加速（表紧张） 高频
race = 快速跑动，heart raced = 心快速跳动 = 心跳加速，常用来表达紧张或激动。
My heart raced when I saw the email sender's name. （课文）
近义：My heart beat fast. / My heart was pounding.
rejection 拒绝；拒信 考纲内
动词 reject（拒绝）→ 名词 rejection（被拒绝 / 一封拒信）。高考常考词形变化。
the rejection meant the end of the road for my science career. （课文）
面对拒绝：face rejection；收到拒信：receive a rejection
end of the road 走投无路，走到头了 高频
road = 路，end of the road = 路的尽头。习语，表示"结束了、没有办法了"。
the end of the road for my science career （课文）
jumped at the chance 立刻抓住机会 高频
jump at = 跳向，jump at a chance = 立刻抓住机会，毫不犹豫答应。
I jumped at the chance. （课文）
= grab the chance. 也可说：jump at the opportunity.

pursue = 追，努力去得到某样东西。可以接职业、梦想、目标。

pursue a career 从事某职业；pursue a goal 追求目标；pursue one's dream 追梦
give it my all 全力以赴 高频
all = 全部，give it my all = 把我全部都给进去 = 全力以赴，不留余力。
I was going to give it my all. （课文）
= do my best. 写作：give it your all 全力以赴。
undoubtedly 毫无疑问地 考纲内
doubt = 怀疑，un- = 没有，undoubtedly = 没有任何怀疑地 = 毫无疑问地。

rather than = 比起...宁愿，表示取舍和对比。Rather than A, B = B 而不是 A。
Rather than setting plans in stone, I've learned to take opportunities. （课文）

most = 最多，make the most of sth = 把某样东西用到最大程度 = 充分利用。

24. How did the author feel upon seeing the email sender's name?
A. Anxious.
B. Angry.
C. Surprised.
D. Settled.
答案：A
"My heart raced when I saw the email sender's name."

题目问"作者看到发件人名字时什么感受"。原文说 My heart raced（心跳加速），心跳加速是紧张/焦虑（anxious）的典型身体反应。选 A。

✓ A— heart raced = 心跳加速，这是 anxious（焦虑/紧张）的直接体现。
✗ B— Angry（愤怒）：文中没有愤怒的信号，拒信让作者伤心/难受，不是愤怒。
✗ C— Surprised（惊讶）：看到发件人名字时是紧张，不是惊讶；后来看到Devon的邀请才是 shocked（惊讶）。
✗ D— Settled（平静的）：完全相反，心跳加速说明根本不平静。
25. After talking with Professor Devon, the author decided to ______.
A. criticise the review process
B. stay longer in the Sahara Desert
C. apply to the original project again
D. put his heart and soul into the lab work
答案：D
"What she proposed didn't seem as exciting as the original project I had applied to, but I was going to give it my all."

虽然Devon提出的方向没有原来那么令人兴奋，但作者说 give it my all（全力以赴）。give it my all = put his heart and soul into it = D。

✗ A— 文中没有提到批评审核过程。
✗ B— 作者根本没去沙漠，项目是远程做的。
✗ C— 文中没提要重新申请原来的项目，恰恰相反，他接受了新方向。
✓ D— give it my all = put his heart and soul into it = 全力以赴地做实验室工作。
26. According to the author, the project with the robotics professor was ______.
A. demanding
B. inspiring
C. misleading
D. amusing
答案：B
"In the end, I had a new scientific interest to pursue."

机器人学项目让作者最终找到了"a new scientific interest to pursue"（一个新的科研兴趣去追寻）。这个项目给了他新的方向和动力，这就是 inspiring（激励人的、令人振奋的）。选 B。

✗ A— demanding（艰难费力的）：文中没有说这个项目特别难做，重点是它成功了。
✓ B— inspiring（激励的）：项目让作者找到了新的科研兴趣和方向，正是受到了启发。
✗ C— misleading（误导的）：项目是成功的，根本没有误导。
✗ D— amusing（好玩的）：重点是科研成果和新兴趣，不是好玩。
27. What can we learn from this passage?
A. An invitation is a reputation.
B. An innovation is a resolution.
C. A rejection can be a redirection.
D. A reflection can be a restriction.
答案：C
"Rather than setting plans in stone, I've learned that sometimes I need to take the opportunities that are offered, even if they don't sound perfect at the time, and make the most of them."

整篇文章讲的是：被拒绝 → 意外转机 → 更好的结果。作者从拒信中得到了新机会和新方向。C（A rejection can be a redirection，被拒可以是一次转向）完美概括全文主旨。这四个选项都用了押韵的写法（-tion），只有 C 和文章内容相符。

✗ A— invitation / reputation：文章讲的不是"邀请等于名声"。
✗ B— innovation / resolution：文章没有讲创新与决心的关系。
✓ C— rejection（被拒）可以是 redirection（新方向）：这就是整篇文章的核心教训。
✗ D— reflection / restriction：文章没有说反思会带来限制。
阅读原文▼

Sitting in the garden for my friend's birthday, I felt a buzz in my pocket. My heart raced when I saw the email sender's name. The email started off: "Dear Mr Green, thank you for your interest" and "the review process took longer than expected." It ended with "We are sorry to inform you…" and my vision blurred. The position—measuring soil quality in the Sahara Desert as part of an undergraduate research programme—had felt like the answer I had spent years looking for.

I had put so much time and emotional energy into applying, and I thought the rejection meant the end of the road for my science career.

So I was shocked when, not long after the email, Professor Mary Devon, who was running the programme, invited me to observe the work being done in her lab. I jumped at the chance, and a few weeks later I was equally shocked—and overjoyed—when she invited me to talk with her about potential projects I could pursue in her lab. What she proposed didn't seem as exciting as the original project I had applied to, but I was going to give it my all.

I found myself working with a robotics professor on techniques for collecting data from the desert remotely. That project, which I could complete from my sofa instead of in the burning heat of the desert, not only survived the lockdown but worked where traditional methods didn't. In the end, I had a new scientific interest to pursue.

When I applied to graduate school, I found three programmes promising to allow me to follow my desired research direction. And I applied with the same anxious excitement as before. When I was rejected from one that had seemed like a perfect fit, it was undoubtedly difficult. But this time I had the perspective to keep it from sending me into panic. It helped that in the end I was accepted into one of the other programmes I was also excited about.

Rather than setting plans in stone, I've learned that sometimes I need to take the opportunities that are offered, even if they don't sound perfect at the time, and make the most of them.

阅读技巧▼

记叙文阅读策略：这类文章考"情感变化"和"道理"。读时注意：

  • 情感词（shocked, overjoyed, anxious, difficult）→ 直接对应情感类题目
  • 转折词（but, however, yet）→ 后面往往是重点，故事在这里转向
  • 最后一段 → 通常是"作者从中得到的道理"，主旨题答案在这里

本文故事线：收到拒信（低谷）→ 意外邀请（转机）→ 新项目成功（收获）→ 研究生申请再次被拒→ 最终录取 → 结论：被拒可以是新方向。

作文拓展：好句式 & 好短语▼

可以直接用进作文的句子和短语：

  • Rather than setting plans in stone, I've learned to make the most of the opportunities offered.（不把计划定死，充分利用眼前的机会）
  • I was going to give it my all.（我打算全力以赴）
  • A rejection can be a redirection.（被拒可以是一次新方向）
  • 短语：jump at the chance，make the most of，pursue a goal，rather than
