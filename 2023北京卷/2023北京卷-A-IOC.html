A
The International Olympic Committee (IOC) Young Leaders programme empowers talents to make a positive difference in their communities through sport. Twenty-five Young Leaders are being selected every two years for a four-year period. They promote the Olympic values, spreading the message of sport for good.
国际奥林匹克委员会（IOC）青年领袖项目赋予有才能的年轻人，让他们能够通过体育在自己的社区中产生积极影响。每两年选拔25位青年领袖，服务四年。他们推广奥林匹克价值观，传播体育向善的理念。
To be an IOC Young Leader, you need to first complete the 4-Week Learning Sprint.
要成为IOC青年领袖，你首先需要完成"四周学习冲刺"项目。
4-Week Learning Sprint
The 4-Week Learning Sprint, which will take place during November 2023, is a virtual learning programme.

句子结构：主干是 The 4-Week Learning Sprint is a virtual learning programme（四周学习冲刺是一个线上学习项目）。

which 非限制性定语从句：which will take place during November 2023 是对主语的补充说明（2023年11月举行）。逗号 + which = 非限制性，不影响主句理解，可以先跳过。

与21题的关系：21题问参加Sprint会做什么 → 答案 B（attend a virtual learning programme）就来自这句话。

The sessions can be attended live or watched back after they are made available on the IOC channel. Each week, participants will be asked to complete a topic-specific reflection task.
四周学习冲刺将于2023年11月举行，是一个线上学习项目。课程可以实时参加，或在IOC频道发布后观看回放。每周，参与者将被要求完成一项针对特定主题的反思任务。

生词与短语（10个） ▼
empower 赋予能力，使有能力做某事 考纲内
em-（使…）+ power（力量/能力）= 赋予某人能力或权力。高考写作高频词。
The programme empowers talents to make a positive difference. （课文）
作文：Education empowers us to achieve our dreams. 教育赋予我们实现梦想的能力。
make a positive difference 产生积极影响，带来正面改变 高频
make a difference = 起作用，产生影响。make a positive difference = 带来积极的改变。高考作文高频表达。
empowers talents to make a positive difference in their communities. （课文）
作文：Volunteering can make a positive difference to society. 志愿服务能为社会带来积极影响。
promote 推广，宣传（也有"晋升"之意） 考纲内
pro-（向前）+ mote（移动）= 推进，推广。promote values = 传播/推广价值观。也可以是"晋升"，看语境。
They promote the Olympic values. （课文）
promote cooperation 促进合作；get promoted 被晋升
virtual 虚拟的，线上的 考纲内
virtual = 通过网络/电脑模拟的，非现实的。virtual learning = 线上学习。virtual reality（VR）= 虚拟现实。
is a virtual learning programme. （课文）
a virtual meeting 线上会议；virtual reality 虚拟现实
reflection 反思，思考 考纲内
reflect = 反射；也有"深思"之意。reflection = 反思，对自己行为或学习的思考总结。
complete a topic-specific reflection task. （课文）
self-reflection 自我反思；on reflection 经过深思
submit 提交（申请/文件/计划） 考纲内
sub-（在下面/向下）+ mit（发送）= 把文件"递交"上去。比 hand in 更正式。
you will need to submit a plan for a sport-based project. （课文）
submit an application 提交申请；submit a report 提交报告
self-motivated 有自我驱动力的，自律的 拓展
self-（自己）+ motivated（有动力的）= 不需要别人推动，自己有内在驱动力。
You are self-motivated and committed. （课文）
作文：Being self-motivated is key to success. 自我驱动是成功的关键。
passionate about 对...充满热情 高频
passion（激情）→ passionate（充满激情的）→ passionate about sth（对某事热情高涨）。高考写作必背。
You are passionate about creating positive change. （课文）
作文：I am passionate about environmental protection. 我热爱环境保护事业。
committed 有承诺的，坚定投入的 考纲内
commit（承诺/全力投入）→ committed（切实承诺并投入的）。committed to a goal = 坚定地为目标努力。
You are self-motivated and committed. （课文）
committed to improving 致力于改进；a committed teacher 一位尽职的老师
backgrounds 背景，经历（来自不同背景） 考纲内
background = 背景（成长环境、教育、文化）。from different backgrounds = 来自不同背景的人，可以是不同国家、文化、职业等。
work with people from different backgrounds. （课文）
diverse backgrounds 多元背景；cultural background 文化背景
题目解析（3题，21—23） ▼
21. In the 4-Week Learning Sprint, participants will ______.
A. create change in their community
B. attend a virtual learning programme
C. meet people from different backgrounds
D. promote the IOC Young Leaders project
答案：B
"The 4-Week Learning Sprint...is a virtual learning programme."
"The sessions can be attended live or watched back..."

细节题，直接定位到"4-Week Learning Sprint"部分。第一句说这是一个 virtual learning programme（线上学习项目），第二句说可以 attended live（实时参加）。选 B（attend a virtual learning programme = 参加线上学习项目）。

✗ A— create change in their community：这是整个Young Leaders项目的目标（P1：make a positive difference in their communities），不是Sprint期间做的事。
✓ B— attend a virtual learning programme：与课文"is a virtual learning programme / can be attended live"直接对应。
✗ C— meet people from different backgrounds：这是申请人的要求（able to work with people from different backgrounds），不是Sprint期间的活动。
✗ D— promote the IOC Young Leaders project：Sprint是申请的前置步骤，不是用来推广Young Leaders项目的。
22. If selected as an IOC Young Leader, one will need to ______.
A. complete a reflection task each week
B. watch sports on the IOC channel
C. work on a sport-based project
D. coach and advise their peers
答案：C
"...submit a plan for a sport-based project, which you will work on if selected as an IOC Young Leader."

细节题，关键词 "if selected as an IOC Young Leader"。课文第8句明确说：提交一份体育项目计划，如果被选为Young Leader，你将"work on"（实施）它。选 C（work on a sport-based project）。

✗ A— complete a reflection task each week：这是Sprint期间每周要做的，不是选上后的任务。时间段不同。
✗ B— watch sports on the IOC channel：Sprint可以在IOC频道观看回放，但这不是Young Leader的职责。
✓ C— work on a sport-based project：直接来自课文"which you will work on if selected as an IOC Young Leader"。
✗ D— coach and advise their peers：申请人要求是"open to being coached and advised by experts and peers"，是接受辅导，不是去辅导别人。
23. Which is a requirement for the applicants?
A. Spreading the message of sport for good.
B. Having at least one-year work experience.
C. Showing great passion for project planning.
D. Committing themselves to becoming an expert.
答案：B
"You have at least one year of work experience."

细节题，直接对照"Requirements for the Applicants"列表。选项 B 和课文完全对应："You have at least one year of work experience" = "Having at least one-year work experience"。选 B。

✗ A— Spreading the message of sport for good：这是已入选的Young Leaders的职责（P1："spreading the message of sport for good"），不是申请人要求。
✓ B— Having at least one-year work experience：与课文要求列表第三条完全对应。
✗ C— Showing great passion for project planning：课文要求是"passionate about creating positive change in your community"（热爱在社区创造改变），不是"project planning（项目规划）"，偷换了概念。
✗ D— Committing themselves to becoming an expert：课文要求是"open to being coached and advised by experts"（乐于接受专家指导），不是自己要"成为专家"。
阅读原文▼

The International Olympic Committee (IOC) Young Leaders programme empowers talents to make a positive difference in their communities through sport. Twenty-five Young Leaders are being selected every two years for a four-year period. They promote the Olympic values, spreading the message of sport for good.

To be an IOC Young Leader, you need to first complete the 4-Week Learning Sprint.

4-Week Learning Sprint

The 4-Week Learning Sprint, which will take place during November 2023, is a virtual learning programme. The sessions can be attended live or watched back after they are made available on the IOC channel. Each week, participants will be asked to complete a topic-specific reflection task.

The 4-Week Learning Sprint is open to anyone, with the target audience aged between 20 and 28.

After successfully completing the 4-Week Learning Sprint, you will need to submit a plan for a sport-based project, which you will work on if selected as an IOC Young Leader.

Requirements for the Applicants

  • You have successfully completed the 4-Week Learning Sprint.
  • You have completed your high school studies.
  • You have at least one year of work experience.
  • You have strong public speaking skills.
  • You are self-motivated and committed.
  • You are passionate about creating positive change in your community.
  • You are open to being coached and advised by experts and peers.
  • You are able to work with people from different backgrounds.
阅读技巧▼

告示/通知类文章读法（A篇是此类文章的典型）：

  • 第一步：看标题和分段标题。本文有两个小标题："4-Week Learning Sprint"和"Requirements for the Applicants"。先扫描这些标题，知道文章分几部分、每部分讲什么。
  • 第二步：看题目，带着问题读。21题问"Sprint期间做什么"，直接定位到Sprint部分；22题问"选上后做什么"；23题问"申请要求"。
  • 第三步：细节题要找原文依据，不能凭感觉。答案一定能在文中找到对应的原句，不然就是干扰项。

常见陷阱（本篇三道题都有）：

  • 时间段混淆：Sprint期间 ≠ 选上后，要注意区分不同阶段各自的要求。
  • 偷换概念：C（project planning）vs 课文（creating positive change in community）——看起来相似，但意思不同。
  • 主体混淆：D（committing to becoming an expert）vs 课文（open to being coached by experts）——变成了相反的方向。
作文拓展：好句式 & 好短语▼

可以直接用进作文的高频表达：

  • empower sb to do sth（赋予某人做某事的能力）—— Technology empowers us to learn more efficiently.
  • make a positive difference to / in...（产生积极影响）—— Reading widely can make a positive difference to your writing.
  • passionate about（对...充满热情）—— I am passionate about helping others.
  • committed to（致力于...）—— We should be committed to protecting the environment.
  • promote（推广）—— We should promote healthy lifestyles among young people.

告示文体写作结构（如果考作文也是通知类）：

  • 开头：介绍项目目的（empower / aim to / be designed to）
  • 中间：分条说明要求/流程（Requirements: / Steps: / You should...）
  • 结尾：欢迎参加（We welcome all applicants who... / We look forward to...）
