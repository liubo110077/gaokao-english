2023北京卷完形填空 · The Shy Essay Writer

Q1–10
完形填空（共10题，每题1.5分）
阅读下面短文，从短文后各题所给的四个选项（A、B、C和D）中，选出可以填入空白处的最佳选项。
I was always timid (羞怯的). Being new to the school made me even (1)shyer, so it was surprising I'd (2)chat to anyone around me. Now I was paying the price — to write a five-page essay on "Why I Should Not Talk in Class". That would take all night!
After I got home, though, I took my time petting the cat — postponing the pain.
When I finally sat down to (3)write, I began with the reasons Ms Black would want to hear.
Talking kept me and my neighbours from (4)learning. One paragraph down; now what? I chewed on my pencil. Aha! What if talking were the first step towards life as a criminal? Without the education I was throwing away, I'd turn to theft and go to prison. When I got out, people would say, "She used to talk in class." The pages began (5)flying by.
But when mum got home from work, I was still (6)complaining, "Five pages! That's impossible!"
"Well, you'd better get back to work," she said. "and I want to read it when you're through."
Soon after dinner, I handed the essay to mum. I half expected a (7)lecture — at least an "I hope you've learned your lesson". (8)Instead, mum laughed and laughed as she read.
The next day, when Ms Black read the essay to the class, everyone laughed. I could (9)tell they weren't making fun of me: they laughed because I had the power to tell a funny story. My (10)confidence still needed some nudging (激励), but I did learn I wasn't shy in print.
答案解析（1—10题）▼
1. B — shyer （比较级）
I was always timid（胆小的），转学让"我"更胆小。shyer = shy 的比较级，与 timid 语义呼应。freer（更自由）/ calmer（更平静）/ busier（更忙）均与语境相反。
2. D — chat （聊天）
so it was surprising I'd chat to anyone——"我"这么胆小，居然会和别人聊天，所以才"令人惊讶"。后文"Why I Should Not Talk in Class"也呼应了爱聊天这一点。nod（点头）/ argue（争吵）/ smile（微笑）均与"令人惊讶"的程度不符。
3. C — write （写作）
任务是写一篇5页的作文，所以"我"终于坐下来 write（写）。上文"write a five-page essay"已明确任务，直接呼应。
4. A — learning （学习）
Talking kept me and my neighbours from learning（上课说话妨碍了学习）。这是"我"给老师的第一个理由——说话影响学习。与"Ms Black would want to hear"（老师想听到的理由）呼应。
5. B — flying by （飞逝）
The pages began flying by（页数飞速增加）——"我"越写越有创意，时间/页数都在快速过去。fly by = 时间/事物快速流逝。breaking up（断裂）/ starting over（重新开始）/ cutting in（插嘴）均不符合语境。
6. C — complaining （抱怨）
mum 回家时"我"还在 complaining（抱怨）"五页！不可能！"——后面的引号里就是抱怨的内容，所以 complaining 完全准确。wondering（疑惑）/ dreaming（做梦）/ requesting（请求）均不符。
7. A — lecture （训诫）
I half expected a lecture（训诫/说教）——"我"以为妈妈会说教"我"。lecture 在这里是名词，意为"严厉的责备"。after all 下文"I hope you've learned your lesson"就是典型的说教语气。praise（表扬）明显相反；reminder（提醒）/ mistake（错误）均不符合"预期妈妈会说教"的语境。
8. D — Instead （反而，相反地）
I half expected a lecture. Instead, mum laughed——"我"以为会被训诫，反而妈妈笑了。Instead 表示"与预期相反"，是完形填空中极高频的转折连接词。Besides（此外）/ Otherwise（否则）/ Therefore（因此）均不能表达"与预期相反"。
9. C — tell （辨别，判断）
I could tell they weren't making fun of me——"我"能判断出他们不是在嘲笑我。can tell = 能辨别，能判断。这是高考常考的 tell 用法（非"告诉"）。can guess（猜测）/ can see（看见）/ can decide（决定）均不如 tell 准确。
10. B — confidence （信心）
My confidence still needed some nudging（我的信心还需要一点激励）——"我"学会了写作上不害羞，但信心还不足。全文主题是从羞怯到发现自己能力的成长故事，confidence（信心）是贯穿始终的核心词。curiosity（好奇心）/ responsibility（责任感）/ patience（耐心）均与文章主题不符。
重点词汇（10个）▼
timid 胆小的，怯懦的 课文
timid = 因害怕或缺乏自信而胆小。比 shy 更强调"胆怯、不敢"的感觉。
She was always timid around strangers. 她在陌生人面前总是很胆小。
postpone 推迟，拖延 考纲内
postpone = 把某事推迟到以后做。post- = 在…之后，-pone = 放置。
I took my time petting the cat — postponing the pain. （课文）
The meeting was postponed due to bad weather. 会议因天气恶劣而推迟。
turn to 转向，走上（某条路） 考纲内
turn to sth = 转而做某事（尤指不好的事）。
I'd turn to theft and go to prison. （课文）
When stressed, some people turn to food for comfort. 有些人压力大时转而靠食物寻求安慰。
fly by （时间）飞速流逝 高频
fly by = 时间或事物快速流逝/过去。The pages began flying by = 页数飞速增加。
The weekend just flew by. 周末转眼就过去了。
lecture （名词）训诫，严肃责备 考纲内
lecture 除了"讲座/授课"，还可以作名词表示"说教、训诫"，或动词 lecture sb = 训诫某人。
I half expected a lecture. （课文）
My parents always lecture me about my grades. 父母总是训诫我的成绩。
Instead 反而，相反地（句首） 考纲内
Instead 放在句首，表示"与前面所说的相反，实际上却..."。完形填空中极高频！
Instead, mum laughed and laughed. （课文）
I expected to fail. Instead, I got top marks. 我以为会不及格，反而得了最高分。
tell（判断义） 辨别，判断，分辨 考纲内
can tell = 能判断/辨别出。这是 tell 的一个重要用法，不是"告诉"，而是"能看出来"。高考必考！
I could tell they weren't making fun of me. （课文）
Can you tell the difference between the two? 你能分辨出这两者的区别吗？
make fun of 取笑，嘲弄 考纲内
make fun of sb = 取笑某人，嘲弄某人（有时带贬义）。
they weren't making fun of me. （课文）
Don't make fun of others' mistakes. 不要嘲笑别人的错误。
confidence 信心，自信 考纲内
confidence = 相信自己能力的感觉（自信）。confident = 形容词。全文主题：从羞怯到找到自信。
My confidence still needed some nudging. （课文）
She spoke with great confidence. 她说话非常自信。
nudge 激励，轻轻推动 课文
nudge = 用肘轻推；比喻"轻轻激励、推动"。nudging = 激励（题目括号里已给出）。
My confidence still needed some nudging. （课文）
