2026北京卷完形填空 · Jealousy Between Friends

Q1–10
完形填空（共10题，每题1.5分）
阅读下面短文，从短文后各题所给的四个选项（A、B、C和D）中，选出可以填入空白处的最佳选项。
Ivy and Mia were best friends. But as they grew older, there was a shift.
Mia shone easily in after-school activities. She was a talented singer and won the (1)admiration of all. The teachers praised her, and friends gathered around her, requesting her to sing. Ivy felt (2)left out. Mia, however, didn't know it and went her way as usual.
Ivy recognised a(n) (3)unsettled feeling when Mia was applauded, and an ache in the heart when she compared herself with Mia. Jealousy (嫉妒) grew silently in her heart, like a shadow.
One day, Mia came to Ivy and told her excitedly, "I've been selected for the inter-school competition!" Ivy (4)forced a smile. "That's great" was all she said. That evening, she sat in her room, staring into space. Why her? Why not me? Such thoughts filled her mind.
She started (5)avoiding Mia. Their conversations became short. Having noticed this, Mia asked Ivy, "Did I hurt you? You seem to be (6)distant." Ivy hesitated, and then admitted how she felt. Mia was surprised but smiled gently. "Do you know?" she said, "I've always admired you. I might be able to get attention (7)effortlessly, but you have the strength for steady growth. We're not competing. We're just different."
Over time, the (8)shadow faded (消退). Ivy finally realised: Jealousy doesn't come from the success of others—it comes when we forget our own (9)worth. And once that takes root, there's no need to (10)compare.
答案解析（1—10题）▼
1. B — admiration （钦佩，赞赏）
赢得所有人的钦佩/赞赏。admiration = 钦佩，与前文"talented singer"和"teachers praised her"呼应。credibility（可信度）/ sympathy（同情）/ confidence（自信）均不能表达"赢得众人的赞赏"。
2. C — left out （被冷落）
感觉被冷落/被排斥在外。朋友都围着Mia转，Ivy感觉自己被遗忘了。left out = 被排除在外/被冷落。taken in（被欺骗）/ picked on（被欺负）/ turned down（被拒绝）均不符合"被忽视"的语境。
3. C — unsettled （不安的）
不安的、心神不宁的。看到Mia被鼓掌时内心的不安感，与后面的 jealousy 呼应。contented（满足的）/ refreshing（令��耳目一新的）/ inclusive（包容的）均与嫉妒的情感发展不符。
4. B — forced （勉强挤出）
勉强挤出一个微笑。Ivy内心嫉妒但表面装开心，forced a smile 是固定搭配，表示笑得很勉强。radiated（散发）/ expected（期望）/ witnessed（目睹）均不能与 a smile 构成"假装开心"的语义。
5. A — avoiding （躲避）
开始躲避Mia。后文 conversations became short 说明Ivy在回避与Mia的交流。accepting（接受）/ forgiving（原谅）/ frightening（吓唬）均不能解释"对话变短"这一结果。
6. C — distant （疏远的）
你好像变得疏远了。与前文 "conversations became short" 和 avoiding 呼应。guilty（内疚的）/ shocked（震惊的）/ confused（困惑的）均不能准确描述Mia观察到的Ivy的变化。
7. D — effortlessly （毫不费力地）
我也许能毫不费力地获得关注。Mia承认自己的天赋让关注来得容易，但肯定Ivy有稳步成长的力量。fearlessly（无畏地）/ desperately（拼命地）/ hesitantly（犹豫地）均不能表达"天赋使然的轻松"。
8. B — shadow （阴影）
那道阴影（嫉妒）消退了。与前文 "Jealousy grew silently in her heart, like a shadow" 前后呼应。hope（希望）/ bond（纽带）/ light（光）均不能与前文的比喻形成首尾呼应。
9. A — worth （价值）
当我们忘记自己的价值时。嫉妒来自忘记自身价值，而非他人的成功。forget our own worth = 忘记了自己的价值。limit（极限）/ past（过去）/ fault（错误）均不能传达"认识自我价值"的主题。
10. D — compare （比较）
一旦认识到自身价值，就不需要比较了。与全文嫉妒源于比较的主题呼应——Ivy一直在compare herself with Mia，领悟之后便不再需要比较。control（控制）/ comment（评论）/ confirm（确认）均不符合文章主旨。
重点词汇（10个）▼
shift 转变，变化 考纲内
shift n./v. 转变，变化，转移。这里指友谊关系发生了微妙的变化。
But as they grew older, there was a shift. （课文）
There has been a shift in public opinion. 公众舆论发生了转变。
admiration 钦佩，赞赏 考纲内
admire v. 钦佩 → admiration n. 钦佩/赞赏。won the admiration of all = 赢得所有人的赞赏。
She won the admiration of all. （课文）
I have great admiration for her courage. 我非常钦佩她的勇气。
applauded 鼓掌，喝彩 考纲内
applaud v. 鼓掌/喝彩/称赞。applause n. 掌声。was applauded = 受到喝彩。
Ivy recognised an unsettled feeling when Mia was applauded. （课文）
The audience applauded loudly after the performance. 演出结束后观众大声鼓掌。
jealousy 嫉妒 考纲内
jealous adj. 嫉妒的 → jealousy n. 嫉妒心。区分：envy 羡慕（想拥有），jealousy 嫉妒（怕失去/不平）。
Jealousy grew silently in her heart, like a shadow. （课文）
His success aroused jealousy among his colleagues. 他的成功引起了同事们的嫉妒。
staring into space 发呆，出神 短语
stare v. 凝视，into space = 望向虚空。staring into space = 发呆/出神，表示心事重重。
She sat in her room, staring into space. （课文）
He was just sitting there, staring into space, lost in thought. 他就坐在那里发呆，陷入沉思。
hesitated 犹豫 考纲内
hesitate v. 犹豫/迟疑 → hesitation n. 犹豫。Ivy hesitated = Ivy犹豫了，在考虑要不要说实话。
Ivy hesitated, and then admitted how she felt. （课文）
Don't hesitate to ask if you need any help. 如果需要帮助，请不要犹豫。
admired 钦佩，欣赏 考纲内
admire v. 钦佩/欣赏。I've always admired you = 我一直很欣赏你。注意：admire ≠ 爱慕（中文容易理解偏）。
"I've always admired you." （课文）
I really admire your determination. 我真的很钦佩你的决心。
steady 稳定的，持续的 考纲内
steady adj. 稳定的/持续的。steady growth = 稳步成长。与effortlessly对比：Mia靠天赋，Ivy靠坚持。
You have the strength for steady growth. （课文）
She has made steady progress in her studies. 她的学业取得了稳步进展。
faded 消退，褪色 考纲内
fade v. 逐渐消失/褪色。the shadow faded = 那道阴影消退了，即嫉妒消失了。fade away = 渐渐消失。
Over time, the shadow faded. （课文）
The memories slowly faded with time. 记忆随着时间慢慢消退了。
takes root 扎根，根植于心 短语
take root = 生根/扎根（比喻：某种认识深入内心）。once that takes root = 一旦（对自身价值的认识）扎了根。
And once that takes root, there's no need to compare. （课文）
The idea of equality has taken root in modern society. 平等的观念已在现代社会扎根。
