B
Multigenerational Living
After we had twins and became a family of five, my husband and I decided to move from London back to Yorkshire, to be closer to my parents. Around the same time, they sold their house and we decided to build them a bungalow (平房) in our garden. We moved them in with us until the build was complete.
我们有了双胞胎、成为五口之家后，我和丈夫决定从伦敦搬回约克郡，离我父母近一些。差不多同一时间，他们卖掉了自己的房子，我们决定在花园里给他们建一栋平房。在房子建好之前，我们先让他们搬来和我们一起住。
Packing up my parents' house felt like a massive goodbye — that bedroom, those memories, the feeling of safety and shelter I always felt there. That evening, I sat in the living room watching TV, temporarily free of my own responsibilities, lost in nostalgia (留恋). Even though I'm a grown woman in my 40s with my own family, it felt scary not to have "home" to go back to.

这句的结构是：Even though（让步状语从句）+ it felt scary not to have "home" to go back to（主句，it做形式主语）。

Even though I'm a grown woman... 让步——虽然我已经是40多岁有自己家庭的成年女性。it felt scary not to have "home" to go back to——没有"家"可以回去这件事让人害怕。it 是形式主语，真正主语是 not to have "home" to go back to。

收拾父母的房子感觉像一次巨大的告别——那间卧室、那些回忆、我在那里总能感受到的安全和庇护。那天晚上，我坐在客厅看电视，暂时从自己的责任中解脱出来，沉浸在留恋之中。尽管我已经是一个40多岁、有自己家庭的成年女性，没有"家"可以回去的感觉还是让人害怕。
长难句拆解
让步状语从句Even though I'm a grown woman in my 40s with my own family, 形式主语it 谓语felt scary 真正主语not to have "home" to go back to.
1
先找主干：it felt scary not to have "home" to go back to→没有「家」可以回去，这让人害怕
2
让步状语从句：Even though I'm a grown woman in my 40s with my own family→虽然我已是40多岁有家庭的成年女性
3
it 是形式主语，真正主语是后面的不定式短语：not to have "home" to go back to→没有「家」可以回去
💡 Even though 引导让步状语从句，表示「虽然/尽管」。it 做形式主语 + 不定式做真正主语是高考常考句型：It is important to learn English = 学英语很重要。
On our final morning in the house, we took a break from the mind-bending, never-ending work of packing to sit down for that last breakfast. Mum voiced what we were all thinking: It was strange to see the place so empty. I worried that they'd begin to think they'd made a mistake.
在房子里的最后一个早晨，我们从令人头大的、永无止境的打包工作中歇了一口气，坐下来吃最后一顿早餐。妈妈说出了我们心里都在想的话：看到这个地方如此空荡荡的，感觉很奇怪。我担心他们会开始觉得自己做了一个错误的决定。
"How do you feel?" I asked. There was a silence as Dad continued to drink his tea, and I held my breath.
"你们感觉怎么样？"我问道。一阵沉默，爸爸继续喝着茶，我屏住了呼吸。
"Well, it's pretty awesome, isn't it?" he replied. I breathed a huge sigh of relief: I had expected sadness, maybe even doubt, but Dad was already looking to the future. His response had an impact on me.
"嗯，这挺棒的，不是吗？"他回答道。我如释重负地长舒了一口气：我原本预期的是伤感，甚至可能是犹豫，但爸爸已经在展望未来了。他的回答对我产生了影响。
Yes, it was a huge goodbye in so many ways, but it was also the start of something new: an adventure in multigenerational living. I realised this wasn't about loss at all, but about movement and trust. Hearing Dad's cheerfulness, I realised that what I'd been seeing as "the end of an era" was actually the start of a new one.

这句的结构是：Hearing Dad's cheerfulness（现在分词短语做状语）+ I realised that（主句）+ what I'd been seeing as "the end of an era" was actually the start of a new one（that引导的宾语从句，其中what引导主语从句）。

核心含义：听到爸爸的乐观态度，我意识到我一直认为的「一个时代的终结」实际上是一个新时代的开始。what I'd been seeing as... 中的过去完成进行时表示「我一直以来都把……看作」。

Sitting at the table, making my mini sandwich, I realised I was ready for our next chapter.
是的，这在很多方面都是一次巨大的告别，但它也是某种新事物的开始：一场多代同堂生活的冒险。我意识到这根本不是关于失去，而是关于前行和信任。听到爸爸的乐观态度，我意识到我一直视为「一个时代的终结」的东西，实际上是一个新时代的开始。坐在桌旁，做着我的迷你三明治，我意识到我已经准备好迎接我们的下一个篇章了。
长难句拆解
现在分词状语Hearing Dad's cheerfulness, 主句I realised that what主语从句what I'd been seeing as "the end of an era" 谓语was actually the start of a new one.
1
先找主干：I realised that … was actually the start of a new one→我意识到……实际上是一个新的开始
2
分词状语：Hearing Dad's cheerfulness→听到爸爸的乐观态度（表示时间/原因）
3
what 引导宾语从句中的主语从句：what I'd been seeing as "the end of an era"→我一直视为「一个时代终结」的东西
4
过去完成进行时 I'd been seeing→表示「一直以来都这样看」，强调持续性
💡 现在分词做状语（Hearing …, I realised …）是高考高频句型，表示伴随、时间或原因。what 引导名词性从句 = the thing that，作文中可套用：What I learned from this experience was that …
生词与短语（10个） ▼
bungalow 平房 超纲
bungalow 指只有一层的独立小房子，常见于英国乡村。build them a bungalow = 给他们建一栋平房
搭配：a detached bungalow 独栋平房
mind-bending 令人费解的/极其复杂的 超纲
mind = 头脑，bending = 弯曲。字面意思是「让大脑弯曲的」，形容事情极其复杂、令人头大
搭配：mind-bending puzzle 令人费解的谜题 · the mind-bending work of packing 令人头大的打包工作
multigenerational 多代人的 超纲
multi- = 多，generation = 代，-al = 形容词后缀。multigenerational living = 多代同堂生活
搭配：multigenerational household 多代同堂家庭
massive 巨大的 拓展
a massive goodbye = 一次巨大的告别。massive 比 big 更强烈，强调体量或程度极大
搭配：massive change 巨大变化 · massive amount 大量
nostalgia 留恋/怀旧 拓展
lost in nostalgia = 沉浸在怀旧之中。nostalgia 指对过去美好时光的怀念和眷恋
搭配：a wave of nostalgia 一阵怀旧之情 · feel nostalgia for 对……感到怀念
voiced 说出 拓展
voice 做动词时意为「说出（想法或感受）」。Mum voiced what we were all thinking = 妈妈说出了我们心里都在想的话
搭配：voice one's opinion 表达意见 · voice concern 表达担忧
sigh of relief 如释重负的叹息 拓展
sigh = 叹气，relief = 释然/宽慰。breathe a sigh of relief = 松了一口气，如释重负地叹了口气
搭配：a collective sigh of relief 众人一起松了口气 · a huge sigh of relief 大大地松了口气
cheerfulness 乐观/开朗 拓展
cheer = 欢呼/愉快，-ful = 充满，-ness = 名词后缀。Dad's cheerfulness = 爸爸的乐观态度
搭配：radiate cheerfulness 散发出乐观的气质
era 时代 拓展
the end of an era = 一个时代的终结。era 指一个有特定特征的历史时期
搭配：a new era 新时代 · the era of … 某某时代
chapter 篇章/阶段 拓展
chapter 本义是「章节」，这里比喻人生的一个阶段。our next chapter = 我们的下一个人生篇章
搭配：a new chapter in life 人生新篇章 · close a chapter 翻篇
题目解析（4题，24—27） ▼
24. After they had twins, the author and her husband decided to________.
A. visit her parents
B. move back to Yorkshire
C. rent a house for the family
D. raise their children in London
答案：B
"After we had twins and became a family of five, my husband and I decided to move from London back to Yorkshire, to be closer to my parents."

解题思路：原文第一句直接说"decided to move from London back to Yorkshire"，完全对应B选项。属于细节题，原文原词复现。

A. visit her parents ✗是搬回去住在父母附近，不是去探望
B. move back to Yorkshire ✓原文原词：decided to move from London back to Yorkshire
C. rent a house for the family ✗文中未提及租房
D. raise their children in London ✗恰恰相反，他们离开了伦敦
25. During the breakfast, the author worried that________.
A. she might lose her shelter in the future
B. there would be too much work in packing
C. her parents might regret selling their house
D. it would be hard to live a multigenerational life
答案：C
"I worried that they'd begin to think they'd made a mistake."

解题思路：原文明确写到"I worried that they'd begin to think they'd made a mistake"——担心父母会觉得自己做错了决定（即后悔卖房）。C选项"regret selling their house"是对原文的同义转述。

A. she might lose her shelter in the future ✗「失去庇护」是作者自己在第2段的感受，不是早餐时的担忧
B. there would be too much work in packing ✗打包的辛苦是事实描述，不是她担忧的内容
C. her parents might regret selling their house ✓they'd made a mistake = 后悔卖房，完全对应
D. it would be hard to live a multigenerational life ✗多代同堂的困难在文中从未被提及为担忧
26. How did the author feel after hearing her dad's response?
A. Uplifted.
B. Puzzled.
C. Concerned.
D. Honoured.
答案：A
"I breathed a huge sigh of relief: I had expected sadness, maybe even doubt, but Dad was already looking to the future."
"Hearing Dad's cheerfulness, I realised that what I'd been seeing as 'the end of an era' was actually the start of a new one."

解题思路：Dad说"pretty awesome"，作者"breathed a huge sigh of relief"（如释重负），并受到鼓舞，意识到这是新篇章的开始，最后说"I was ready for our next chapter"。Uplifted = 振奋的、受鼓舞的，完美对应。

A. Uplifted ✓sigh of relief + 意识到新开始 + ready for next chapter = 受鼓舞/振奋
B. Puzzled ✗作者虽然意外但没有困惑，而是立刻受到感染
C. Concerned ✗担忧是听到回答之前的状态，听到后是释然和振奋
D. Honoured ✗荣幸/受宠若惊与语境不符，作者感受是被鼓舞
27. What does the story mainly tell us?
A. Silence is the best cure for nostalgia.
B. Distance makes the heart grow fonder.
C. Optimism lights the path for tomorrow.
D. Parenting sets the pace of a child's growth.
答案：C
"'Well, it's pretty awesome, isn't it?' he replied."
"Hearing Dad's cheerfulness, I realised that what I'd been seeing as 'the end of an era' was actually the start of a new one."

解题思路：全文主旨——Dad的乐观态度（cheerfulness / "pretty awesome"）让作者从留恋过去转向拥抱未来，意识到这不是终结而是新开始。C选项"Optimism lights the path for tomorrow"（乐观照亮明天的路）完美概括了这一主题。

A. Silence is the best cure for nostalgia ✗文中的沉默只是短暂的等待，不是治愈怀旧的方式
B. Distance makes the heart grow fonder ✗文章讲的是搬到一起住，不是远离
C. Optimism lights the path for tomorrow ✓Dad的乐观 → 作者从留恋过去转向拥抱未来 = 乐观照亮明天
D. Parenting sets the pace of a child's growth ✗文章不是在讨论育儿方式对孩子成长的影响

我这篇的卡点：

标记：

阅读技巧：家庭/情感叙事类文章怎么读 ▼

B篇常考"个人叙事/情感感悟"类——作者通过亲身经历得出人生道理。本文是典型的家庭生活叙事。有3个核心策略：

① 抓住情感转折点

叙事类文章一定有一个turning point（转折点）。本文的转折点是Dad说"it's pretty awesome, isn't it?"——从此作者的心态从留恋与担忧转向乐观与期待。找到转折点，前后的"情感变化"就清楚了：之前不舍过去 → 之后拥抱未来。

② 关注末段的总结句

感悟类文章的核心观点通常在最后一两句。本文末段连续三个 "I realised" 层层推进：① 不是失去，而是前行和信任 ② 不是终结，而是新的开始 ③ 我准备好了。Q27主旨题直接考的就是这些感悟。做题时先看末段，事半功倍。

③ 注意"谁的情感"和"什么时候"

本文有多处情感描写，做题时要注意区分是谁的情感以及发生在什么时候。比如Q26问的是"听到Dad回答之后"的感受，不能选"听到之前"的担忧（Concerned）。又如Q25问的是"早餐时"的担忧，不能选第2段"那天晚上"的感受。时间线很重要：收拾房子 → 当晚怀旧 → 最后早餐 → Dad回答 → 作者感悟。

作文拓展：好句式 & 连接词速查 ▼
从文章偷来的好句式
Even though ..., it felt scary not to have ...
虽然……，没有……的感觉还是让人害怕（Even though让步 + it做形式主语）
原句：Even though I'm a grown woman in my 40s with my own family, it felt scary not to have "home" to go back to.
作文套用：Even though we live in the digital age, it feels lonely not to have real friends around. 写情感类作文时表达矛盾心理，非常好用。
Hearing / Seeing ..., I realised that ...
听到/看到……，我意识到……（现在分词状语 + 主句）
原句：Hearing Dad's cheerfulness, I realised that what I'd been seeing as "the end of an era" was actually the start of a new one.
作文套用：Seeing my mother's tired smile, I realised that love often comes in the quietest forms. 写亲情/感悟类作文，一句话搞定转折。
This wasn't about A at all, but about B.
这根本不是关于A，而是关于B（否定+肯定，对比深刻）
原句：I realised this wasn't about loss at all, but about movement and trust.
作文套用：The competition wasn't about winning at all, but about pushing our limits and growing together. 议论文/叙事文中升华观点用。
... not A, but B
不是A，而是B（经典对比）
原句：it was also the start of something new: an adventure in multigenerational living.
作文套用：Moving to a new city was not an ending, but a beginning full of possibilities. 展示辩证思维，升华结尾。
I was ready for our next chapter.
我已准备好迎接下一个篇章（chapter比喻人生阶段）
原句：Sitting at the table, making my mini sandwich, I realised I was ready for our next chapter.
作文套用：Standing at the school gate for the last time, I knew I was ready for the next chapter of my life. 毕业/成长类作文结尾，简洁有力。
连接词速查（按功能分）
递进 / 补充
furthermore moreover in addition what's more not only...but also
比 "also" 和 "and" 正式，议论文首选
转折 / 对比
however yet instead in contrast on the other hand
本文多处用 but / even though 来表示情感转折
结果 / 因此
as a result therefore consequently thus
thus 最简洁，thus + 分词短语直接高分
举例说明
for instance for example take...for example such as
for instance 比 for example 更口语化一点点，两者都可用
总结 / 结尾
in conclusion to sum up in short overall in a word
结尾段第一句用，考官扫一眼就知道你会写结论
顺序 / 衔接
first of all secondly finally meanwhile at the same time
列举多个原因/步骤时用，让文章结构清晰
