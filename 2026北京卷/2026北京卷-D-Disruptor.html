Chesterton's Fence
The 21st century has created a whole range of new types of professionals that we have never had before—or if not quite new, at least newly repackaged. The world has always had influential people, for instance, but only now do we have "Influencer". Similarly, the corporate world has certainly known its fair share of disruption, yet it is only in the last couple of decades that the "Disruptor" has become a recognisable figure in the way we talk about big business.

这句的核心结构是：the corporate world has known disruption, yet it is only...that the "Disruptor" has become...。前半句用 has known its fair share of 表示"经历过不少"，yet 转折后用强调句 it is only in the last couple of decades that... 强调"颠覆者"作为一个可辨识的角色是最近二十年才出现的。

核心意思：商业界当然经历过不少颠覆，但"颠覆者"作为一个可辨识的角色是最近二十年才出现在我们谈论大企业的方式中。

21世纪创造了一系列我们从未有过的新型职业身份——即使不完全是新的，至少是重新包装过的。比如，世界上一直都有有影响力的人，但直到现在我们才有了"网红"。同样，商业界当然经历过不少颠覆，但"颠覆者"作为一个可辨识的角色出现在我们谈论大企业的方式中，也不过是最近二十年的事。

状语Similarly, 主语the corporate world 修饰has certainly known its fair share of disruption, yet 转折 + 强调句yet it is only in the last couple of decades that the "Disruptor" has become a recognisable figure in the way we talk about big business.

2

In an earlier era, the Disruptor would have been called an Innovator. Yet while an Innovator creates something new, the Disruptor is defined as much by what they destroy as by what they create. The Disruptor forces change; the clean-up is somebody else's problem. It's an attitude best summed up as "Move fast and break things."
在更早的时代，颠覆者会被称为创新者。然而，创新者创造新事物，颠覆者则既以他们创造的东西来定义，也以他们破坏的东西来定义。颠覆者强行推动变革；善后是别人的事。这种态度最好被概括为"快速行动，打破一切"。
Back in 1929, G. K. Chesterton identified the core problem with moving fast and breaking things through a thought experiment. Imagine there is a fence along a road. Suppose it's been there a long time, and was put there for some purpose. But that purpose is lost over time. To us, now, the fence seems to be nothing but an impediment. Why not remove it?
早在1929年，G·K·切斯特顿通过一个思想实验指出了"快速行动、打破一切"的核心问题。想象一下，有一道栅栏沿着道路而立。假设它已经在那里很久了，当初是为了某种目的而建造的。但那个目的随着时间流逝而失传了。对我们来说，现在这道栅栏似乎只是一个障碍。为什么不把它拆了呢？
When faced with such a fence, Chesterton thinks the fact that we can't see the point of the fence is why we should not tear it down. Some fences keep foxes out of henhouses, for instance. If you think you can do without the fence because you've never seen a fox, consider if the existence of the fence is precisely why you've never seen one.

这句是多层嵌套的条件句：外层是 If you think...consider if...。think 后面跟宾语从句 you can do without the fence，because 引导原因状语从句。consider if 后面又引出一个条件/疑问从句：the existence of the fence is precisely why you've never seen one。逻辑链条：你以为不需要栅栏→因为从没见过狐狸→但也许正是栅栏的存在才让你没见过狐狸。

面对这样一道栅栏，切斯特顿认为，我们看不到栅栏的意义这一事实，恰恰是我们不应该拆除它的原因。比如，有些栅栏是为了把狐狸挡在鸡舍外面。如果你觉得可以不要这道栅栏，因为你从没见过狐狸，那请想想：栅栏的存在是否恰恰就是你从没见过狐狸的原因。
长难句拆解
If 条件If you think you can do without the fence because you've never seen a fox, 主句consider if the existence of the fence is precisely why you've never seen one.
1
外层条件：If you think...→如果你认为……
2
think 的宾语从句：you can do without the fence→你可以不要这道栅栏
3
because 原因从句：because you've never seen a fox→因为你从没见过狐狸
4
主句 + consider if 从句：consider if the existence of the fence is precisely why you've never seen one→想想栅栏的存在是否恰恰就是你从没见过狐狸的原因
💡 这句话的精妙在于因果反转：你以为"没有狐狸→不需要栅栏"，但实际可能是"有栅栏→看不到狐狸"。高考阅读中常考这种逻辑推理。
Importantly, Chesterton is not saying that things can never be reformed. His is a preventive principle rather than an outright prohibition. We could rephrase it like this: understand the wisdom in past decisions before you reject them. If we're too impetuous, we can end up tearing out things we don't yet realise we rely upon. But uncritical devotion to the "way things are done" can also deny us the possibility of better days.
重要的是，切斯特顿并不是在说事物永远不能改革。他的是一项预防性原则，而非彻底的禁令。我们可以这样换句话说：在你否定过去的决策之前，先理解其中的智慧。如果我们太冲动，最终可能会拆掉那些我们还没意识到自己依赖的东西。但对"现有做法"不加批判的执着，也会让我们失去拥有更美好未来的可能性。
What is required, then, is not the fearlessness of the Disruptor, but the humility to stop and ask questions and to know that our own perspective is neither unique nor comprehensive.

这句的核心结构是：What is required is not A, but B。What 引导主语从句（所需要的东西），then 是插入语，is 是系动词，not...but... 构成表语部分。not the fearlessness of the Disruptor 是被否定的A，but the humility to... 是被肯定的B。后面两个 and to... 并列修饰 humility。

Disruption can be a force for good. It can also just leave a big mess. We need the ability to work out which messes are worth avoiding—because however impressively fast you move, some things can't be easily unbroken.
那么，我们需要的不是颠覆者的无畏，而是停下来提问的谦卑，以及认识到我们自身的视角既不独特也不全面。颠覆可以是一种积极的力量。但它也可能只留下一团糟。我们需要判断哪些混乱值得避免——因为无论你行动得多么迅速，有些东西一旦打碎就无法轻易复原。

题目解析（4题，31—34） ▼
31. Regarding Chesterton's principle, the author is________.
A. supportive
B. doubtful
C. conflicted
D. disappointed
答案：A
"We could rephrase it like this: understand the wisdom in past decisions before you reject them."

解题思路：态度题。作者在第5-6段对 Chesterton 的原则表示认同——理解过去决策的智慧再做改变，需要谦卑而非鲁莽。第6段 "What is required...is...the humility to stop and ask questions" 明确呼应了 Chesterton 的原则。

A. supportive ✓作者全文论证支持 Chesterton 的预防性原则
B. doubtful ✗作者没有表示怀疑，而是认同并进一步阐释
C. conflicted ✗虽然作者提到 disruption 也有好的一面，但整体立场是明确支持的，不矛盾
D. disappointed ✗文中没有任何失望的情感表达
32. What does the word "impetuous" underlined in Paragraph 5 most probably mean?
A. Short-sighted
B. Narrow-minded
C. Hot-headed
D. Old-fashioned
答案：C
"If we're too impetuous, we can end up tearing out things we don't yet realise we rely upon."

解题思路：词义猜测题。impetuous = 冲动的、鲁莽的。上下文"tearing out things we don't yet realise we rely upon"说明太冲动会拆掉我们需要的东西。与"Move fast and break things"呼应。Hot-headed = 急躁冲动的，最接近原意。

A. Short-sighted ✗目光短浅的，强调看不到长远，但 impetuous 强调的是行动冲动
B. Narrow-minded ✗思想狭隘的，与上下文不符
C. Hot-headed ✓急躁冲动的，与"tearing out things"的鲁莽行为一致
D. Old-fashioned ✗守旧的，与语境完全相反——impetuous 的人恰恰是急于变革
33. What can we infer from this passage about the Disruptor?
A. They may clear up other people's mess.
B. They are in favour of the preventive principle.
C. They preserve more traditions than they break.
D. They may create more problems than they solve.
答案：D
"The Disruptor forces change; the clean-up is somebody else's problem."

解题思路：推断题。原文多处暗示颠覆者可能制造比解决的更多的问题："the clean-up is somebody else's problem"（善后是别人的事）+ "Disruption can also just leave a big mess"（颠覆也可能只留下一团糟）+ "some things can't be easily unbroken"（有些东西无法轻易复原）。

A. They may clear up other people's mess. ✗恰恰相反，原文说"the clean-up is somebody else's problem"
B. They are in favour of the preventive principle. ✗预防性原则是 Chesterton 提出的，颠覆者的态度是"Move fast and break things"
C. They preserve more traditions than they break. ✗颠覆者以破坏著称，不以保守传统著称
D. They may create more problems than they solve. ✓多处原文支持：留下一团糟、有些东西无法复原
34. What does the author intend to tell us?
A. Humility should be a driving force for human progress.
B. Innovation should move forward the wheel of tradition.
C. Reform should draw from the wellspring of past wisdom.
D. Disruption should be given priority in social development.
答案：C
"understand the wisdom in past decisions before you reject them."

解题思路：主旨题。全文核心观点：改革应该汲取过去智慧的源泉。"understand the wisdom in past decisions before you reject them"（在否定之前先理解过去决策的智慧）正是文章的中心论点。

A. Humility should be a driving force for human progress. ✗谦卑是手段之一，但文章核心不是谦卑推动进步，而是改革要基于理解
B. Innovation should move forward the wheel of tradition. ✗文章讨论的是改革前要理解传统，不是创新推动传统前进
C. Reform should draw from the wellspring of past wisdom. ✓完美对应"understand the wisdom in past decisions before you reject them"
D. Disruption should be given priority in social development. ✗与文章立场相反——文章批评了盲目的颠覆

标记：

阅读技巧：议论文/思辨类文章怎么读 ▼

D篇常考议论文/思辨类文章——作者围绕一个核心概念展开论证，题目考你能不能抓住论点与论据的关系、理解类比的作用、读懂转折结构。有3个核心策略：

① 抓住类比与思想实验

本文的核心类比是 Chesterton's Fence（切斯特顿的栅栏）——用一道看似无用的栅栏来比喻那些我们不理解其价值的传统/制度。思想实验不是论点本身，而是帮你理解论点的工具。做题时要问：这个类比在说明什么道理？

② 注意"不是A而是B"的论证结构

本文两处关键对比：Innovator vs. Disruptor（创新者 vs. 颠覆者）和 preventive principle vs. outright prohibition（预防性原则 vs. 彻底禁令）。作者通过对比来精确界定自己的立场——既不是盲目反对改变，也不是支持鲁莽颠覆。这种"两边都不极端"的立场是高考议论文的常见考点。

③ 因果逻辑链的反转

第4段的栅栏-狐狸论证是经典的因果反转：表面因果是"没有狐狸→不需要栅栏"，实际因果是"有栅栏→看不到狐狸"。这种逻辑推理在高考推断题中经常考到。遇到 because / why / the reason 时要特别注意因果方向是否被反转。

作文拓展：好句式 & 连接词速查 ▼
从文章偷来的好句式
What is required is not A, but B
所需要的不是A，而是B
原句：What is required, then, is not the fearlessness of the Disruptor, but the humility to stop and ask questions.
作文套用：What is required in education is not blind obedience, but the courage to think independently. 用 What 主语从句 + not...but 对比，非常高级。
understand the wisdom in past decisions before you reject them
在否定之前先理解其中的智慧
原句：We could rephrase it like this: understand the wisdom in past decisions before you reject them.
作文套用：We should understand the wisdom in traditional customs before we reject them as outdated. 适合写传统文化/改革类话题。
defined as much by X as by Y
既由X定义，也由Y定义
原句：The Disruptor is defined as much by what they destroy as by what they create.
作文套用：A person is defined as much by how they handle failure as by how they celebrate success. 用 as much...as 做平行对比，体现辩证思维。
however impressively X, some things can't be easily Y
无论...多么...，有些东西无法轻易...
原句：However impressively fast you move, some things can't be easily unbroken.
作文套用：However impressively technology advances, some human connections can't be easily replaced. 让步 + 强调，适合科技话题结尾。
neither unique nor comprehensive
既不独特也不全面
原句：...to know that our own perspective is neither unique nor comprehensive.
作文套用：We must acknowledge that our viewpoint is neither unique nor comprehensive, and learn to embrace different perspectives. 用于议论文中表达谦逊和开放心态。
