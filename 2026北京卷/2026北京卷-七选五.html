2026北京卷七选五 · Morality & Happiness

Q35–39
七选五阅读理解
根据短文内容，从短文后的选项中选出能填入空白处的最佳选项。选项中有两项为多余选项。
Most of us have experienced both how good it can feel and how hard it can be to do the right thing. Helping strangers can leave you with a deep sense of satisfaction, but you've likely also felt the stress of showing up for others when you're already stretched thin. These ordinary moments speak to a philosophical puzzle: Are moral people happier? [35]D Others might reason that moral behavior often requires self-sacrifice.
[36]A Past studies clearly show that people feel good when they perform acts of kindness for others. But researchers had not directly examined if moral character, broadly speaking, is linked to well-being. So my team conducted a set of studies to take this challenge seriously.
In one of our studies, undergraduate students reported on their own well-being, rating their happiness as well as their sense of how meaningful their life is. [37]E Then these close others, such as friends and family members, rated the participant's moral character using our questionnaire (调查问卷), which asked how much that person had the various characteristics identified as most morally relevant. [38]F
Doing the right thing isn't always easy. [39]C But our findings indicate that, in general, there's no trade-off between morality and well-being. Instead, people with stronger moral character tend to experience greater meaning in life and happiness. This suggests that moments of moral effort may be part of a life that, taken as a whole, feels more satisfying.
选项 A–G（有两项多余）
A.Until recently, scientific evidence had been limited to one aspect of morality: kindness.
B.Despite various debates, there's no agreement on what it means to be morally good.
C.Standing up for someone who is being treated unfairly can feel uncomfortable.
D.Some might assume morality and happiness naturally go together.
E.They also gave us contact information for people who know them.
F.We found that those rated as more moral reported a stronger sense of well-being.
G.This might be in part because of the difficulties of defining and measuring kindness.
答案解析（35—39题）▼
35题 答案：D — Some might assume morality and happiness naturally go together.
上文：Are moral people happier? 下文：Others might reason that moral behavior often requires self-sacrifice.
逻辑：前文提出问题"道德的人更幸福吗？"，后文说"Others might reason that moral behavior requires self-sacrifice"（另一些人认为道德需要自我牺牲）。D说"Some might assume morality and happiness naturally go together"（一些人认为道德和幸福自然相伴），与Others形成对比——一部分人认为相伴，另一部分人认为需要牺牲。
排除 B/G：B 说"没有共识"与上下文对比结构不符；G 讨论"衡量善良的困难"放在此处语境不通。
36题 答案：A — Until recently, scientific evidence had been limited to one aspect of morality: kindness.
下文：Past studies clearly show that people feel good when they perform acts of kindness for others. But researchers had not directly examined if moral character broadly speaking is linked to well-being.
逻辑：后文说"Past studies clearly show that people feel good when they perform acts of kindness"，但"researchers had not directly examined if moral character broadly speaking is linked to well-being"。A说"直到最近，科学证据仅限于道德的一个方面：善良"，承接后文从kindness到broader moral character的研究转变。
排除 B：B 谈"没有共识"与本段"研究证据"的话题不吻合。
37题 答案：E — They also gave us contact information for people who know them.
上文：undergraduate students reported on their own well-being. 下文：Then these close others … rated the participant's moral character.
逻辑：前文说学生报告了自己的幸福感，后文说"Then these close others rated the participant's moral character"。E说"他们还给了我们认识他们的人的联系方式"，逻辑上连接：学生先自评 → 提供联系人 → 联系人再评价他们的道德品质。these close others 回指E中"people who know them"。
38题 答案：F — We found that those rated as more moral reported a stronger sense of well-being.
上文：which asked how much that person had the various characteristics identified as most morally relevant.
逻辑：前文描述了研究方法（学生自评幸福 + 他人评价道德），F说"We found that those rated as more moral reported a stronger sense of well-being"——给出了研究结果，是方法段的自然收尾。"those rated as more moral"呼应上文他人评价道德品质，"a stronger sense of well-being"呼应前文学生自评幸福感。
39题 答案：C — Standing up for someone who is being treated unfairly can feel uncomfortable.
上文：Doing the right thing isn't always easy. 下文：But our findings indicate that, in general, there's no trade-off between morality and well-being.
逻辑：前文说"Doing the right thing isn't always easy"，后文用But转折说"there's no trade-off between morality and well-being"。C说"Standing up for someone who is being treated unfairly can feel uncomfortable"——举例说明做正确的事不容易，然后But转折表明尽管如此，道德与幸福并不矛盾。
排除 B/G：B 说"没有共识"与此处具体举例的语境不符；G 谈"衡量善良的困难"与上下文不搭配。

七选五做题技巧

  • 看衔接词：Some … Others / But / Until recently / Instead 等是关键线索，直接锁定逻辑关系（对比/转折/时间/递进）。
  • 找代词呼应：代词（they / these close others / those）必须指向上文某个具体名词；代词能对上，选项通常就对了。
  • 读前后句：空格填进去后，上下句必须"话题连贯"——前句话题收尾，所选句承接，下句继续展开。
  • 排除法最有效：先排除话题不符或逻辑矛盾的选项（B 与上下文"道德与幸福"主线无关；G 讨论"定义和衡量善良的困难"放在各空均不合适）。
重点词汇（10个） ▼
stretched thin 分身乏术 拓展
stretch v. 拉伸 → stretched thin 被拉得太薄 = 分身乏术、力不从心。
when you're already stretched thin = 当你已经分身乏术时  ·  be stretched thin at work 工作上力不从心
philosophical 哲学的 考纲内
philosophy n. 哲学 → philosophical adj. 哲学的。
a philosophical puzzle = 一个哲学难题  ·  philosophical debate 哲学辩论  ·  philosopher n. 哲学家
self-sacrifice 自我牺牲 考纲内
self-（自我）+ sacrifice（牺牲）= 自我牺牲。
moral behavior often requires self-sacrifice = 道德行为常常需要自我牺牲  ·  self-sacrificing adj. 自我牺牲的
well-being 幸福感 高频
well（好）+ being（存在）= 幸福感/健康状态。
is linked to well-being = 与幸福感有关联  ·  physical/mental well-being 身体/心理健康  ·  a sense of well-being 幸福感
undergraduate 本科生 考纲内
under-（在…之下）+ graduate（毕业生）= 尚未毕业的学生 = 本科生。
undergraduate students = 本科生  ·  对比：graduate 研究生 / postgraduate 研究生
questionnaire 调查问卷 考纲内
question n. 问题 → questionnaire n. 调查问卷。注意拼写：-nn- 双写。
fill out a questionnaire = 填写调查问卷  ·  using our questionnaire 使用我们的调查问卷
morally relevant 道德相关的 拓展
morally adv. 道德上 + relevant adj. 相关的 = 道德相关的。
characteristics identified as most morally relevant = 被认定为最具道德相关性的特征  ·  moral adj. 道德的 → morally adv.
trade-off 权衡/取舍 高频
trade（交易）+ off = 权衡取舍。做名词时常写作 trade-off。
there's no trade-off between morality and well-being = 道德和幸福之间没有取舍关系  ·  face a trade-off 面临取舍
satisfying 令人满足的 考纲内
satisfy v. 使满足 → satisfying adj. 令人满足的 → satisfaction n. 满足感。
feels more satisfying = 感觉更令人满足  ·  a satisfying result 令人满意的结果  ·  对比：satisfied 感到满足的（修饰人）
standing up for 为...挺身而出 高频
stand up for = 为…挺身而出/维护/支持。
standing up for someone who is being treated unfairly = 为被不公平对待的人挺身而出  ·  stand up for your rights 维护你的权利
