a programme that intends to reduce waste. （课文）

题目解析（3题，21—23） ▼
21. The programme aims to________.
A. create new materials
B. cut down on waste
C. set up social platforms
D. promote language learning
答案：B
"UPCYCLING is a programme that intends to reduce waste."

细节题。题目问项目的目标。定位到 About the Programme 部分第一句：UPCYCLING is a programme that intends to reduce waste（旨在减少废物）。选项 B "cut down on waste" 是对原文 "reduce waste" 的同义替换。选 B。

✗ A— create new materials：课文说的是 "reimagining items"（重新设想物品）和 "reclaimed materials"（回收材料），是利用已有废旧材料，不是创造新材料。偷换概念。
✓ B— cut down on waste：与课文 "intends to reduce waste" 直接对应。reduce = cut down on（同义替换）。
✗ C— set up social platforms：课文提到 "promote projects through social platforms"（通过社交平台推广项目），但"通过社交平台推广"不等于"建立社交平台"。张冠李戴。
✗ D— promote language learning：课文在 Free-time Activities 部分提到 "have language classes"（上语言课），但那是空闲时间的可选活动，不是项目的目标。以偏概全。
22. The programme provides certain community members with________.
A. free-time activities
B. upcycled goods
C. hotel services
D. airport pickups
答案：B
"Community Services: support communities by providing upcycled goods to those in need..."

细节题。题目问项目为社区成员提供什么。定位到 What to Expect 部分的 Community Services："providing upcycled goods to those in need"（向有需要的人提供升级改造的物品）。选 B。

✗ A— free-time activities：空闲活动是提供给志愿者的，不是提供给社区成员的。对象混淆。
✓ B— upcycled goods：与课文 "providing upcycled goods to those in need" 直接对应。those in need = certain community members（有需要的社区成员）。
✗ C— hotel services：课文提到 "accommodation with breakfast"（含早餐的住宿）和 "Breakfast at the hotel"（在酒店吃早餐），但这些是提供给志愿者的，不是社区成员的。对象混淆。
✗ D— airport pickups：机场接机是提供给志愿者的服务（Info box 中 "You'll get: airport pickup"），不是提供给社区成员的。对象混淆。
23. What can volunteers do?
A. Schedule the projects
B. Organise creative workshops
C. Design art classes
D. Participate in water sports
答案：D
"In your free time, you can explore the city, have language classes, surf or choose your favourite from among the following: Riding, Hiking, Swimming, Water Sports, Concert, Museum."

细节题。题目问志愿者可以做什么。定位到 Free-time Activities 部分：列出了志愿者可选的活动，其中包括 Water Sports（水上运动）。选项 D "Participate in water sports" 与原文吻合。选 D。

✗ A— Schedule the projects：课文没有说志愿者可以安排项目日程。志愿者是按既定时间（morning, afternoon or evening shift）参与工作。无中生有。
✗ B— Organise creative workshops：课文说的是志愿者在 Creative Workshops 中 "learn basic repairs and artistic redesign"（学习基本修理和艺术重新设计），是参加工坊学习，不是组织工坊。主体混淆。
✗ C— Design art classes：课文没有提到志愿者设计艺术课程。虽然有 Creative Workshops，但志愿者是学习者，不是课程设计者。无中生有。
✓ D— Participate in water sports：Free-time Activities 部分明确列出 Water Sports 作为志愿者可选活动之一。

About the Programme

UPCYCLING is a programme that intends to reduce waste. By reimagining items that might otherwise end up in landfills (垃圾场), UPCYCLING not only helps conserve natural resources but also encourages innovation and environmental awareness.

We welcome volunteers to help transform reclaimed materials into objects with new and creative purposes.

What to Expect

  • Furniture Restoration: take an active role in transforming reclaimed furniture and materials into useful items, giving them a new life;
  • Creative Workshops: learn basic repairs and artistic redesign of old furniture, while sharing knowledge and ideas with fellow volunteers.
  • Social & Environmental Action: help address social and environmental challenges through creativity and cooperation.
  • Community Services: support communities by providing upcycled goods to those in need and help promote projects through social platforms to widen outreach.

A Typical Day

You will work on a morning, afternoon or evening shift at your project site for 3 to 5 hours per day, Monday to Friday.

8:00–10:00 amBreakfast at the hotel
10:00 amWork at the project sites, or enjoy your free time

Free-time Activities

In your free time, you can explore the city, have language classes, surf or choose your favourite from among the following: Riding, Hiking, Swimming, Water Sports, Concert, Museum.

阅读技巧▼

志愿者招募/项目介绍类文章读法（A篇是此类文章的典型）：

  • 第一步：看标题和信息框。本文标题"Volunteers Wanted — UPCYCLING"说明是志愿者招募。信息框给出年龄、时长和待遇等基本信息。先浏览这些，快速了解文章主题。
  • 第二步：看题目，带着问题读。21题问"项目目标"，定位到 About the Programme 部分；22题问"为社区成员提供什么"，定位到 Community Services 部分；23题问"志愿者能做什么"，定位到 Free-time Activities 部分。
  • 第三步：注意"提供给谁"的区别。本文中有些服务是给志愿者的（airport pickup, accommodation, free-time activities），有些是给社区成员的（upcycled goods）。22题的干扰项就是利用了这个区别。

常见陷阱（本篇三道题都有）：

  • 同义替换：21题B选项 "cut down on waste" 替换原文 "reduce waste"，意思相同。
  • 对象混淆：22题ACD三个选项的内容都在课文中出现过，但都是提供给志愿者的，不是提供给社区成员的。做题时务必注意"谁提供给谁"。
  • 角色混淆：23题B "Organise creative workshops" 把志愿者的角色从"学习者/参与者"偷换成了"组织者"。
作文拓展：好句式 & 好短语▼

可以直接用进作文的高频表达：

  • cut down on waste / reduce waste（减少浪费）—— We should cut down on waste by recycling and upcycling.
  • conserve natural resources（保护自然资源）—— It is our duty to conserve natural resources for future generations.
  • take an active role in（积极参与）—— Students should take an active role in community service.
  • address challenges through cooperation（通过合作应对挑战）—— We can address environmental challenges through cooperation.
  • widen outreach（扩大影响/推广范围）—— Social media helps widen the outreach of volunteer projects.

环保/志愿类写作结构（如果考作文也是此类话题）：

  • 开头：介绍项目名称与目的（We are launching a programme that intends to... / Volunteers are wanted for...）
  • 中间：分条说明活动内容/志愿者职责（Volunteers will be responsible for... / Activities include...）
  • 结尾：号召参加（Join us and make a difference! / Your participation will contribute to a greener future!）
