北京卷D篇精读 · The Best of Friends

D
The evidence for harmony may not be obvious in some families. But it seems that four out of five young people now get on with their parents, which is the opposite of the popularly-held image of unhappy teenagers locked in their room after endless family quarrels.

句子结构：主干是 four out of five young people get on with their parents（五分之四的年轻人和父母关系融洽）。

which 是谁？逗号 + which 引导非限制性定语从句——这个 which 不是指前面某一个词，而是指整个主句的结论。which is the opposite of... = 这件事（4/5年轻人和父母相处融洽）和大众刻板印象正好相反。

后面的分词定语：locked in their room after endless family quarrels 是过去分词短语，修饰 teenagers。 = teenagers who are locked in their room...（被关在房间里的那种青少年）。高考阅读里这种「名词 + 分词短语」很常见，别被它割断了对主句的理解。

和睦的证据在某些家庭里可能不那么明显。但看起来，每五个年轻人中就有四个能和父母和睦相处——这与大众对于青少年的刻板印象恰恰相反：那种吵完架就把自己锁在房间里的不开心的青少年。
An important new study into teenage attitudes surprisingly shows that their family life is more harmonious than it has ever been in the past. "We were surprised by just how positive today's young people seem to be about their families," said one member of the research team. "They're expected to be rebellious and selfish but actually they have other things on their minds: they want a car and material goods, and they worry about whether school is serving them well. There's more negotiation and discussion between parents and children, and children expect to take part in the family decision-making process. They don't want to rock the boat."
一项重要的关于青少年态度的新研究令人惊讶地显示，他们的家庭生活比以往任何时候都更和谐。"我们对当今年轻人对家庭如此积极的态度感到吃惊，"一位研究团队成员说，"人们以为他们会叛逆、自私，但实际上他们心里想的是别的：他们想要车子和物质财富，他们担心学校是否真正对他们有帮助。父母和孩子之间有了更多协商和讨论，孩子们也希望参与家庭决策过程。他们不想惹麻烦。"
So it seems that this generation of parents is much more likely than parents of 30 years ago to treat their children as friends.

句子结构：开头 it seems that... 是形式主语，真正的主语是后面整个 that 从句。

比较级核心：much more likely than parents of 30 years ago to treat their children as friends——拆开看：more likely than A（比A更有可能）+ to treat...（做什么）。much 是程度副词，让比较级更强。

主干提炼：this generation of parents is likely to treat their children as friends（这代父母倾向于把孩子当朋友）。

"My parents are happy to discuss things with me and willing to listen to me," says 17-year-old Daniel Lazall. "I always tell them when I'm going out clubbing. As long as they know what I'm doing, they're fine with it." Susan Crome, who is now 21, agrees. "Looking back on the last 10 years, there was a lot of what you could call negotiation. For example, as long as I'd done all my homework, I could go out on a Saturday night. But I think my grandparents were a lot stricter with my parents than that."
所以，这一代父母似乎比30年前的父母更有可能把孩子当作朋友来对待。"我父母很愿意和我讨论事情，也愿意听我说，"17岁的Daniel Lazall说，"我每次出去夜店都会告诉他们。只要他们知道我在干嘛，他们就没意见。"现年21岁的Susan Crome也表示赞同。"回顾过去10年，有很多可以称之为谈判的东西。比如，只要我把作业都做完了，周六晚上就可以出去。但我觉得祖父母对我父母那一代严多了。"
Maybe this positive view of family life should not be unexpected. It is possible that the idea of teenage rebellion is not rooted in real facts. A researcher comments, "Our surprise that teenagers say they get along well with their parents comes because of a brief period in our social history when teenagers were regarded as different beings.

句子主干：Our surprise comes because of a brief period（我们的惊讶源于历史上一个短暂时期）。

两个从句：

① that teenagers say they get along well with their parents：同位语从句，解释 surprise 的具体内容（惊讶于什么？→ 惊讶于青少年说和父母相处很好）。

② when teenagers were regarded as different beings：定语从句，修饰 period（哪个时期？→ 青少年被视为异类的那段时期）。

整句意思：我们之所以对「青少年和父母相处融洽」感到惊讶，是因为历史上曾有短暂一段时期，青少年被视为与其他人完全不同的异类。

But that idea of rebelling and breaking away from their parents really only happened during that one time in the 1960s when everyone rebelled.

句子主干：that idea really only happened during that one time in the 1960s（那种「青少年叛逆」的概念其实只在1960年代发生过一次）。

of rebelling and breaking away from their parents：介词短语作定语，修饰 idea。整个 that idea of... = 「叛逆、脱离父母」这个概念。

when everyone rebelled：定语从句，修饰 that one time（那个所有人都在反叛的年代）。

really only：really 是强调，only 是限定。really only happened = 确实只是在那一次发生过，表达「就发生过这一次，不是常态」的语气。

The normal situation throughout history has been a smooth change from helping out with the family business to taking it over."
也许这种对家庭生活的积极看法不应该让人感到意外。青少年叛逆这个概念可能并没有现实依据。一位研究人员评论道："我们对青少年声称和父母相处融洽感到惊讶，是因为我们社会史上有一个短暂时期，青少年被视为另类。但那种反叛和脱离父母的想法真的只在1960年代那个全民反叛的时代发生过一次。纵观历史，正常的情况一直是从帮助家族生意到接管家族生意的平稳过渡。"
生词与短语（9个） ▼
get on with 与...相处融洽 高频
on 表示顺畅运转，get on with sb = 和某人相处好、关系融洽。就像机器「顺畅运转」一样
= get along with（也很常考）。反义：not get on with = 合不来。搭配：get on well with sb 和某人相处很好
take part in 参与，参加 高频
part 是「部分」，take part = 拿走一部分（参与进去）。take part in sth = 参加某事。= participate in（更正式）
高考超高频！写作文必备：take part in a competition/activity/discussion
As long as 只要…（条件状语） 高频
long 是「长」，as long as 表示条件：只要满足这个条件，后面就成立。= provided that（更书面）
高考写作超高频！As long as you work hard, you will succeed. 只要你努力，就会成功。
As long as they know what I'm doing = 只要他们知道我在干嘛（课文例句）
rebelling and breaking away from 反叛和脱离（父母） 高频
rebelling = rebel 的动名词，反叛。breaking away from = 脱离、摆脱。两个动名词并列：反抗父母、脱离父母的掌控
break away from 也很常用：break away from bad habits 摆脱坏习惯
smooth 光滑的；顺畅的，平稳的 高频
smooth road 平坦的路，smooth process 顺畅的过程。smooth change = 平稳的变化，没有剧烈反抗
副词 smoothly：everything went smoothly 一切进展顺利。
写作：a smooth transition from... to... 从...到...的平稳过渡
regarded as 被看作，被视为 考纲内
regard 是「看待、认为」。be regarded as = 被认为是、被视为。
teenagers were regarded as different beings = 青少年曾被视为另类（课文）
高考同义替换：= be considered as，= be seen as，= be viewed as（写作文这四个换着用）
generation 一代人，这一代 考纲内
generate（产生）→ generation（被产生出来的一群人）= 一代人。this generation of parents = 这一代父母（现在当父母的人）
搭配：the younger generation 年轻一代，generation gap 代沟，Generation Z Z世代
harmonious 和谐的（形容词） 拓展
harmony 的形容词形式，加 -ous 后缀。more harmonious than it has ever been = 比以往任何时候都更和谐。
① Their family life is more harmonious than ever. 他们的家庭生活比以往更和谐。（课文）
② 搭配：harmonious relationship 和谐关系，harmonious atmosphere 和谐氛围
rooted in 根植于，源于 拓展
root 是「根」，树根。rooted in = 有根基在某事物里 = 源于、基于。not rooted in real facts = 没有现实依据，不是基于真实事实
① The idea is not rooted in real facts. 这个想法没有现实依据。（课文）
② 搭配：deep-rooted 根深蒂固的，be rooted in tradition 根植于传统
题目解析（5题） ▼
1. What is the popular image of teenagers today?
A. They worry about school.
B. They dislike living with their parents.
C. They have to be locked in to avoid troubles.
D. They quarrel a lot with other family members.
答案：D
"...the popularly-held image of unhappy teenagers locked in their room after endless family quarrels."

题目问"大众对当今青少年的刻板印象是什么"。第一段直接说了：popularly-held image = 不开心的青少年，吵了无数架之后把自己锁在房间里。D（和家庭成员大量争吵）最接近。

✗ A— 文中提到青少年担心学校，但这不是大众对他们的"印象"，而是他们自己心里在想的事。
✗ B— 文中没有提到不喜欢和父母住在一起。
✗ C— 被关在房间是事实，但原因说反了。不是为了"避免麻烦"，而是争吵之后被关或自己躲进去。
✓ D— 与原文"after endless family quarrels"（无休止的家庭争吵）直接对应。
2. The study shows that teenagers don't want to ______.
A. share family responsibility
B. cause trouble in their families
C. go boating with their family
D. make family decisions
答案：B
"They don't want to rock the boat."

这道题考习语 rock the boat（制造麻烦、捣乱）。rock the boat = cause trouble。选 B。

✗ A— 文中说孩子们希望参与家庭决策，不是不愿意分担责任。
✓ B— rock the boat 的直接释义就是 cause trouble。
✗ C— go boating（划船）是对 rock the boat 的字面误读陷阱，别被绕进去。
✗ D— 恰好相反，文中说孩子们"expect to take part in the family decision-making process"，他们是想参与的。
3. Compared with parents of 30 years ago, today's parents ______.
A. go to clubs more often with their children
B. are much stricter with their children
C. care less about their children's life
D. give their children more freedom
答案：D
"...this generation of parents is much more likely than parents of 30 years ago to treat their children as friends."
"...my grandparents were a lot stricter with my parents than that."

两处证据：① 现在的父母更可能把孩子当朋友 ② Susan 说祖父母对父母比现在的父母对她严多了。两者都说明：现在的父母给孩子更多自由 = D。

✗ A— 文中 clubbing 是孩子出去玩，不是和父母一起去夜店。
✗ B— 恰好相反，是祖父母那一代更严格。
✗ C— 现在的父母更愿意倾听和讨论，不是不关心。
✓ D— 把孩子当朋友 + 允许孩子出去玩，都体现了给予更多自由。
4. According to the author, teenage rebellion ______.
A. may be a false belief
B. is common nowadays
C. existed only in the 1960s
D. resulted from changes in families
答案：A
"It is possible that the idea of teenage rebellion is not rooted in real facts."

作者用 it is possible that... + not rooted in real facts 表达：青少年叛逆这个概念可能根本没有现实依据。= may be a false belief（可能只是个错误的信念）= A。

✓ A— "not rooted in real facts" = 可能是假的信念，直接对应 "may be a false belief"。
✗ B— 恰好相反，作者认为现在青少年并不叛逆。
✗ C— 文章说叛逆在1960年代比较明显，但没说"only existed in the 1960s"，表述太绝对。
✗ D— 文章没有说叛逆是由家庭变化引起的。
5. What is the passage mainly about?
A. Negotiation in family.
B. Education in family.
C. Harmony in family.
D. Teenage trouble in family.
答案：C

文章标题"The Best of Friends"就是暗示，全文从各角度说明现在的青少年和父母关系很好、家庭和睦。研究发现、青少年的证词、研究员的分析，都在证明「家庭关系比以往更和谐」。选 C。

✗ A— negotiation（协商）只是文中提到的一个现象，不是主题。
✗ B— 文章几乎没有涉及教育话题。
✓ C— 全文主旨：家庭和谐，青少年与父母关系比以往更好。
✗ D— 恰好相反，文章论证的是青少年并没有给家庭带来麻烦。
